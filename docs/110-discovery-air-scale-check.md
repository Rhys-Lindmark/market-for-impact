# CBE: distinguish pollution burden from achievable reduction

Root planning check, September11,2026; not a CBE cost-effectiveness report or a final selection.

The [March2021 Air District staff report](https://www.baaqmd.gov/~/media/dotgov/files/rules/reg-6-rule-5-particulate-emissions-from-refinery-fluidized-catalytic-cracking-units/2020-amendment/documents/20210330_sr_0605-pdf.pdf?la=en), tables11–12, printed pages39–40, separates baseline mortality from modeled ScenarioB reductions. Chevron:5.1–11.6 baseline annual deaths,23% reduction. Martinez:2.8–6.3,50% reduction. Combined modeled reduction is therefore2.573–5.818 deaths/year, not the sum of the full baseline burdens. These historical model bounds are not a confidence interval for CBE's impact. Footnotes include infant mortality; no single age-specific QALY value is supplied. Tables were visually checked after web screenshots failed.

Illustratively assigning10 finite QALYs per death gives25.73–58.18 gross QALYs for one year's acceleration, before nonprofit contribution, gift allocation or harms. Ten is an analyst scenario, not a value estimated by that report. Dollar valuations must not be divided by an arbitrary dollars/QALY rate.

## Consequence for selection

This weakens a casually optimistic 'huge population, tiny advocacy budget' argument. The policy worker's verified $7.882M whole-organization expense requires78.82 attributable Bay QALYs per annual budget-equivalent to clear $1M/10. One year of this historical mortality-only improvement, even assigned entirely to the organization under the illustrative ten-QALY assumption, would not reach that hurdle. Longer genuinely accelerated implementation, other independent pathways or a specific marginal bottleneck could change the comparison; neither is established by the example.

A $100k targeted intervention can look much better if it has a material chance of accelerating compliance. But that is not automatically the return to an unrestricted CBE gift. Any selected whole-gift model must connect ordinary gift allocation to the residual work, keep the rest of CBE's portfolio in scope, and expose null/harm cases. Do not switch between marginal project cost and whole annual organization cost merely to improve the answer.

The existing2024settlement and2025/2026implementation process identified in the policy screen remain the counterfactual. Fresh diligence should examine current residual timing and other Bay exposure work, not credit today's gift with the2021rule. CBE remains a worthwhile high-variance comparison candidate, not a demonstrated sub-$100k opportunity.
