## Best finite clinical benchmark found

The [2024 randomized trial conducted with LCC](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/27536130241274240) randomized31 Spanish-speaking Latinx breast-cancer patients to six weekly1.5-hour remote mindfulness sessions or wait-list/usual care. At three-month follow-up, anxiety improved by3.6 GAD-7 points (CI−6.9 to−.2); clinical importance was uncertain, depression and sleep were not significantly improved. This supports a short, specific distress pathway—not navigation efficacy, cancer survival, or a utility gain for all contacts. No preference-based QALY result or present annual completed-course count is supplied. Avoid transferring the point-score change directly into QALYs.
