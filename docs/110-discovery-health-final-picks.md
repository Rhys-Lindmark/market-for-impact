## Primary evidence and contrary evidence

Acknowledge [FY2025 original990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202611329349304406/IRS990) reports20schools/412educators/>10,000students reached and $167,455 dedicated Beechwood therapy expense; reach is not treatment. $1,116,502 program revenue plus $55,000 government grants means school-paid baseline is substantial. The50course figure is a bounded planning judgment, not450sessions=450patients. Existing school counselors and financed contracts could make ordinary-gift response null.

WCRC [original990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202610279349301416/IRS990) and [ScheduleI](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202610279349301416/IRS990ScheduleI) establish272cash recipients and53potentially overlapping gift-card recipients. Small one-time aid, eligibility/referral and receipt follow-up do not establish treatment completion. No need to infer zero health, but grant-to-survival conversion should have a large null region.

LCC [original990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202521689349300002/IRS990) establishes full expense, not completed courses. Its [local randomized trial](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/27536130241274240) randomized31participants to six-week remote mindfulness or wait-list/usual care. Anxiety difference at3months was−3.6GAD7 (CI−6.9 to−.2), clinical significance uncertain; depression/sleep did not significantly improve. This is useful finite local evidence, but neither a QALY estimate nor evidence all navigation is effective. No lifetime cancer-survival extrapolation.
