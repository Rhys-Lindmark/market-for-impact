## Signed finite clinical bridges and strongest counterweights

- **Rescue (Micah/NEED):**unique at-risk cohort × integral of intervention-minus-current-care survival over finite horizon × utility, after a limited active access period; subtract independent harms and retain null access. Repeated rescues change opportunity hazard, never restart lifetime credit. Existing public/peer naloxone, timely EMS, inventory availability and actual witness use determine the difference. [Walley interrupted-time-series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23372174/) supports the mechanism observationally, not local deaths per dose; [selected rescue cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24684801/) cannot supply unconditional lethal rate. Reuse accepted HEPPAC/F2F sensitivities and common post-overdose survival evidence; do not silently improve prognosis for a preferred candidate. Syringe infection/wound pathways need non-overlapping clinical episodes.
- **PrEP/ART (Oakland/Rainbow):**unique eligible starts × extra retained exposure beyond alternative care × untreated/comparator infection or disease risk difference × finite treated-era health difference, plus separately deduped short STI morbidity. Adherence is not multiplied twice if effect already ITT. Null linkage/coverage baseline must be explicit; no historical untreated-AIDS lifetime credit for a modern avoidable infection. Clinical treatments may be efficacious while this donor's incremental uptake is zero.
- **Home repair/lead (RTP):**unique high-risk residents × causal change in clinically consequential fall/injury or exposure × finite morbidity/survival gain, including no effect and unsafe-work harm. [Stark trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34463746/) had null primary time-to-first-fall despite secondary fall-rate signal; [OTIS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34254934/)1331participants point estimate favored morefalls(IRR1.17,CI.99–1.38). Do not choose only supportive fall outcomes. [Residential lead-hazard RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30178064/) found no significant overall bloodlead difference(P=.20); exposure remediation is not automatic cognitive-QALY gain. IQ/earnings and health utility are distinct. Need population-specific elevatedexposure before invoking long-term child benefit.
- **Cancer navigation (LCC):**unique diagnosed patients × additional appropriate treatment or shorter delay beyond hospital/insurance navigation × stage/type-specific finite health difference; add short distress only when distinct. [Randomized navigation/QOL trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23045543/) found no significant disease-specificQOL effect, and [another randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32864723/) no significantHRQoL difference. These do not prove targeted access cannot help; they block an automatic positive utility award per contact. Screening can also cause false-positive workup/overdiagnosis, so signed mixture is necessary.
