For any whole-gift budget C, the $1M/10 threshold requires net additional Bay QALYs >= C/100,000; the $100k/10 threshold requires >= C/10,000. ## Lessons to apply at the 25→10 gate

1. Obtain full gross cash expense and legal gift recipient before modeling. Donated labor/device value is a separate resource diagnostic, not cash savings or free societal inputs.
2. For rescue, use one unique cohort with finite survival, explicit recurrence and common post-support hazards; distributions/reversal reports are selected observations, not prospective lethal event rates. Reuse accepted HEPPAC/F2F helper and shared-prior sensitivity, never event-linear future lifetimes.
3. For HIV/HCV/cancer, count additional retained treatment relative to public/insured care, not every test or referral. Do not multiply separate discounts that describe the same access failure.
4. Use controlled effect/active-comparator evidence where applicable, but local transfer, course duration and utility may remain broad signed priors. Expose symmetric assumptions versus current reports.
5. Whole-gift partial-health results remain partial. They cannot automatically rank as complete expected value or a guaranteed lower bound. Unknown funding offer is not an automatic exploratory rejection, but donation recommendation requires a real marginal purpose.
