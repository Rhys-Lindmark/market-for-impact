## Why these 25 receive the next comparison

The ten health leads compare lean rescue access, completed clinical care, repair/lead prevention, neonatal prevention and low-cost supervised therapy. The nine systems leads test population-scale emissions, safety, health-access enforcement and implementation support, not policy passage as health. The six access leads compare finite restoration of essential function and treatment barriers under whole-organization budgets. This queue deliberately contains alternatives: not all deserve one of the finalten.

These are qualitative research-priority judgments. Public funding is not a numerical impact discount; unknown clinical denominators are not assumed zero; donated inputs are not unlimited. Final selection must compare expected promise under realistic, explicitly uncertain assumptions rather than prefer only easy-to-measure work. Primary finance/clinical checks are in the lane memos. No outcome probabilities or favorable estimates are asserted by inclusion.
