# Whole gift is not necessarily the average budget

Manager selection principle, September11,2026. Arithmetic/methodology, not a new causal estimate.

Retaining all ordinary-gift uses does not prove that the next private dollar buys the average mix of historical activity. The annual expense/net health ratio is a useful diagnostic, but it is not automatically a marginal donation price. High fixed costs, reimbursement, restricted contracts and existing volunteer capacity can make the derivative better or worse than that average.

For a gift D, model additional health as the sum over pathways of additional completed activity caused by D, multiplied by each pathway's finite net health relative to alternatives. Add shared fixed capacity effects only once. The gift's entire amount remains the denominator, including whatever is retained, displaces other support, or finances unquantified activity. A pathway not yet quantified is not evidence of zero welfare. Necessary provider/public/donated resources remain a separate complete-resource check.

Historical whole cost is a transparent starting proxy when marginal allocation is unknown. If using that proxy, say so. Do not multiply by the public-revenue share as an automatic additionality discount; government revenue does not establish how a new private gift behaves. Likewise, do not use a small program budget as the price of an unrestricted gift without an allocation model. These are opposite mistakes.

## Practical selection tests

- Lean volunteer delivery: would cash enable more staffed hours or refills, or are donated labor/inventory already the binding constraint? Include preservation of useful capacity if genuinely at risk, not only expansion.
- Clinical-access assistance: is a private gift filling an actual uncovered gap, or has public eligibility changed? Current BACC evidence shows this question can overturn an appealing older diagnostic pathway.
- Health consortium: are funded enrollment transactions already happening, and does extra help change coverage duration beyond existing reminders? Do not count enrollment as an additional lifetime of effective care.
- Systems implementation: can flexible money change a live design, deadline or compliance decision? Historical passage and completed infrastructure cannot establish today's marginal change.

For the ten selections, compare both the conservative annual-budget diagnostic and an explicit ordinary-gift allocation scenario when there is a plausible reason for different marginal productivity. A speculative targeted offer must remain separate from the unrestricted-gift estimate. Use no favorable scenario as a verified recommendation or calibrated expected return.
