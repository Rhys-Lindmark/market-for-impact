# Phase A: seven policy/system candidates for the combined 25

## Recommendation

**Keep CBE, NICJR and Alameda Health Consortium in the serious comparison set.** They have credible region-wide or multi-provider implementation mechanisms, not merely broad missions. Their additional-donation effects remain unknown. **Keep Marin County Bicycle Coalition and ANRF as second-tier alternatives.** Legal Aid San Mateo and Law Foundation Silicon Valley remain legitimate exploratory candidates, but their easier-to-describe individual access work does not currently establish better whole-gift value.

This ordering is a judgment about the value of further research, not an estimated ranking or authorization to write seven reports. No candidate has a verified current marginal offer. An unknown offer alone does not exclude a prior-driven model; the decisive issue is whether the population/outcome/finite-health/contribution chain can be made credible.

## Whole-finance anchors and break-even requirements

For an annual whole-cost diagnostic, clearing $1M per 10 Bay QALYs requires annual attributable Bay health greater than expense / $100,000. Clearing $100k requires ten times that. This is **not** a claim a marginal gift scales average expenditure or outcomes linearly. All nonquantified programs remain in cost; publicly financed services and partner resources belong in an additional resource ledger.

## 1. CBE: strongest large-population exposure lead, but residual change is the entire question

**Mechanism:** scientific/legal work changes actual refinery particulate controls or their implementation date. A [Nov 12, 2025 Air District technical presentation, pp31–35](https://www.baaqmd.gov/~/media/files/board-of-directors/2025/ssc_agenda_111225_op-pdf.pdf?rev=87306cf91e544ba5badd2fbc48319d46&sc_lang=en) explicitly uses air-quality modeling → population exposure → health endpoints for Rule 6-5. This is a serious causal-health mechanism. It includes mortality, cardiovascular and respiratory outcomes; dollar valuations are not QALYs. The relevant denominator is exposed Bay residents in modeled plumes, not all Californians or all residents around every CBE campaign.

**Already funded/enforceable baseline:** [2024 Air District settlement summary](https://www.baaqmd.gov/en/publications/air-currents/2024/050124) says Chevron and Martinez ended litigation and accepted emissions reductions and delay penalties. The [executed Chevron agreement](https://www.baaqmd.gov/~/media/files/communications-and-outreach/news-and-events/penalties-and-assessments/2024/rule-6-5-chevron-baaqmd-settlement-agreement-final-fully-executed-with-attachments-pdf.pdf?rev=190d875ee0fa42a093cb98f0cdaa6fb4&sc_lang=es-mx) anticipates noncompliance past July 21, 2026 while permitting/construction proceeds, with interim reductions and an enforcement framework. These provisions are not proof that full compliance has occurred by this retrieval date.

A [Nov 12, 2025 committee packet](https://www.baaqmd.gov/~/media/files/board-of-directors/2025/cehj_agenda_111225_op-pdf.pdf?rev=d6e440e1393f4c4ca43f63233f286e92&sc_lang=en) identified $20M in the Richmond Community Air Quality Fund and anticipated 2026 awards/2027 work. The schedule is a dated plan, not proof of an executed CBE award or spent money. It creates both an implementation opportunity and substitution risk.

**Residual hypothesis:** an additional CBE legal/engineering effort shortens a genuinely avoidable delay, improves an interim measure, or turns community-funded mitigation into a materially more effective project. Do not credit a new gift with 2021 rule passage or the entire settlement benefit. Need a specific unresolved compliance decision, its realistic CBE contribution and timing—not only an old advocacy fact sheet.

**Judgmental break-even:** $100k requires 1 net Bay QALY at the $1M threshold. If one year of the specified residual controls produces 100 finite Bay QALYs and a gift raises the chance of accelerating it by one year by 1%, the product is 1 QALY before harms. **Both 100 and 1% are hypothetical**, not estimates derived from the presentation. A full annual whole-cost assessment needs 78.82 attributable Bay QALYs, so a 100-QALY gross change with only 10% organization-level contribution does not clear it. This distinction prevents mixing a speculative marginal opportunity with average organization costs.

**Finite/harm boundary:** include the actual lag until regulation/industry would act anyway; no perpetual emissions benefit. Avoid overlap between fatal events and later survival, or asthma admissions and symptom utility. Include capital/operating compliance resources, consumer burdens, industrial-safety risks and geographical pollution displacement. **Keep in 25; first systems diligence priority**, contingent on finding the residual engineering/legal decision.

## 2. NICJR: real Oakland implementation and a documented cancellation, not all city progress credited to TA

The original return distinguishes CAPS ($4.649M, including $1.274M grants), CVIPI ($1.713M, including $1.625M grants) and other work. It says NICJR leads CAPS in Oakland, and that the federal CVIPI award was cancelled in spring 2025 while training/TA was early. This is a real funding-shock lead, **not proof the cancelled subgrantees were Bay organizations or that their work remains unfunded today**.

[NICJR's CAPS page](https://nicjr.org/CAPS) describes city coordination meetings reviewing shootings and needs of high-risk individuals, individualized TA and grants, and collaboration with Oakland DVP. It reports citywide declines, but those are uncontrolled trends. Its AIR evaluation described on the page is a process evaluation, not identification of NICJR's marginal impact. [Oakland's separate $500k violence/housing pilot announcement](https://www.oaklandca.gov/News-Releases/Mayor-Lee-Secures-500K-for-Groundbreaking-Community-Violence-Intervention-Strategy-Centering-Housing-Stability) names NICJR and Center for Justice Innovation; the search copy was available but direct fetch returned403. Treat $500k as announced funding for the collaborative pilot, not a priced NICJR donation slot.

**Transfer:** [original Oakland Ceasefire evaluation, May 2019](https://crimejusticelab.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Ceasefire-Evaluation-Final-Report_Oakland_2019.pdf) and [NIJ's methodological summary](https://crimesolutions.ojp.gov/ratedprograms/ceasefire-oakland-calif) offer quasi-experimental direct/spillover evidence, not a randomized current CAPS effect. Population is the high-risk network plus affected residents; whole-city homicide is an outcome, not a recipient count. Police, outreach providers, public services and secular trends share causality.

**Judgmental hurdle:** whole annual costs need122.85 Bay QALYs. At an assumed10 finite QALYs per additional prevented death, that is12.28 deaths attributable to NICJR's Bay contribution if mortality alone is counted. With a10% contribution to an implementation change, gross Bay effect would need122.85 deaths—not a reasonable default to assume merely from an announced pilot. Conversely, for a genuinely marginal $100k gift, an additional0.1 prevented fatality at10QALYs clears $1M/10 before harms; whether TA/grantmaking can produce that is the open issue. Nonfatal injuries could contribute, but not all assaults deserve death-level utility.

Signed cases must include zero effect, geographic/nonfirearm substitution, justice-system harms and opportunity costs. Resources paid through grants count once; downstream resources above those transfers count separately. **Keep in25**, and obtain Schedule I/Bay grant geography plus an actual unfinished service/TA tranche before report selection. Current assets and substantial funded CAPS activity make undifferentiated emergency fundraising claims unconvincing.

## 3. Alameda Health Consortium: strongest newly anchored denominator, with a funded baseline and eligibility limits

FY25 return reports **7,323 Medi-Cal enrollments and9,339 renewals** through a joint consortium/county/member-clinic program—not16,662 proven unique additional insured people. It also reports83 trained community health workers,51 HIV care-team trainees and support for23 pediatric/integrated behavioral-health coordinators. These are distinct potential pathways; do not count training as treatment or coordinator minutes as QALYs.

[HHS RECESS award record](https://taggs.hhs.gov/Detail/AwardDetail?arg_AwardNum=2Y2CMS331871&arg_ProgOfficeCode=190) identifies $1.5M total awards and July19,2022–June30,2025 performance period. Targets include enrollments and renewals, not demonstrated effects. Its2026 administrative entries are $0: neither new funding nor proof there is no replacement funding. [Alameda County's June2026 budget communication](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/CAALAME/bulletins/41e48ac?reqfrom=share) describes $85M for FQHCs/community clinics and continued pressures. This money is a clinic-system baseline, not necessarily an AHC grant.

**Residual:** prevent administrative disenrollment among people still eligible; remove a clinical-workforce/retention bottleneck that member clinics would not solve otherwise. Do not model enrollment staff overturning statutory ineligibility. The current staff page shows eligibility/enrollment staff remain; RECESS's past end date alone is not a charitable funding cliff.

**Finite bridge:** [Oregon randomized insurance experiment,2013](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMsa1212321) examined approximately two-year outcomes. It does not license automatic mortality gains per insurance enrollment or a lifetime universal utility gain. Separate coverage persistence, service use and condition-specific improvement; insurance can change financial security without equivalent clinical QALYs.

**Judgmental screen:** annual whole-cost break-even is75.95Q /16,662 transactions = **.004558 net Bay QALY per recorded enrollment/renewal transaction**. If25% represent genuinely additional one-year coverage and each such year generates .02Q beyond alternatives, the product is .005Q/transaction,83.31Q overall: the hypothesis barely clears $1M/10 before duplicate people, burden and public-resource additions. If5% are incremental with the same health yield, it gives16.66Q and fails. These percentages/utility are expressly unvalidated scenarios. This is a useful discriminating test, not a favorable model value.

**Keep in25**, with a higher evidence-readiness rank than in the brainstorm. Need deduplicated additional covered/treated patient-years, clinical mix, current contracts and CHCN/clinic cost separation. The large member reach is not itself attributable output.

## 4. Marin County Bicycle Coalition: modest whole cost and city infrastructure upside, not class-count health

The original return anchors a $1.425M whole organization, including education, bike provision, road and trail work. The [public Safe Routes description](https://www.saferoutestoschools.org/about/) assigns administration to TAM and identifies existing crossing-guard/capital programs. [Marin County's December2025 safety-project update](https://www.marincounty.gov/news-releases/board-slated-hear-summary-safety-related-public-works-projects) describes an already awarded $200k quick-build allocation becoming available in early2026. The [spring2026 program newsletter](https://www.saferoutestoschools.org/sr2s-newsletter-spring-2026/) describes current philanthropic support, not an unfunded offer.

Population should be exposed trips/users on specific routes, with preexisting injury risk. [DiMaggio/Li,2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23319533/) associates NYC SRTS implementation with lower child pedestrian injury during school travel; this is a combined local implementation, not evidence MCBC education alone reduces injury44%. [Later timing analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5005758/) explicitly discusses preintervention-trend interpretation. Finite changes in active travel and serious injury can both matter, with double counting avoided.

At10 assumed finite QALYs per prevented death, the annual whole-cost hurdle is1.425 attributable deaths equivalent, or14.25Q from combined injury/health effects. A10% organization contribution requires a142.5Q gross implementation effect. This could be plausible across multiple dangerous routes; it is not justified by thousands of students reached. The residual lever is improved design or earlier completion beyond awarded capital, not the whole project budget.

The2025 report also describes an election-cycle consultant strategy for a tunnel project. **Exclude that persuasion route from this research scope**; it does not invalidate technical route/design work. Retain maintenance costs, crashes from increased cycling exposure and displaced traffic. **Keep as second-tier25 candidate**, pending route-specific population/project counterfactual rather than generic SRTS transfer.

## 5. ANRF: a real funding interruption, with strong implementation disconfirmation

[July9,2025 ANRF statement](https://no-smoke.org/smokefree-lists-and-maps-july-1-2025/) says a CDC contract received a stop-work order and state contracts were threatened. [August2026 update](https://no-smoke.org/the-fight-for-smokefree-air-continues/) discusses funding pressure and local work, but mixes ANR/Foundation language; it does not establish a Bay-restricted charitable tranche. The original Foundation finance is distinct from its affiliate.

Residual population is Bay residents still exposed in specific buildings/workplaces despite existing protections. A housing rule is not an exposure change: [NYC primary exposure evaluation,2020](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2772536) and [implementation analysis,2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8656672/) found little short-run air-quality improvement under the public-housing policy. A [three-year follow-up](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9717387/) is a necessary duration lead; not fully reviewed here. Do not freeze a one-year null as proof of permanent ineffectiveness.

Annual wholecost needs39.28 net Bay QALYs. At an assumed.01Q per genuinely reduced-exposure resident-year, that is3,928 Bay resident-years; at.001 it is39,275. A5% Bay share would require20times as many national equivalent resident-years. These are sensitivity thresholds, not sourced health yields/Bay shares. Need actual incremental compliant exposure and finite disease/symptom gains, less displacement, housing loss and enforcement burdens. **Keep second tier**, because the funding shock is concrete but Bay implementation and effectiveness remain weaker than the clinical-access or specific-air-control leads.

## 6–7. Legal Aid San Mateo and Law Foundation Silicon Valley: preserve, but do not privilege tractability

LASMC has current [health-access work](https://www.legalaidsmc.org/obtaining-health-care) and an [official2025 impact page](https://www.legalaidsmc.org/) reporting over1,900 cases across the whole portfolio. The returned original filing puts only$517,791 in Health Consumer Center, versus$5.822M grosswholecost; LIBRE and housing grants remain in expense. Annual health hurdle is58.22Q, or .03064Q per1,900case reference denominator. If only10% of cases restore clinically material care, it would require.3064Q per such case, before other pathways/harm. Neither the10% nor per-case health is observed. An undated successful insurance appeal illustrates mechanism, not a success rate or completed treatment. **Reserve in25; seek actual care-access case mix and completed care.**

Law Foundation's officialFY25 report describes health access, public-benefit appeals, mental-health facility monitoring and due-process representation alongside housing/youth law. It reports15,205people served, not15,205medical improvements. Its158.22Q hurdle averages.01041Q/person across that reported population; assuming only10% experience additional health change raises required benefit to.1041Q/person in that subset. This can be plausible for a severe case mix but is unsupported as a central input. Counting avoided commitment as intrinsically health-improving, or all benefit appeals as effective treatment, would be erroneous. **Reserve rather than immediate full-report selection**; resources already include pro bono support, government contracts and broad legal work.

For both, finite duration should be the delay in effective care actually avoided; rights, income and clinical health are different outcomes. Signed scenarios include unsuccessful cases, burden/delays, loss of beneficial services, adverse clinical effects and displacement of other counsel. The legal-service whole-cost hurdle is not satisfied merely because an individual story is compelling.

## Selection gates and information value

1. **CBE:** current compliance/mitigation decision, population-weighted residual health and credible timing contribution. Highest upside among these seven, most dependent on a specific remaining implementation lever.
2. **NICJR:** Bay grant/service ledger, cancelled-award recipients and whether funded CAPS/pilot leaves a genuinely additional lever. Large citywide health possible; TA attribution is decisive.
3. **AHC:** additional coverage/treatment persistence from 16,662 reported transactions; replace illustrative utility with actual clinical mix. Strongest new output denominator and useful whole-cost break-even test.
4. **MCBC/ANRF:** actual route/exposure implementation beyond already financed work. Do not eliminate because outcomes are harder; do not substitute policy/class counts.
5. **LASMC/Law Foundation:** whole-resource clinical case mix/outcomes, not merely generic legal aid plausibility.

These judgments are not probability estimates. No new report values, priced offers or claims of current unrestricted funding capacity are established. Unknown room remains modeled uncertainty if a later report is selected; a missing entire causal implementation pathway remains a more serious problem.
