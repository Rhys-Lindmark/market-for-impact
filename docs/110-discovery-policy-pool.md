## Common arithmetic and rules

A whole unrestricted $100,000 gift needs **1 net Bay QALY** to clear $1 million per 10 QALYs, or **10** to clear $100,000. Every row’s favorable path must survive whole-portfolio allocation, alternative funding, non-Bay benefits, finite duration, counterfactual service access and signed harm. Unknown marginal offers are uncertainty, not automatic rejection.

The following are **illustrative screening hurdles, not sourced local effect estimates**:

- **Access (A):** 20–200 genuinely additional Bay treatment-years at an assumed .05 net QALY per year would yield 1–10 QALYs. Do not substitute approvals, legal cases or benefit dollars for completed care.
- **Safety (S):** .1–1 genuinely additional deaths prevented at an assumed 10 finite QALYs per death would yield 1–10 QALYs. Serious nonfatal injury can contribute separately. No automatic lifetime survival or attribution of an entire public road project.
- **Implementation (I):** a 1% donor-caused change in implementation probability requires a conditional 100–1,000 net Bay QALYs to yield 1–10 expected QALYs. The 1% is not a recommended prior. An earlier implementation date may matter more than ultimate passage.
- **Exposure (E):** 100–1,000 additional Bay resident-years of materially reduced harmful exposure at an assumed .01 QALY per resident-year yields 1–10 QALYs. Actual dose-response, baseline risk and duration must replace this placeholder.

These hurdles keep citywide upside visible without inventing attribution. All downstream public/provider/capital resources must be reported separately; a government grant is not free health, and the same transferred dollars must not be counted twice. Housing costs or eviction avoidance require a separately supported finite health bridge, not a reused favorable scalar.
