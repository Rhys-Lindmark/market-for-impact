## Mandatory report safeguards / wording clarifications

1. **Denominator is prospective at-risk persons, not necessarily the kit recipients.** A recipient may be a friend/worker carrying naloxone for others; a person at risk may receive multiple kits through multiple distributors. Explicitly map annual dispensed units → use-ready access → unique exposed at-risk people → incremental rescue availability. The 174-person hurdle does not mean174 observed recipients suffice. Reported successful reversals also do not identify prospective Poisson opportunity rates.
2. **.070 is a prior, not a measured distribution-cohort hazard.** Historical post-overdose survivors are selected differently from all people reached by a dispenser. Larochelle's17,568 Massachusetts overdose survivors had4.7 all-cause deaths/100 person-years; Olfson's first-year cohort reported778.3/10,000 person-years (=7.783/100). Neither identifies community kit-recipient mortality, .002 hazard reduction, ten-year constant mortality, or modern Bay risk. Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29913516/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29926090/ . Fresh primary abstracts checked in this audit.
3. **Rate is not annual probability.** At hazard .070, one-year mortality probability is1−exp(−.07)≈6.76%, not7%. For probability p, constant hazard is−ln(1−p). Historical person-year rates can motivate scale but are not exact constant hazards. No numerical change is needed because the memo correctly says hazard.
4. **Separate the two additionality questions exactly once.** Community .002 hazard difference already represents incremental rescue access relative to ordinary care/public/peer alternatives for a covered person. The .5 response represents whether an ordinary gift generates/sustains additional such coverage relative to funded operations. Do not also multiply by HEPPAC attribution/outcomeAdditionality, or treat the gift as generating an additional cohort and then apply the same cash-response discount again. A separate attribution factor is permissible only for a genuinely different boundary, explicitly defined.
5. **Public inventory is both a complement and an alternative, not an automatic government-funding discount.** Free DHCS product can enable additional local access, yet also makes replacement supply likely. Maintain whole gross organization expenses and separate donated inventory/labor resources. Current public stock, volunteer capacity and donor cash response remain unknown; supply donations not needed at NEED does not imply no cash use. Do not monetize public inventory twice if already included in accounting expenses.
6. **Finite signed scenarios are still required.** The displayed .5 is nonnegative deployment; a 'harmful response' should mean negative net health or an explicitly negative clinical/access effect, not a negative share of budget deployed. Add null access and independent adverse morbidity/behavioral or displacement scenarios to eventual reports; this calibration is intentionally not a probability-weighted model. Repeated events must never reset the survivor horizon.
7. **HOPE's $40k remains an assumed whole-year expense.** County $10k installation agreement is neither annual expense nor new unfunded room. Micah's entire$66,076 and NEED's$357,858 stay in scope, including non-naloxone activities; other pathways may be modeled with nonoverlapping health, not silently removed from cost.
