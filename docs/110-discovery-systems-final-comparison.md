## Recommendation

**AHC is the strongest of these three for a whole-organization exploratory report, but not on the prior optimistic enrollment-only argument.** Separate intensive application assistance from marginal renewal reminders, and include its actual behavioral-care/workforce pathways without double-counting member clinics. **MCBC is the next choice if a genuinely systems-focused comparison is desired:** San Marin Drive has a current, concrete design opportunity, unlike already-funded quick builds. However, a single corridor does not yet justify a favorable whole-organization price. **Do not automatically substitute ANRF**: the funding shock is real, but Bay implementation and household exposure effects remain weakly linked to an ordinary gift.

None of these bounded pathways now has a central or favorable case below $1M/10 Bay QALYs under the explicit illustrative priors below. That is not a finding that their full portfolios fail the threshold; it is a reason not to select a top-10 candidate from unsupported citywide scale. If the final ten must prioritize strongest expected health rather than comparison value, retain AHC as a reserve against the clinical shortlist, not a guaranteed slot.

## MCBC: actual next project, not the funded baseline

[MCBC's July 16, 2026 San Marin Drive article](https://marinbike.org/news/novato-needs-a-safer-san-marin-drive/) proposes using pipeline-related reconstruction to replace outside traffic lanes with buffered bike lanes. It identifies a route through San Marin Drive and Novato Boulevard. This is a **design-change opportunity**, not a priced donor offer, construction commitment, or observed injury reduction. The linked [Marin Water ARC project](https://marinwater.org/yourwater/atmospheric-river-capture-arc-project/) independently confirms a proposed 13-mile water pipeline, environmental review, and draft EIR expected summer/fall 2026. The pipeline's entire length is not a measured future bike corridor. Construction and safety redesign are not established as fully financed or approved in the sources read.

This differs materially from the earlier $200k three quick-build projects and TAM-funded Safe Routes baseline in `/private/tmp/mfi-110-policy-screen25.md`: do not give a new gift all already-awarded infrastructure. The ordinary-gift lever would be incremental technical design/coordination that changes the reinstated road, multiplied by project realization and MCBC contribution. No electoral strategy, voter persuasion, or outreach was undertaken or modeled.

Required missing measurements: corridor-specific fatal/serious injury history, projected users, proposed design effectiveness, agency default design, decision date and incremental construction/maintenance costs. Without those, town population is not an exposed population. Water-supply benefits belong to the water project, not MCBC. Potential harms include traffic displacement and increased cyclist exposure; active-travel health must be based on sustained additional cycling, not event attendance.

## AHC: stronger causal evidence, smaller incremental effect than a generic 15%

AHC's original June-2025 return reports 7,323 Medi-Cal enrollments and 9,339 renewals. They are transactions, not deduplicated additional coverage-years. The [AHC enrollment description](https://www.alamedahealthconsortium.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/EE-WW-H-FLyer-1.pdf) places county eligibility workers in member clinics. The [county FY2024–25 budget](https://budget.alamedacountyca.gov/Content/pdf/FY24-25/FY2024-25ProposedBudget-Rev_6_11_24.pdf) describes HealthPAC partnering with AHC on enrollment/retention. Both establish real infrastructure and a public baseline, not donor-only production. Its [data page](https://www.alamedahealthconsortium.org/expertise/data-better-care/) attributes relevant analytics to **CHCN**; do not quietly treat CHCN as the same recipient.

Two original randomized evaluations materially discipline the renewal prior:

- [Myerson, Espeseth and Dague, NBER working paper 34191, 2025](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34191): adding a call about available assistance to existing written outreach increased renewal by **1 percentage point**, reducing procedural denials by 1.1 points. This is outreach assignment, not intensive-assistance treatment-on-treated and not AHC's completed-renewal denominator. The working paper PDF returned 403; the authors' NBER abstract was available via search. Do not claim full-paper review.
- [Federal Office of Evaluation Sciences, 2025](https://oes.gsa.gov/projects/2406-increasing-medicaid-renewals-with-texts/): randomized 7,468 Norfolk cases June–November 2024; all already received three reminders. Added credibility texts/calls produced no statistically detectable renewal increase. This supports a null scenario beyond competent baseline, not proof that all navigation is ineffective.

For health transfer, the [2013 Oregon randomized study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3701298/) found mixed effects over two years: depression and financial protection improved; measured physical outcomes did not significantly improve. [2018 depression analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5835676/) is helpful for mechanism but untreated-depression reduction is not a QALY. A short procedural coverage interruption may yield much less than a full year uninsured-versus-Medicaid comparison. Thus the former blended 15% additional-coverage fraction and .02 utility should not be treated as clinical estimates.

Use separate new-enrollment and renewal fractions, explicit alternative-care timing, and one partner-adjusted additionality term. Keep all $7.595M whole expense in scope. Workforce training and integrated behavioral care may be material, but trained staff/minutes are not additional health without a separate finite chain. Statutory ineligibility cannot be corrected through faster paperwork.

## ANRF alternative: neither blanket null nor proven household health

The actual July 2025 CDC stop-work evidence and national whole expense $3,927,527 remain in the prior screen. No current Bay marginal tranche was verified. The original [three-year New York public-housing study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36041039/) (published online August 30, 2022; January 2023 issue) updates the earlier one-year null: hallway nicotine declined relative to comparison buildings, while household nicotine and PM2.5 did not show statistically detectable differential changes. Therefore it is wrong both to claim policies never work and to equate a housing policy with broad household dose elimination. Nonrandomized building comparison, changing adherence, exposure location and time matter.

A useful Bay mechanism would be a named property implementer using ANRF assistance to improve compliance without eviction or shifting exposure indoors. A list/database entry is not a health intervention. ANRF versus affiliated ANR legal and advocacy boundaries need to remain separate. Lease-enforcement displacement is a signed downside, not an incidental footnote.

## Reproducible finite screening scenarios

These are **judgmental partial-pathway central/favorable hypotheses**, not empirical estimates or fitted expected values. Whole annual resource expense is the numerator; an ordinary $100k gift scales both cost and modeled benefit proportionately. That scaling is uncertain. No verified gift price is implied. Nonprofit cost is not total societal cost: induced public care, road construction, and landlord implementation must be retained as complements and cannot be called free. Do not rank near-zero net values literally.

Recreation/other routes remain omitted benefits.

Favorable: `30,000 × .2 × .005 × 3 × .3 × .8 − 1 = 20.6`. **No observed Bay reach or validated QALY-per-dose coefficient supports these population/utility test values.** They illustrate why an apparently large policy catchment alone does not settle the case. Do not convert hallway nicotine directly into these utilities.

All have an explicit null world of zero additional benefit and no harm. Downside stress values per annual-cost unit: AHC −2Q (coverage errors/privacy burden), MCBC −1Q (crash/traffic displacement), ANRF −2Q (housing displacement or worsened exposure). These are judgments, not observed rates. No favorable finite price for net zero/negative health is reported. The unfavorable central result is preserved rather than tuned.

## What would justify selection now

AHC: a broad-portfolio report can honestly test additional retained care plus behavioral-care coordination; proceed only with explicit prior-driven labels and public/member-clinic overlap. MCBC: retain if a systems comparison slot is valuable, with San Marin route data first. ANRF: reserve until an actual Bay implementation recipient/cohort is identified; database maintenance alone is not a sufficient health bridge. Unknown funding room is not the disqualifier—the principal issue is demonstrable scale and causal transmission beyond the baseline.
