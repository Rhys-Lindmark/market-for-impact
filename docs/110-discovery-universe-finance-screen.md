Best comparison ILRCSF; EDI has concentrated mission but government contract baseline is overwhelming.

**HERS — hold for clinical mix.** Original PartIII describes fitted bras, prostheses, lymphedema garments and wigs at Fremont/Livermore/SanLeandro; it does not report unique annual patients or clinically effective compression episodes.
