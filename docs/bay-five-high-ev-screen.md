# Five new Bay-serving whole-gift screens

September8,2026. Outcome-first bounded research, not five new reports. **No candidate presently supports a credible sub-$100,000-per10-QALY ordinary-gift estimate. Do not create a full positive model from this screen.** Via Heart Project is the best next *cheap disconfirmation* because a posted price, actual local public contract and a randomized causal mechanism all exist; the important mismatch is maintenance versus a newly equipped response system. Ceres is second for further evidence work, not a donation recommendation. HEPPAC remains biologically plausible but lacks a measured residual-access/unit-cost bridge. All five have distinct missing gates below.

## Deduplication and scope

Read current HRT worktree BACKLOG.md and enumerated app/charities, including the accepted/pending HKF, HRT, NEXT, CalWalks and Fistula additions. Searched current data/docs/research-queue plus existing temporary screens. The five below have no existing report or decision packet in the inspected set; matches in the IRS universe are discovery metadata only. Excluded already-reviewed local clinics, SFAF/DOPE/HYA/Roots, Operation Access/PVF, Project Open Hand, vision and food banks. Existing kidney-access and SFDUU screens were considered rather than reopened under a new name.

Initial extra candidate **West Oakland Punks With Lunch was removed from the five independent ordinary-gift recipients**. Its [official donation page](https://www.punkswithlunch.org/donate) directs tax-deductible checks to fiscal sponsor HEPPAC and labels a memo for Punks With Lunch; [HEPPAC also hosts its donation route](https://heppac.org/support-pwl/). A project-restricted gift is not another unrestricted HEPPAC organization. The PWL page's printed EIN94-3025535 differs from current HEPPAC registry discovery94-3205535; resolve exact legal identity before any full packet, rather than silently correcting or using another city's PWL nonprofit.

The screen evaluates direct clinical/service mechanisms only. No policy-passage, legislative-impact or political-persuasion quantities are modeled. All break-even examples are algebraic gates, not effect estimates or selected optimistic central values. For a whole gift with health-bearing spending fraction a, complete cash/course C and financing additionality f, the course needs net health greater than C/(10,000×a×f). Outside real resources raise the requirement unless independently established savings offset them. Regional health must be allocated by actual residence; the full gift remains every regional numerator.

## 1. Via Heart Project — strongest priced hypothesis; defer pending mismatch test

**Actual Bay mechanism/cost:** [Current maintenance enrollment](https://viaheartproject.org/join/) posts$350/year for most AED models, including expiration monitoring, replacement batteries/pads, medical oversight and readiness support. [Its sponsorship request page](https://viaheartproject.org/donation-request/) accepts requests for equipment, maintenance and training; neither page is a promise that all unrestricted gifts buy unfunded high-risk sites. [The Peninsula Health Care District program](https://viaheartproject.org/phcd/) explicitly pays for local donated/reduced-cost devices, training and subsidized maintenance. This is verified San Mateo County service and public baseline, not proof of SF-resident health from its SF office. [San Mateo's May2019 grant](https://sanmateocounty.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=AFD40C15-EB79-46F4-8692-BD5D8C9753F4&ID=3945006) authorized up to$78,000 for20 South San Francisco school sites: historical bundled$3,900/site, not a2026 quote or spare donor tranche.

**Causal evidence:** [PAD trial, NEJM2004](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa040566) randomized993 community units to CPR-only versus a structured CPR-plus-AED response. Discharge survivors were30 versus15; functional status did not differ. The causal unit was the equipped/monitored response system, not one extra annual maintenance payment. Sites and devices differ; [the trial's adverse-event study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16784998/) reports1,716 devices in the AED arm, so a community-unit effect cannot be assigned to every individual device. A maintained AED's counterfactual may already be functioning, and public/paid commercial clients already fund many devices.

**Cheap gate:** even at the posted$350 maintenance fee, with illustrative a=f=.5, added maintenance needs>.14 QALY per financed AED-year. At a hypothetical finite5-QALY benefit per genuinely additional discharge survivor, it needs>2.8 percentage points of *incremental death prevention per AED-year*. This is not a predicted probability. A complete new-site package costs more and must include training, multiple devices if required, replacement/inspection labor and expected medical resources. Published cumulative uses/saved-lives claims do not supply a time-at-risk denominator or maintenance counterfactual.

**Fail/reopen test:** obtain/read a current publicly documented unfunded site list with arrest history/footfall, AED accessibility and baseline readiness, and which public/owner funding is unavailable. Determine new-response-package versus maintenance-only allocation. If the high-incidence accessible sites already have funded rescue-ready devices, reject the threshold hypothesis. No whole-gift model yet; we have a real price and trial but not matching marginal interventions.

## 2. Ceres Community Project — direct randomized operator link; exploratory mortality is insufficient

**Actual Bay activity/cost:** [Current home page](https://www.ceresproject.org/) reports226,787 meals in2025 and a combined food/teen-development mission. [2024 primary annual report](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/605b5dc033224821d80ad63a/t/681be50757f3b4560975e26e/1746658572358/Ceres%2B2024%2BAnnual%2BReport%2B-%2BDigital%2B(1).pdf) reports226,432 meals,1,489 clients and operating expense$5,082,274. Its70% category combines client meals and youth programming, not post-discharge clinical treatment alone. Operations exclude its support corporation/capital project. The report documents Partnership HealthPlan meal delivery in Napa/Solano; [DHCS](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/long-term-care-alternatives-home-and-community-based-service-options/medically-tailored-meals-pilot-program/) identifies Ceres's Marin/Sonoma role. Public/insurer care, kitchens, volunteer labor and youth programming remain within the whole-gift/resource boundary. No SF delivery claim follows from research partnership with Project Open Hand.

**Causal evidence:** Ceres [participated in KP NOURISH](https://www.ceresproject.org/kaiser-permanente-nourish), not merely cited generic nutrition research. The [investigator's primary2022 abstract](https://divisionofresearch.kaiserpermanente.org/publications/effect-of-medically-tailored-meals-on-clinical-outcomes-in-recently-hospitalized-high-risk-adults/) reports1,977 randomized patients:993 meal intervention and984 usual care. Primary90-day all-cause hospitalization was null (aHR1.02,95%CI.86–1.21). Mortality aHR.65 (.43–.98) was exploratory; investigators call for replication. Do not relabel it a confirmed35% mortality reduction for all Ceres clients or convert a hazard ratio directly to absolute prevented deaths. The current direct NIH full-text endpoint challenged access; no full table extraction or subgroup claim is made.

**Cheap gate:** the historical broad operating-expense/meal ratio is about$22.44, not a marginal meal price. Seventy meal-equivalents correspond to about$1,571 of broad historical operations. Even assigning *all* that proportional operating gift to a trial-like course and assuming50% financing additionality requires>.314 QALY/course before further post-discharge case-mix dilution. This ratio already allocates all operating costs to meals—do not add a second overhead/portfolio dilution to it. Family meals and nonmeal activity make it a screen, not a cost model. A finite90-day survival difference plus later mortality catch-up and utility is needed; multiplying an exploratory relative hazard by a lifetime is not acceptable.

**Fail/reopen test:** current complete cost and proportion of genuinely added post-discharge high-risk courses beyond insurer coverage; replicated/confirmatory absolute mortality or validated quality-of-life effect; short finite follow-up and resource savings with causal support. Current capital expansion does not itself establish clinical additionality. Rank second for bounded evidence diligence, defer full positive report.

## 3. HEPPAC — plausible cheap overdose access, but public/partner baseline unresolved

[Primary current services](https://heppac.org/) verify Alameda/Contra Costa mobile syringe services, naloxone, drug checking, wound care and HIV/HCV linkage. [Current EBDC page](https://heppac.org/east-bay-drug-checking-ebdc/) has monthly reports through July2026, supporting current activity. This is an East Bay operator, not proven SF benefit. The [December16,2025 county packet](https://www.alamedacountyca.gov/board/bos_calendar/documents/DocsAgendaReg_12_16_25/HEALTH%20CARE%20SERVICES/Regular%20Calendar/HCSA_397004.pdf) proposes extending the9/1/23–8/31/25 contract through8/31/26 and adding$700,000, from$979,768 to$1,679,768. Do not treat this multiyear amendment as a fresh private gift, final execution, or proof of renewal/cessation after August2026. [Alameda's current homeless-health services](https://health.alamedacountyca.gov/program/substance-use-treatment/) describes existing provider naloxone access and links HEPPAC.

**Gate:** with purely illustrative$50 complete added-access cash, a=f=.5 requires>.02 net QALY/course. Naloxone's rescue mechanism is real, but a reportable kit/refill is not an additional unique rescue; free state supplies, public staffing, PWL/NEED/other-network overlap and otherwise available EMS/peer kits matter. No complete current cost per otherwise-unserved person or residual rescue coverage effect was found. Drug-checking sample counts do not establish reduced mortality and cannot substitute for that bridge.

**Fail/reopen test:** current grant renewal/capacity, nonoverlapping uncovered witness networks, cost of actual peer outreach including failures, kit carriage/readiness and unique-person counterfactual use. Price staffing/service gaps, not retail medicine already publicly available. No full packet until identity and residual access/cost denominator are jointly supported.

## 4. Crisis Support Services of Alameda County — large current output, no marginal severe-health coefficient

[FY2024–25 primary impact page](https://www.crisissupport.org/mission) reports55,000 calls and8,000988 text sessions, plus18,000 education participants. It states partial Alameda behavioral-health funding. [Current crisis services](https://www.crisissupport.org/crisis-services) describes90-hour counselor training, supervision, possible follow-up and referrals to separate mobile crisis teams. Do not count those teams as its own new clinical capacity, or assume calls equal distinct people or deaths prevented. Program geographic residence is mainly Alameda but988/contract calls can originate elsewhere; its local role does not imply SF share.

**Gate:** an illustrative$50 full unique support course, a=f=.5, needs>.02 QALY/course. Call cost is not yet measured; a dying-person-versus-no-help comparator is not justified by all crisis calls. Neither an improved feeling at call end nor a count of calls avoided becoming emergency dispatches identifies a fatality difference. Coercive/dispatch harms, repeated callers and alternative national routing remain relevant.

**Fail/reopen test:** independently supported repeat-attempt/death or sustained-utility effect for its actual follow-up protocol, complete cost per unique added high-risk person, and otherwise unanswered/unsupported cohort beyond current988/public staffing. Defer rather than reusing a mortality assumption from another crisis-line report.

## 5. Berkeley Free Clinic — current cheap-access premise partly interrupted

[Current primary service page](https://www.berkeleyfreeclinic.org/) explicitly says limited services during relocation. Dental services are paused while space is built; TB testing notice says unavailable. Current STI screening/treatment, peer counseling, benefits navigation and partnered eyeglasses remain described. Do not assume historical dental/TB operation, infer that a gift automatically completes the buildout, or use zero patient charge as zero donor/resource cost. New co-location/clinical and optical partners' inputs require separate attribution. No evidence establishes SF resident benefit.

**Gate:** an illustrative$100 full course with a=.25 and f=.5 needs>.08 QALY per added clinical course. Neither screening alone, volunteers, visit counts nor broad free-care labels establish that gain. A serious-infection treatment pathway could matter but no current positive-case yield, completed otherwise-missed treatment, timing or full cost denominator was established.

**Fail/reopen test:** current funded service restoration plan and complete staff/volunteer/partner cost, then a narrow active outcome-bearing service with documented additional completed care and finite health. Until then, no full ordinary-gift model.

## Decision

No new model/report JSON, no count change, no Site work or outreach. Keep **Via Heart's incremental-ready high-incidence-site test** as the single next bounded research question. It is more decisive than another generic philanthropic cost-effectiveness model because its price and causal trial exist but presently price different interventions. Ceres remains an evidence-replication/cost-transfer reserve. Missing a quoted marginal offer alone is not the rejection reason: all five also lack the linked residual causal health and full-gift cost/population bridge needed for a credible threshold claim.
