## Material corrections

1. **Correct the net-asset classification everywhere.** The FY2025 Form 990 checks the box for an organization that does **not** follow FASB ASC 958. Line 27, “net assets without donor restrictions,” is blank. The $1,193,255 appears on line 31, “retained earnings, endowment, accumulated income, or other funds,” and line 32, total net assets/fund balances. Replace `fy2025NetAssetsWithoutRestrictionsUsd` with `fy2025NetAssetsOrFundBalancesUsd`; replace every “unrestricted net assets” claim in the model, report, content JSON, source ledger and RFMF with “total net assets/fund balances; restriction/availability status is not reported on this filing.” Cash of $642,665 and investments of $405,895 are balance-sheet assets, not proof that $1.049 million is unrestricted or deployable. This does not force a change to the 3%–35% additionality prior, but its rationale must rely on liquid-looking asset balances plus low liabilities, not a false restriction classification.

2. **Add the omitted current renovation/funding evidence to RFMF and the gross boundary.** The City of Berkeley's March 10, 2026 item states that BFC undertook **$196,809** of renovations at 830 University, that the renovated facility would expand capacity and clinic hours, and that BFC sought council contributions of up to $500 per councilmember to help cover those costs. Council minutes show the item was adopted. This is a real, current, priced capital use and evidence of a funding ask, but it is not a priced clinical-output tranche: the expenditure was already undertaken, the incremental clients/hours are unspecified, and assistance may replenish the balance sheet rather than cause new service. Add the primary source: <https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-03-10%20Item%2012%20Berkeley%20Free%20Clinic%20Critical%20Renovations%20Relinquishment.pdf>. State whether the $196,809 is outside, capitalized within, or otherwise unreconciled to the FY2025 expense denominator; do not silently count it as current operating expense or add it to gross resources without accounting evidence.

## Exact bounded wording/source corrections

3. **Fix the Oregon result wording.** “Reduced depression screening” is wrong. The experiment found a lower rate of positive depression screens / lower depression prevalence, while increasing diagnosis and care use. Use “reduced the rate of positive depression screens” or “reduced measured depression prevalence.” Apply this in the Markdown and any corresponding report-content narrative.

4. **Do not call the 31,000-hour source merely ‘dated’ without its age/status.** The archival UC Berkeley opportunity-list PDF itself gives no visible publication date; search indexing places it roughly 13 years before this audit. Label it an **undated, approximately 2013 archival opportunity listing**, and preserve that the 25,833-hour figure additionally assumes historical hours per volunteer remain constant when scaling 180 people to the current 150. The resulting $1.037 million volunteer value and 3.286× factor remain permissible only as an explicitly illustrative diagnostic—not observed current gross resources, a lower bound, or replacement cost.
