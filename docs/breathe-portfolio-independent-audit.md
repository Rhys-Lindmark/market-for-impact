## Finite cessation and counterfactual

Correctly replaces a lifetime per-quitter multiplier with two living states plus absorbing death. Initial difference is one extra former smoker replacing one current smoker at the six-month outcome, scaled by extra quits among all offered adults and distinct funding additionality. Later background quitting and relapse operate in both arms. Integrating utility-weighted state differences counts survival and morbidity once; no extra disease or life-saving multiplier is added. Central follow-up ends ten years after that outcome, discounted from the gift with .5-year delay. No pre-six-month health is credited.

[Lung Health Study primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15710956/) supports the mechanism and finite mortality-follow-up context; treatment-arm outcomes are not passed off as conditional smoker/former-smoker state hazards or a Breathe course effect. Hazards, utility, incremental quit probability and transfer remain explicit priors. Cohort-calendar mortality recovery does not track each person's time since later quitting/relapse; the report exposes that approximation rather than concealing it. No-mortality-benefit, relapse, background quitting, short-horizon and signed-harm stresses remain.

## Asthma source and population boundary

Primary Healthy Homes II record concerns children and incremental nurse-plus-CHW support versus nurse care; the packet correctly treats the reported symptom-day contrast as a finite morbidity bridge, not QALYs or no-treatment efficacy. Direct PMC access returned a challenge in this audit; indexed primary content and author source interpretation were available. The [2024 Philadelphia randomized trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapediatrics/fullarticle/2825197) independently confirms 626 children and no overall primary between-group asthma-control advantage; pandemic disruption and secondary findings mean it is not proof all CHW programs are ineffective. Packet appropriately uses it to motivate null uncertainty, not erase the other evidence.

The 24.4 annualized symptom-free days receive separate .5 local transfer and .1 utility-gap priors, spread uniformly over at most one year after delay. The .6 child share limits health to 4.375 additional children centrally. All-age costs and harms remain. Adult smokers and child asthma recipients are disjoint; no secondhand-smoke, caregiver or household multiplier is added. Child survival benefit is not claimed. Existing [Kick It California care](https://kickitca.org/faqs), insurance and asthma payer services remain in baseline rather than disappearing without philanthropy.
