# California youth and mobility acceptance

Youth ALIVE!, Vision To Learn and Walk San Francisco are accepted as initial, conditional research reports, not giving recommendations. California reaches 13/25; USA remains 12/25. Every new edition remains at 0/10 accepted in-depth reviews.

The models retain full recipient gift costs and prominently identify the limited health pathways counted. Youth ALIVE! gives a signed scenario-weighted illustration of $27.34 million per 10 California QALYs; Vision To Learn gives a conditional $2.00 million and Walk SF $4.44 million. These are subjective marginal models, not measured charity effects. All include substitution, finite health horizons and zero-effect cases; Youth and Walk include explicit negative scenarios. Uncredited portfolio effects are unknown, not proven absent.

Independent Astra Medium review reproduced model arithmetic and reopened selected decisive primary sources. Original returns revealed separately netted event costs: Youth gross expenses are $5,360,166 / $6,466,145 / $7,328,818 for 2022–2024 (mean $6,385,043); Walk SF gross expenses are $903,009 / $947,214 / $992,695 for FY2023–2025 (mean $947,639.33). The corrections affect organizational size, not the independently normalized gift models. Original-source links are in each report. Vision To Learn's three-year mean remains $23,073,076.33.

Youth's 96% without reinjury is uncontrolled monitoring, not effectiveness. Its five-year QALY anchor is a published Markov model with serious limitations; the favorable 5% scenario supplies 66.1% of weighted benefit. The current $5 million state award overlaps the modeled service. Vision To Learn's 40% marginal California share, effective wear and child utility are explicit judgments; no automatic Medi-Cal match is credited. Course delivery cost and separate support allocation do not double-charge overhead. Walk SF's probability of earlier implementation is hypothetical; existing cameras and funded city plans are not new philanthropic impact. Its constructed severe-injury baseline is not an observed 2025 count.

Audit limitations: selected decisive evidence was reopened, not every source replicated. The independent review could not reopen Youth's NICE and BSCC project PDFs; retained author evidence is not described as independently reproduced. Research author intervals remain unchanged; independent review and integration are not relabeled as author time. No beta credit is claimed.
