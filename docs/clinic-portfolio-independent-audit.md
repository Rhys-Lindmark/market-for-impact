## What changed enough to resolve v1's block

V1 quantified dental relief/counseling while leaving most medical work without health estimates. V2 adds a selected hypertension-access survival mechanism and **six separately costed, timed and clinically described residual groups**: acute/rheumatologic symptom care; non-BP symptomatic medication care; completed specialty/referral treatment; non-extraction dental; other mental/psychiatric care; food-related morbidity. They have different target/response probabilities, utility priors, catchup hazards, duration limits and outside resources. Non-BP medical spending is split40/40/20 across its three groups, while remaining dental/mental/food budget follows the original gift allocations.

This is not a generic multiplier added to preserve the original price. Decomposition changes outputs. The model preserves costs of failed/non-target courses, excludes duplicate credited patient-periods and uses a shared residual capacity cap. Independent checks confirm BP + selected dental + selected counseling + residual budgets equal the whole gift in all scenarios. Residual costs are not added as a second gift; outside resources are added once under their stated exclusions.

The named pathway identities are supported by the clinic's actual [medical](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/services), [mental](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/dental-1), [food](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/food-pharmacy) and [eligibility](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/eligibility) pages. **The numerical case mix, costs, utilities and responsiveness are analyst judgments, not observed clinical effects.** The differing mechanisms/time assumptions are sufficiently explicit for conditional research; they do not demonstrate clinical completeness. Rare prevention tails, BP nonfatal morbidity and indirect benefits remain unresolved, and the report says so. Their omission is not claimed evidence of zero benefit. A measured total-organization return remains unavailable.

## Funded care and finite health

[HDFP](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/490882/) is systematic stepped hypertension care versus community referral, not untreated generic primary-care patients. Its five-year mortality risks imply a constant-hazard approximation, not an observed first-year effect. Local current hazard, modern care-gap and implementation transfer are visibly separate priors. Trial adherence is not multiplied again as though the source were per-protocol. The [Oregon randomized access contrast](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23635051/) appropriately cautions against assuming all added access lowers BP.

The BP survival curves diverge only after specified latency during funded care and then use equal hazards; the remaining survival gap is integrated only to finite H. Analytic horizon does not purchase treatment years. **Both donor and outside annual BP course costs now scale by max(1,funded years)**, resolving the draft's free-duration possibility. All-cause mortality is not stacked with separately credited fatal MI/stroke. Cohorts are explicitly alive at treatment start; delay discounts only. Per-person harms are gift-datePV, not discounted twice.

Dental finite relief and triangular counseling remain coherent. Group-specific acute/chronic/referral and food intervals are finite; referral benefit is conditional on completed appropriate care with downstream resource allowances, not an exam/scan count. Chronic benefits are not lifetime diabetes prevention. The uncertainty is in the parameters and mixture, not concealed indefinite duration.
