# Harm Reduction Action Center — conditional Denver alpha (29 September 2026)

The Harm Reduction Action Center (HRAC), a fiscally sponsored project of CNDC, is accepted as an **illustrative initial research report**, not a donor recommendation. The central model is **$884,837 per 10 Denver-metro QALYs**. It is a scenario based on observed project accounting cost but judged risk exposure, causal effect, survival, funding response, and metro residence. The donor decision remains **HOLD**. It is not a priced marginal gift or measured cost-effectiveness.

The independent reviewer read the original [2024 CNDC audit](https://cndc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CNDC-2024-Audited-Financials.pdf) and [2025 CNDC audit](https://cndc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CNDC-2025-Audited-Financial-Statements.pdf). The HRAC combining-schedule expenses are $2,017,429 in 2024 and $1,884,959 in 2025. They already include project intercompany support and recorded in-kind resources; no extra sponsor allocation or in-kind gross-up is added. The audit is of CNDC's consolidated statements, with the project schedules covered by an in-relation-to opinion, not a separate HRAC audit.

An [original Denver council presentation](https://denver.legistar.com/View.ashx?GUID=513792A7-945D-4508-A948-D6765652EFE2&ID=13628013&M=F) says the project serves more than 4,000 people annually. The model rounds this to 4,000 as a historical reach proxy, not a matched or audited opioid-risk cohort. The [2026 contract exhibit](https://denver.legistar.com/View.ashx?GUID=56F973ED-72FC-4E98-91F4-3428409794AE&ID=15815597&M=F) establishes Denver delivery and targets, not completed results or client residence. A public [gift page](https://www.harmreductionactioncenter.org/donate) establishes solicitation, but the linked checkout could not be verified and a new donation's additional capacity is unknown. The model's 22.8 local QALYs and all sensitivity calculations were independently reproduced; zero-benefit scenarios have no finite price.
