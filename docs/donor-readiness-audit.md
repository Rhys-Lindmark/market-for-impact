# GiveBetter: cheapest-ten recommendation eligibility audit

September 11, 2026. Read-only audit of current `work/market-for-impact-v2-contents`, supplemented by live original/official sources. No Site edits, publication, outreach or transactions. Space Saver and Token Saver applied: reused source reports and installed runtime; workspace baseline 62 MB and filesystem 61 GiB available; no installs or bulk downloads.

## Decision

The homepage should not automatically turn the four cheapest models into “Our Top Charities.” On the present evidence, **none of these ten has a verified marginal funding offer sufficient for a confident health-based major-gift recommendation**. That does not mean none is a worthwhile charity. Several are well-established, transparent organizations worth donor diligence. Organizational legitimacy, an accounting ratio, a credible intervention, and the next donation's effect are separate questions.

HOPE should be excluded from a recommended-giving shortlist until annual whole-organization financials and the additional coverage bought by cash are established. Its absent detailed filing is an evidence gap, not evidence of fraud. Hearing and Speech has a different, more concrete problem: a documented historical-EIN automatic-revocation record with no verified reinstatement or successor recipient. ReCARES' strong-looking price is accompanied by substantial reported financial assets and no verified cash bottleneck.

A page explicitly called “Research leads under review” could feature ReCARES, PHC, Compass and SFAF with their distinct limitations; this is an editorial diligence queue, not a new effectiveness ranking or a recommendation to distribute $10 million among them. If the homepage promises vetted charitable recommendations, an empty/under-review state is more defensible than silently filling four positions from the price sort.

## Per-organization evidence and decisive next evidence

### 1. ReCARES

The existing September 11 source audit inspected the [original FY2025 990-EZ](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202621259349200012/IRS990EZ) and [Schedule O](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202621259349200012/IRS990ScheduleO): $72,583 whole accrual-accounting expense, over 11,000 reported recipients, over 45,000 items, and $313,082 net assets including $253,225 cash/savings/investments and $60,000 grants receivable. This is unusually inexpensive reported delivery, but recipients are not verified unique safe users; accounting expense excludes much donated equipment/labor. This audit reused that inspected original-source record; its direct web reopen was unavailable, so no fresh original-return verification is claimed.

Whole cost is retained but health is only modeled equipment use. Utility and effective use duration remain judgments. The favorable world contributes 69.3% of the weighted net benefit. Neither existing assets nor their restrictions prove abundant discretionary cash, but the public record does not establish cash starvation. Before recommending: reconcile current restricted/unrestricted funds with a costed additional-capacity or service-preservation plan and safe, unique recipient follow-up. Do not extrapolate its illustrative $10,000 gift to $10 million.

### 2. HOPE Pacifica

The [official organization page](https://www.hope4change650.org/about) currently describes a Pacifica 501(c)(3); existing identity evidence identifies EIN 99-2104067. Its [naloxone locations page](https://www.hope4change650.org/narcan-locations-information) and the [adopted county agreement](https://sanmateocounty.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=CDE2FD32-C53E-4466-A63B-8423077CEAAC&ID=7523215&Options=&Search=) establish more than a merely hypothetical organization. They do not establish annual expense, stock availability, unique at-risk reach, or the additional effect of gifts. The current whole-expense input is a $20,000–$100,000 analyst prior; the central $40,000 is not a filing. A $10,000 county grant is not an annual operating budget.

The [county free-access program](https://www.smchealth.org/post/overdose-prevention-coalition-launches-naloxone-program-expands-free-access-county) includes distribution hardware as well as medication-related access. No residual cash bottleneck is verified. Request basic annual financials, bank/grant reconciliation, filing/status documentation appropriate to the entity, replenishment/coverage records, and a specific cash-funded plan. A missing detailed 990 alone does not establish delinquency or misconduct; small organizations may have different filing obligations. Keep the low-price model as explicitly unsupported financial research rather than a homepage endorsement.

### 3. Project Homeless Connect

The [current official about page](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/about/) identifies PHC as a project of Community Initiatives. The [city grant amendment record](https://hsh.archive.sf.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Consent-Item-11.21-Community-Initiatives-PHC.pdf) supplies external operational corroboration, a multiyear funding history and historical monitoring without fiscal findings; it authorized funding through June 2026, so it is not proof of a post-June-2026 extension. A missing standalone PHC 990 is expected under fiscal sponsorship, not a legitimacy strike.

The model keeps the full project gift but quantifies only optical/hearing/dental health; it is not Community Initiatives' entire sponsored portfolio. [Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/) supports real services but not assumed marginal costs, gift allocation or additional completed treatment. Resolve current project accounts, sponsor fees/restrictions and FY2027 funding; obtain a costed additional-care plan and separate partner contribution. Deduplicate Hearing and Speech's shared patients. Eligible as a conditional research lead, not a verified offer.

### 4. Hearing and Speech Center

The existing September 9 primary-source refresh is decisive and more current than the headline model's June cutoff: fresh downloads reproduced the IRS revocation record for EIN 94-1322198, effective November 15, 2025, posted March 10, 2026, with blank reinstatement; the August 11 California business-master-file extract had no exact EIN/name match. The exact row and download hashes are preserved in `docs/research-queue/20260909/mfi-hsc-recipient-refresh.md`. See [IRS datasets](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search-bulk-data-downloads). A live check of the [IRS-derived ProPublica record](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/941322198) still flags absence from the recent exempt-organization list.

This does not establish closure, fraud or the impossibility of later reinstatement. An active clinic/NPI and an online donation page do not clear the recipient discrepancy. Require an actual reinstatement record or formally documented successor/sponsor with current eligibility, then current accounts and additional supported-fitting capacity. The $1.25M model is a conditional supported-adult-hearing course, not whole-organization impact. Do not substitute PHC as recipient without documentation.

### 5. Compass Family Services

The [FY2025 independent audit](https://www.compass-sf.org/_files/ugd/13331a_cd52c427b2074bb1ae6195c5aded399b.pdf) was opened in this audit: unmodified opinion; schedule reports no financial-statement, federal-award or other compliance findings. The existing report derives $9,704 per family from $2.009M C-Rent expense and 207 prevention-classified families. That is a historical program accounting ratio, not an additional-family quote. Current programs and [annual reporting](https://www.compass-sf.org/annual-report) support real delivery.

The directory's health number applies to C-Rent; it does not price an unrestricted gift across childcare, housing and the full organization. External prevention research is transferred into a subjective local health model. A credible diligence candidate if a restricted C-Rent allocation is actually available: verify current capacity, eligible applicant flow, unit-cost reconciliation, existing public contracts and a gift-funded incremental cohort. No $10M capacity inferred from organizational scale.

### 6. Hamilton Families

The [FY2025 annual report](https://hamiltonfamilies.org/annual-report) and the existing reviewed [original FY2023 990](https://hamiltonfamilies.org/s/FY22-23-Form-990.pdf) establish organizational reporting and a broad housing portfolio. The current report cites 127 families avoiding homelessness, 344 maintaining housing and $17.41M revenue in FY2025. These are reporting/accounting facts, not causal prevention effects.

The favorable directory placement depends on an assumed prevention cost and a transferred randomized-study effect, without prevention-only accounts, applicant flow, comparison-linked outcomes or a current marginal plan. It is not a whole-organization estimate. Keep as research pending those program-specific facts and current original financials; broader organizational credibility cannot validate its $/QALY estimate.

### 7. Friends of the Urban Forest

The existing September 11 original-source review inspected the [FY2025 990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202611049349302071/IRS990): $5,410,095 whole expense. Its official annual report describes 1,506 street trees; separate city reporting almost matches. The [current giving site](https://www.friendsoftheurbanforest.org/support-us) and [city project reporting](https://www.sfcta.org/blogs/transportation-sales-tax-funds-cable-cars-trees) support an active organization with public funding.

Whole expense is charged, but only tree-related health is quantified. Planting counts are not surviving canopy exposure or causal QALYs; additionality must account for existing planting/maintenance funding. Not a strong health-based homepage recommendation on this evidence. Require a costed unfunded tranche, watering/site constraints, survival/growth cohorts, and a more defensible health bridge. Aesthetic, environmental and workforce value may matter without being assigned invented health.

### 8. MELP/AbleCloset

The [current MELP site](https://www.freemedequip.org/) explicitly identifies EIN 27-1212734 and operating pickup hours at two sites; the reviewed [FY2024 original return](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/271212734/202530449349200108/full) and public health-district grant records support legal/operating substance. Pediatric and adult activities share a legal organization; the model should preserve both costs.

The existing September 11 model audit warns about a forecast denominator, historical reserves, public support and unvalidated utility assumptions. Its 3%-weight favorable world supplies 91.8% of weighted expected net health. [Pediatric instructions](https://ablecloset.com/how-it-works/) explicitly require an outside therapist for safety/sizing; lending alone does not establish appropriate sustained use. Hold pending a priced cash-triggered expansion/preservation plan, reserve restrictions and unique safe-use data. No claim of wrongdoing is supported by these gaps.

### 9. Code Tenderloin

The existing September 8 review matched the current [donation page](https://www.codetenderloin.org/donate) to primary IRS California data for Code Tenderloin Inc, EIN 93-3030657. Its [ambassador program](https://www.codetenderloin.org/community-ambassador-program) and the [September 2026 DPH navigation guide](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Treatment_Navigation_Guide.pdf) support current service existence. Historical Center for New Music filings/contracts cannot automatically stand in for the independent entity's accounts or transferred funding obligations.

The whole $100,000 modeled gift is retained, but the 25% peer-response allocation and 50% financing additionality are judgments; most portfolio health is unquantified. Hold until current independent financial statements, sponsor/contract transition, public funding and an actual extra peer-response plan are reconciled. Do not treat older sponsor expenses or a proposed city contract as a current marginal private funding offer.

### 10. San Francisco AIDS Foundation

The live [financial-document index](https://www.sfaf.org/about/financial-legal-documents/) identifies EIN 94-2927405 and links a FY2025 audit plus FY2024 Form 990. The [FY2025 auditor's report](https://res.cloudinary.com/dxca8bsxf/image/upload/v1764782541/San-Francisco-AIDS-Foundation-Jun25AR-Final_hi9fek.pdf), opened here, gives an unmodified financial-statement opinion. This is substantially stronger organizational documentation than HOPE's unobserved expense prior.

The full-gift model nevertheless assigns 15% to overdose work, 20% to PrEP and 65% to other unquantified work by judgment. Additional patient reach, existing public/insurance/340B support and marginal funding room are unmeasured. A suitable organization-level diligence lead, but no verified claim that an ordinary gift purchases the historical targeted-naloxone return. Request its current marginal budget and clinical capacity by pathway. Strong accounts do not establish ability to deploy an extra $10M at modeled effectiveness.

## Implementation implication

Keep the directory's numeric sort available as research. Make homepage inclusion an explicit editorial evidence decision with a recorded reason; do not derive it from `.slice(0,4)`. Explain on each card whether it is a vetted giving recommendation, conditional diligence lead, or exploratory model. Evidence deficiencies must remain specific: missing expense records, unresolved recipient status, program-versus-whole-gift mismatch, uncertain health effect, and unverified marginal funding are not interchangeable.

For any major gift, obtain the recipient's actual spending plan, baseline committed funding, current reserves/restrictions, binding operational constraints, timeline and reporting milestones. The scope should follow the concrete proposal; no arbitrary budget multiple or universal numerical cutoff is introduced. A large gift may need staged commitments, but these sources do not support selecting a tranche size today.
