# California and USA alpha batch acceptance

These are exploratory research reports, not beta acceptance or top-pick endorsements. Author research intervals are recorded separately from integration and deployment. Existing SF reports and models are preserved.

## Operation Access

Accepted for California alpha integration after the following checks:

- Current official patient eligibility includes Northern California and the Central Valley. Direct California attribution is therefore modeled at 100%; continuing residence is an assumption, not a measured lifetime geography outcome.
- The original 2025 audit statement shows $22,876,888 in expenses, including $20,530,000 in waived clinical charges and $89,922 in other in-kind support. Subtracting these yields $2,256,966, not cash flow and not the Form 990 expense total. Waived charges are not net economic resource costs.
- The original 2025 Form 990 page 1 confirms expenses of $2,261,505 and the prior-year $2,678,152. The research packet supplies the original 2023 return's $2,658,742. Their comparable return-based average is $2,532,799.67. The May 7, 2026 signature is not treated as a publication date.
- A regression test reconstructs all seven published scenarios directly from the report's numerical inputs and 21 pathway vectors. It reproduces the central 0.5136735945 QALYs per $100,000 normalization, plus favorable, shortened-duration, alternative-care, null and adverse cases. No Bay-specific dilution is inherited.
- The central price is approximately $1.9 million per 10 California QALYs. Case mix, utility, additionality and alternative-care timing remain judgment calls; specialty service counts do not identify unique beneficiaries or causal effects. Gross resource costs are distinct from donor costs.

Evidence: original URLs and retrieval dates in the report registry; `scripts/geography-reports.test.mjs` contains reproducibility assertions. No beta or marginal-funding approval is implied.

## Legal Action Center

Accepted for USA alpha integration with an unestimated organization-level price:

- Original FY2025 Form 990 page 1 confirms the April 2024–March 2025 period, EIN, $8,981,593 expenses, prior-year $8,652,021 expenses, $6,468,412 revenue and $11,940,561 net assets. The packet's prior return provides FY2023 expenses of $8,187,349; the return-based average is $8,606,987.67.
- Annual-report expenses include donated services and differ from the Form 990. An additional revenue discrepancy is retained rather than converted into an invented runway estimate. Fiscal-year legal-service counts and calendar-year annual-report counts are not combined.
- The October 2025 Disability Rights North Carolina settlement concerns two named facilities, a policy/training remedy and monitoring, with partner attribution. It does not establish national treatment uptake or health outcomes attributable to LAC.
- A conditional treatment-year model exposes the enabling-cost hurdle and downstream treatment resources. It does not supply missing organization-specific implementation yield or repeat-person survival accounting. Policy passage, legal cases and settlement dollars are not counted as QALYs.
- The donation checkout remains unverified, so the report does not publish a verified donation button. Original financial and policy sources remain inspectable.

Evidence: source registry and model skeleton; structural tests ensure an unestimated report has explicit blocking inputs. This is a research lead, not a donation recommendation.

## End Overdose

Accepted for USA and California alpha integration. An independent research reviewer reproduced all nonzero national scenarios and checked the clinical transfer sources. Root separately retrieved the original FY2024 filing: total expenses $970,440, program $730,276, management $207,274, fundraising $32,890, cash $20,042 and net assets negative $16,572. The current campaign explicitly reports 25,000 manufacturer-contributed naloxone units, not 25,000 kits or lives saved.

The USA central is approximately $1.5 million per ten QALYs, with explicit audience-risk, delivery, duplicate-recipient, alternative-rescue, funding and finite-survival assumptions. Donated-stock costs are shown separately. California keeps the identical all-population clinical scenarios but leaves California health unestimated: chapter presence and a California kit offer are not an outcome-weighted marginal share. No population-share shortcut or California recommendation is introduced.

Regression checks reproduce clinical arithmetic, preserve the null California denominator and deduplicate the shared 448-second session. The two edition reports reference 865 unique researcher-seconds in total (448 shared national plus 417 California-specific), not 1,313 seconds. Root integration and general audit time are excluded from author headers.
