# California advocacy estimate revision

Accepted 14 September 2026. Updates WorkSafe and Disability Rights California; no additional reports or top-four endorsements.

## Scope and evidence

- WorkSafe: original FY 2025 audit supports $1,714,887 net expense plus $24,503 netted donor-event benefits, or $1,739,390 gross. Impact-report contents say 36 legal services while page 14 says 40. These are historical outputs, not inspections or measured marginal productivity. The model uses 36 and explicitly varies it to 40.
- The original Levine–Toffel–Johnson inspection study supports a 9.4% injury-claim reduction and descriptive mean of 3.43 annual claims. Neither establishes current WorkSafe baseline risk. Current State Auditor responses place already-implemented July 2026 reforms in the counterfactual.
- DRC: original annual table supports $46,894,183 expenses and 22,297 assistance requests. OCRA separately reports 6,406 clients, 10,475 issues and 4,899 information/referral closures. Selected case stories establish mechanisms, not success rates.
- Original DRC FY 2025 Form 990 is now retrievable. $46,787,232 functional expenses plus $110,521 direct event costs equals $46,897,753, $3,570 above the annual table. The original-access blocker is removed; fiscal comparability and the three-year mean remain unresolved.

## Interpretation

The estimates charge all recipient costs while quantifying only one health pathway. They are not comprehensive organization returns or demonstrated lower bounds. The table and report explicitly identify the partial scope. Gift additionality, conversion rates, health utility and duration are analyst judgments; the underlying observations do not identify them. Central prices are approximately $161.3 million and $64.8 million per 10 California QALYs respectively. Positive stress tests are not confidence bounds. Each model retains zero and negative scenarios and identifies unpriced public or employer resources.

## Verification

Independent root source review covered original financial statements, current implementation records and primary study text. Dedicated regression tests reproduce all ten scenarios, preserve negative outcomes, check partial-scope labels and prevent an unjustified DRC three-year average. Existing threshold tests retain their remaining unestimated organizations. Header checks use recorded author intervals: WorkSafe approximately 14 minutes and DRC 13 minutes, not manager integration time.
