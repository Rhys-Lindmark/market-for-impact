The [original FY2025 impact report](https://lionslighthouse.aflip.in/fy25impactreport) identifies 1,114 Atlanta Public Schools students receiving vision examinations; it does **not** prove that all 1,114 received glasses. The independent audit corrected the model to include an unmeasured prescription-needed/fulfilled share, 0.5 in the conditional scenario and 0 to 1 in alternatives. The report gives no modeled credit to screenings alone or to the statewide surgery count, which cannot be assigned to cataracts or Atlanta-metro residents. A [cross-sectional teen-myopia utility study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1771541/) and a [randomized spectacle-wear study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5847082/) inform plausibility but do not measure a causal Atlanta QALY gain.
