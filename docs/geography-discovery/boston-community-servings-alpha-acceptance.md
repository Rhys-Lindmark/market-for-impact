# Boston: Community Servings alpha acceptance (29 September 2026)

Decision: accept a **conditional initial report**, not a donor recommendation or a validated whole-organization return. Boston is 1/25 initial reports after this integration; the beta count remains 0/10. The report remains donor HOLD.

Independent source and model review checked the [provider-specific randomized crossover trial](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11606-018-4716-z), the [2026 observational Medicaid comparison](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04407-5), the [original Form 990 for the fiscal year ended in 2025 (tax year 2024)](https://www.servings.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2024-Form-990-for-CS-Public-Copy.pdf), the [FY2025 audit](https://www.servings.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/FY25-Financials.pdf), and the [FY2025 annual report](https://www.servings.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/FY25-Annual-Report-Community-Servings-Pages-reduced-1.pdf). Gross recipient expense reconciles to $21,894,207 in Part IX plus $395,291 of direct event expenses, or $22,289,498. The five observed Massachusetts counties in the Boston MSA account for 6,139 of 7,973 reported individuals; 80 people lacking county allocation and unobserved New Hampshire service receive no local credit.

The trial's native secondary outcome is 3.94 fewer days per 30 when mental health interfered with quality of life during a 12-week meal period. It is not a measured QALY. The report converts that outcome with explicit, unvalidated utility, course-completion, population-transfer and financing-response assumptions. Fourteen scenarios independently recompute; the conditional central is 0.710333 Boston-metro QALYs and about $314 million per ten. Null and adverse cases remain. This is a narrow diabetes mental-health illustration; omitted portfolio benefits or harms and a new gift's effect are not established.

The independent audit initially challenged publication because utility and gift response are unmeasured. Rechecking the A25 gate showed that transparent conditional priors are permissible at this stage when the native endpoint, population, geography and cost anchors are real and limitations are prominent. Acceptance therefore does not authorize a top-four ranking or claim of open funding room. Beta would need patient-linked dose and county data, a stronger preference-based health conversion, current costed capacity, financing substitution and broader portfolio assessment.
