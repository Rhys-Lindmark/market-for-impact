# DRC initial income correction, October 7

Fresh primary receipts: https://www.disabilityrightsca.org/2025-annual-report/impact reports FY2025 individual lump awards over $2.62m and ongoing benefits over $958,000/month, separately $306,000 systemic lump awards, 22,297 requests; WIPA 197 referrals, 99 benefit analyses, 341 active cases. These are reported gross outputs, not causal household consumption.

https://www.disabilityrightsca.org/system/files/file-attachments/Exhibit%20C%20-%20Advocacy%20Report.FINAL__0.pdf pp1–5: protective supervision and retroactive payments; restored nursing; eliminated medical debt; working-disabled coverage; purchased supplemental premiums reduce share of cost; reinstated SSDI with debt waived. Mechanisms support resources and service access; selected stories cannot establish representative amounts, success, unique households or new-gift capacity.

Decision: preserve original health model and all scenarios. Holistic conditional resource model anchors gross outputs to $2.62m individual lump awards once plus $958k monthly benefits for six future months, not a retroactive annual guarantee. Assume 10% of R*.6 deduplicated opportunities receive the joint awards, 80% retained after taxes/benefit withdrawals, half positive value remains after health/security overlap, and subtract full $100 first-year premiums/commuting/admin/opportunity burden. All distribution, duration, net-retention, overlap and baseline coefficients are judgments. The lump is one-off, not repeated; household jointly receives both components to avoid counting the same person twice. Systemic awards, WIPA earnings and other portfolio effects remain unknown; no gross benefits or ordinary survivor earnings enter directly. Existing contracts, self-advocacy, other counsel and public programs remain the alternative. No causal net household estimate is observed. Original health-only price remains diagnostic. Public financing transfer incidence is tested disjointly, alternatively to additional real resources in cost denominator, never both for the same expense.

Space baseline: filesystem 23GiB available; reuse read-only source checkout and Node, no dependencies/build copies. Substantive author cap180 seconds, root120 seconds; no nested delegation.
