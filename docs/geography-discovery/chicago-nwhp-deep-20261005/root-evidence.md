## Calibration and material correction

The central estimate remains $13,017,467 per better life: $642,680 divided by 0.372239 clinical QALYs plus 0.121467 signed income-equivalent healthy years, multiplied by ten. The 0.03 utility gain and household resource gains remain explicit conditional judgments, not measured NWHP impacts. Health and resource pathways, attribution, delay, harms, overlap and counterfactuals are separately exposed. This is a recognized annual-work boundary, not a priced next gift or a complete world-net estimate.

One correction was required: repeat only recurring resource flows in later years, not one-off repairs or process costs. A zero-benefit horizon still retains the full one-off burden. Household resource gains use joint logarithms; negative costs are never overlap-discounted. Clinical null scenarios retain separately supported resource assumptions. Unknown full costs remain unpriced rather than silently zero.

Initial price and full original report are retained once in initial-diagnostic.json, referenced from the accepted model. An unchanged central price is not evidence that a deeper review was omitted: duration, public funding, clinical-null and counterparty scenarios substantially change the interpretation and range.
