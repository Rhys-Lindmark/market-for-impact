## Mechanism, causal evidence and geography

[Official FAQ](https://cribsforkids.org/faq/) confirms partner product purchases fund programs, no annual partner reporting requirement, required safe-sleep education, and donated regional shipping for orders of10+. [Georgia multisite original study, PMID34209643](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34209643/) has615 paired surveys and66 follow-ups;66.1% planned a recommended sleep location without the supplied crib. Publisher/PMC direct access was blocked; the original study's indexed abstract corroborates those counts, and this counterfactual percentage is also in the unchanged prior verified row. It is a self-reported alternative, not necessarily an actual purchase or an unbiased national population coefficient. [Michigan75-family original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29472971/) found70/75 supplied cribs used at a visit,67/75 without unsafe items, but only42/75 renounced bed-sharing. Observed supplied-crib use is not all-sleep adherence. [2026 Kansas qualitative study](https://journals.ku.edu/kjm/article/view/25004), eleven families with infants2–11months, documents multiple sleep arrangements despite helpful cribs. This is qualitative warning evidence, not an incidence estimate.

[Bedtime Basics original evaluation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25331608/) reports bed-sharing38% postnatal versus16% follow-up and crib sleeping51% versus90%, but uncontrolled, incompletely paired follow-up cannot identify national marginal causal effect.
