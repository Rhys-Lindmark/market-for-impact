# Denver deep-review proposal

Compare all 25 accepted initial reports, not their partial-channel prices alone. The ten-review order is HRAC, KIND, Hunger Free Colorado, Rebuilding Together Colorado, Energy Outreach Colorado, Doctors Care, Immunize Colorado, Cultivando, Thriving Families, and PPRM. This is a research-priority proposal, not ten endorsed giving offers or a precise expected-value ranking.

The first two need mandatory metric repair: HRAC currently lacks a signed-income assessment; KIND lacks a production central scenario, so its reportPrice is null, not zero. Both have concrete clinical mechanisms and modest local costs worth examining. HRAC's annual attribution must not silently retain an additional marginal-donor discount, and both gift routes remain unresolved.

Hunger Free and Energy Outreach test substantial household resource flows against real public-payer/utility alternatives. EOC is deliberately included despite its high current partial price: the observed bill-channel cost is far narrower than the whole-recipient numerator, and furnace/efficiency benefits are omitted. Rebuilding adds repair safety/function and actually displaced spending, not property retail value. Doctors Care needs patient clinical units separated from household resource grouping and signed fee/time costs preserved. Immunize needs vaccine-specific protection and partner delivery costs rather than publicity or dose counts as utility.

Cultivando is prioritized for an implemented-abatement test, not because adopted benchmarks or monitoring prove health. Thriving Families needs original local perinatal-program evidence and compatible prenatal/postpartum dose, avoiding uncritical ROSE transfer. PPRM offers primary comparative health and four-year economic mechanisms, but regional costs, Denver-resident allocation and provider alternatives—not its headline price—are the decision-changing uncertainties.

The selection JSON gives a concrete question for each priority, one comparative reason for each of 15 exclusions, and all 25 frozen current report prices, health/resource components, model versions and registry hash. Close alternates are Second Wind and DCAC; they may replace a selected candidate if a bounded source challenge establishes a more learnable current cohort or no actionable funding path for a selected candidate. Jobs, housing and policy candidates were assessed directly: SOL's actual youth employment lacks incremental net wages; TREE has no observed earnings; Bicycle has no bounded implemented-project cohort and an unusable intake; Benefits in Action has a post-food-closure cost mismatch; CCHI bill savings need actual collectible-cash incidence. None is excluded merely for an unfavorable partial price or assumed zero impact.

No report, model, cohort count or organization timing was changed. Root alone challenges, stores the accepted queue and dispatches one subsequent bounded deep review. No new discovery or deep research was started.
