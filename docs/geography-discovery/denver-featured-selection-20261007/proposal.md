# Denver: four proposed featured research opportunities

Proposed order: **Hunger Free Colorado; Doctors Care; Rebuilding Together Colorado; Cultivando.** Exactly four from all ten accepted beta reviews. Order emphasizes service/evidence readability, not an empirical expected-value ranking. Conditional prices are scope-dependent references, not comparable verified gift offers. No organization research minutes, report/model mutations or publication added by this comparison.

## Inclusions and donor boundary

1. **Hunger Free Colorado** (`hunger-free-colorado`) — $4,844,132.485651. Matched annual recipient cost/application envelope, direct navigation experiment and a plausible food-access mechanism outweigh its low measured health component. Signed resources +10.353537 explicitly include full worldwide taxpayer losses corresponding to Denver transfers; health .031150. Approvals, sustained receipt, partner share and added donor capacity are judged/unobserved. Existing accepted donor receipt: own EveryAction form returned200, no transaction. Blurb: “Helps households navigate food benefits and food resources through a hotline and community partners.”
2. **Doctors Care** (`doctors-care`) — $7,846,907.505890. Matched FY2025 clinic clients/cost, concrete local care and dated patient-fee/financial-protection evidence support an inspectable giving opportunity. Clinical utility remains judged, and signed net resources are NEGATIVE (−3.218329) despite medical savings; clinical-null total is nonpositive. Medicaid/FQHC/insured providers are real alternatives. Existing accepted own ColoradoGives embedded route returned200, not a gift receipt. Blurb: “Provides primary and behavioral health care and insurance navigation for insured and uninsured patients.”
3. **Rebuilding Together Colorado** (`rebuilding-together-colorado`) — $5,863,232.647516. Seventy-two delivered homeowner cases and concrete repair/accessibility mechanisms are more directly inspectable than a hypothetical policy population. Controlled modifications evidence supports injury mechanisms, not the generic .02 utility/three-year coefficient. Public programs and Brothers Redevelopment/CVNA CAPABLE are actual alternatives; county leakage, volunteer resource costs and net otherwise-paid spending remain material. Existing own FundraiseUp trigger verified, final receipt and marginal repair capacity not tested. Blurb: “Makes critical safety and accessibility repairs for low-income homeowners.”
4. **Cultivando** (`cultivando`) — $3,599,241.420516. Current original April2026/June-effective Regulation30 contains new emission controls, so the pathway is not merely monitoring or aspirational benchmarks. This is a plausible community-led implementation opportunity, NOT an observed10,000-person causal cohort. Conditional implementation .3/recipient attribution .15, toxic-pollutant utility and net resources, compliance/job losses and timing remain uncertain. Full policy-failure advocate costs and external losses are modeled. Existing recipient donation/hostedPayPal identity checked in accepted initial evidence, no transaction or added capacity measured. Blurb: “Organizes Latino community leadership and environmental-health advocacy in North Denver and Commerce City.”

## All six exclusions and comparative alternatives

- **HRAC**, $363,613.411929: strongest direct mortality mechanism and lowest conditional price, but exact official checkout returned404 and acceptance explicitly says donor HOLD. The rounded4,000 service proxy is not matched unique opioid-risk recipients. Excluded for current giving-route readiness and denominator/alternative-supply uncertainty, not because naloxone is unimportant. First reserve if exact recipient intake and capacity are established; domain login is insufficient.
- **KIND**, $11,407,369.219479: original school/clinic counts and sealant/restorative mechanisms make a serious direct-care alternative to Cultivando, not rejected simply for higher price. However acceptance also explicitly retains donor HOLD, and school-tooth equivalents, school/clinic overlap, expense discrepancy and utility mapping remain material. Do not quietly promote an unverified checkout because the evidence is otherwise promising. Reserve once receipt routing/capacity and key cohort scope are resolved.
- **Energy Outreach Colorado**, $21,027,194.748502: strong economic-protection/thermal service candidate and credible alternative to RTC/HFC. Main numerator is judged bill-cost overhead allocation, beneficiary bill share is a proxy from all-program households, and donor-budget tariff incidence convention differs from HFC's full payer ledger. Actual otherwise-paid bill displacement is decision-changing: zero displacement yields nonpositive welfare; whole-cost diagnostic is ~$70.143M. Prefer HFC's inspectable applications plus direct navigation evidence and RTC's delivered repair envelope, without claiming EOC has inferior true portfolio EV.
- **Immunize Colorado**, $31,609,745.738710: matched440supported patients, vaccine prevention and county partnership are credible. But mixed vaccine/age protective dose, actual added uptake beyond publicVFC/other providers and partner delivery resources are missing; infant seasonal trial/lifetime birth-cohort economics do not calibrate this mostly school-age catch-up cohort. Whole coalition cost against one channel adds scope uncertainty. Reserve rather than infer low value from this partial price.
- **Thriving Families**, $72,634,544.725645: direct local MotherWise randomized evaluation is valuable, but depression/material-hardship null findings challenge clinical/earnings inference; distinct LaLuz uses uncertain prenatal-to-postpartum ROSE ITT transport and no current completed subgroup. Negative resources centrally and full cost/subset boundary further limit confidence. Relationship/counseling/parenting benefits remain unknown, not zero; exclusion is lack of decision-ready quantitative/donor pathway, not a statement those services fail.
- **PPRM**, $95,173,948.604977: substantive reproductive access and other clinical benefits are plausible. Strong four-year existing-household consumption-pressure judgment is not measured persistent income, Denver-resident share is unknown, current region/funding differs, and whole regional cost covers unquantified other care. Prefer more inspectable local cohorts now; do not interpret this subset price as proof reproductive care is less valuable. Zero/negative pressure scenarios remain important.

## Why Cultivando over KIND/EOC, and why this is not price sorting

Cultivando's price is not the deciding fact. Its legal-control implementation pathway is independently traceable in a current original rule, and its accepted recipient donation identity is not on the explicit checkout HOLDs affecting HRAC/KIND. RTC and Doctors expose direct delivery while Cultivando adds a plausible environmental opportunity with source-backed legal progress; this is not a category quota. If credible near-term funding use/implementation cannot be established, KIND after donor-HOLD resolution or EOC after bill-cohort/payment-incidence verification can replace it. None of these choices establishes measured marginal returns.
