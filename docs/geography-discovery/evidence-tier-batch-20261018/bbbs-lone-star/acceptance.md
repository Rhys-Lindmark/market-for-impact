# Big Brothers Big Sisters Lone Star (Houston, in-depth): source and model audit

Auditor: claude-opus-houston-beta-audit (Claude Opus 5.5), in a separately timed source-audit interval. **This is the same AI model as the author, not an independent second agent.**

## Source checks

- Eight trial sites including Houston; 1,138 consenting families; 883 linked to tax records; wages of $16,500 (men) and $14,000 (women); $2,778 per active match; imprecise RCT earnings effects but significantly higher college attendance: Bell and Petkova 2023 conference draft (text extracted).
- 19% earnings at ages 20–25 in the observational comparison: Afterschool Alliance and BBBSA summaries of Bell's 2025 paper.
- $56,000 lifetime gain and under $3,000 per participant in six of eight cities: Policy Impacts library entry.
- 5,110 children served in 2024: GuideStar. Houston as a core market and the 2026 Austin merger: agency news and the Simmons and Fletcher profile.
- CY2021–23 expenses: ProPublica API.

Corrected in audit: an unsourced age range and drug-initiation claims (replaced with the sourced absenteeism and suspension findings), and an unsourced waitlist claim (removed).

## Model checks

- 8% × $20,000 × 0.6 net, from age 20 for 25 years (delay 8 from age 12); causal factors 0.5 × 0.6 × 0.9; Houston 0.8. Pass.
- Central about $60K per DALY. The range runs from $13K (19% fully causal) to $159K (3%).

## Clarifications

The earnings effect relies on an observational design; the RCT itself was underpowered for earnings. Volunteer supply, not money alone, may limit matches.

## Decision

Accepted as a Houston in-depth (beta) report.
