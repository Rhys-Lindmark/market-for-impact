# Income materiality screen — USA, NYC and LA

Provisional desk screen of all45 existing initial reports. This is prioritization, not fresh source verification or a claim that any report is empirically90% health. No prices or published completion counts changed. Targeted research26; conditional checks10; health-first9. Actual one-minute focused checks belong inside the existing five-minute initial budget, not on top; fifteen minutes is deep-only. No per-report research minutes were invented for this cross-report planning screen.

A robust health-dominant decision requires comparable income-effect bounds; for positive welfare, income below health/9 implies at least90% health. Check material signed losses separately. Never add arbitrary household savings/costs to tick an income box. Preserve unknowns and overlap, and promote unresolved material cases instead of treating absence of data as zero.
