## Three-sentence what

Help America Hear coordinates donated prescription hearing aids and local professional fitting for financially constrained people nationwide. Its current standard program offers two aids, molds and one year of limited service, while applicants bear some fitting, evaluation, travel and maintenance costs. A separate scholarship program supports students with cash and hearing/assistive devices; those benefits are unpriced in this partial hearing-access forecast, not absent.
