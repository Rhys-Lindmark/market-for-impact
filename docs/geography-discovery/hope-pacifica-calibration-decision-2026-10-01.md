## Health causal bridge

[Walley BMJ 2013](https://www.bmj.com/content/346/bmj.f174), abstract freshly retrieved through the [primary bibliographic record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:23372174%20AND%20SRC:MED&format=json&resultType=core), found observational community mortality rate ratios .73 and .54 for lower/higher OEND implementation versus none, Massachusetts 2002–2009. Acute-care utilization contrasts were nonsignificant. This supports mortality plausibility, not a modern Bay effect per pack or a monetary healthcare-saving coefficient.

[HEALing Communities randomized mortality trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2401177), freshly checked, found RR .91 (95% CI .76–1.09, p=.30), multicomponent intervention across 67 communities. [Primary randomized distribution analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11628717/) found 79% higher naloxone distribution, RR1.79 (1.28–2.51), without significant retail-pharmacy dispensing change. More distribution is not automatically more identified mortality reduction; the trial does not isolate naloxone pharmacology or establish HOPE ineffectiveness. Its interval does not identify subjective scenario weights.

[Larochelle cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29913516/) and [Olfson cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29926090/), abstracts freshly retrieved through Europe PMC after PubMed challenges, report first-year all-cause mortality of 4.7 and 7.783 per 100 person-years after nonfatal overdose. These are selected survivors in historical settings, not HOPE's prospective network or its five-/ten-year prognosis. Central baseline/post-support .07 hazards remain unmeasured transfer priors.

Plausible channel: added ready coverage → timely naloxone not otherwise available → reduced first-year net death hazard → one carried-forward survival gap. Keep the original analytic integral, 3% discount, one-year active period, common subsequent mortality and no repeated life restart. No additional rescue probability should multiply a hazard difference already incorporating use/timing/alternatives. Brain injury, addiction treatment engagement and family grief support remain plausible but unquantified; no generic positive utility add-on is supplied.

It reduces extrapolation, rather than assuming the first-year survival gain disappears immediately. These two changes address distinct uncertainties: gift additionality and conditional subsequent survival.

## Net-income incidence: zero central, signed cases

Do **not** add baseline lifetime earnings just because someone survives: that is not a distinct conditional-alive income effect and duplicates the survival valuation. No native wage effect, conditional-alive employment gain, causal caregiver time release or net public/household medical saving was located. Successful rescue may increase emergency utilization rather than save it; Walley's nonsignificant utilization contrast cannot be monetized. Native family support is not demonstrated household income improvement.

A separate cash channel exists for people who would otherwise buy an equivalent pack: avoided $19 retail spending. Someone who would take another free pack or forego purchase has zero avoided purchase expenditure. Receiving free state medication does not itself create household cash saving, and retail valuation is not net societal resource saving. Transaction/travel burden can make the net household channel negative. Donor cost stays in the numerator without a second invented donor-income deduction.

**Central net income = 0**, a provisional unidentified-sign judgment, not an assertion exact cancellation or absence of economic value. Independent positive stress: ready unique-holder equivalents `M = gift/expense × packs×ready/repeats×funding = 1.5625`; purchaser share .10; one $19 avoided payment per purchaser; consumption reference $50,000; Bay .95; no incremental transaction burden in this case. Income-equivalent years = `.5 × M × .95 × .10 × log1p(19/50000)` = **.000028197768**. The .5 is the published income-to-health crosswalk, not a measured local preference; $50k is its reference, not reported recipient income. Purchase .10 is purely a stress prior, **not externally estimated**. Only the retail amount is freshly price-anchored.

Disjointness caveat: `.8` risk/network is a joint overlap/risk conversion, **not an observed unmet-access share**. Its residual `.2` must not automatically be labeled actual purchasers. For this diagnostic explicitly assign .10 of holder equivalents to otherwise-purchasers **outside the health-credited cohort**, with zero additional health credit for them; numerically .10+.8≤1, but cohort mapping is hypothetical. If purchasers are instead within the health-credited group, recompute/withdraw their additional health rather than layering savings onto protection they already would buy. This is a proposed additional partition, not a fact discovered in the old model. No multiplier from lives saved to household income is used.
