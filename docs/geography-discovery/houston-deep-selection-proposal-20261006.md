# Houston deep-review selection proposal

Proposal only, not accepted beta research or recommendations. Based on the 24 accepted initial reports and pending AVDA packet on October6. Confirm final AVDA acceptance and challenge this comparative choice at the next bounded turn. Do not select the four featured organizations yet. Initial prices are conditional partial-channel references with unlike cost/output boundaries, not calibrated full-portfolio or next-dollar expected values.

| Proposed review | Main question that could change the estimate |
| --- | --- |
| AVDA | Can deduplicated recent legal/counseling outcomes identify incremental safety and net household gains after alternative aid, court burdens and transfer incidence? |
| The Lighthouse of Houston | Do training/low-vision outcomes justify net earnings and utility gains after tuition, time, alternatives and related-entity costs? |
| Crisis Intervention of Houston | How much unique distress improvement survives 988 substitution, repeat contacts and full volunteer costs? |
| Meals on Wheels Montgomery County | Can unique homebound recipients and completed deliveries support nutrition/independence effects beyond displaced food spending and caregiver costs? |
| Christ Clinic | What additional completed clinical treatment and net medical spending follow from donations versus public/charity alternatives and donated clinician resources? |
| Air Alliance Houston | Which implemented exposure changes are attributable to monitoring/permit work, with delay, compliance costs and signed job effects? |
| Bayou City Waterkeeper | What incremental sewage/flood-risk reduction exceeds already-required government action, after implementation and household/provider costs? |
| The Rose | Does navigation change timely diagnosis/treatment rather than just interactions, including outside treatment costs, screening harms and household resources? |
| Healthcare for the Homeless - Houston | Can completed symptomatic care and staffing capacity support a stronger clinical/resource bridge than visits or screening counts? |
| TOMAGWA HealthCare Ministries | Can defined completed treatment cohorts and recognized/donated costs support incremental relief and net medical resources in the local catchment? |

These ten balance decision relevance, evidence that a deeper pass could calibrate a causal mechanism, and potential incremental donor capacity. They include legal safety, work/accessibility, mental health, nutrition, clinical care and environmental policy; these are not category quotas. Health and signed income are assessed in every review. Low initial point estimates alone do not justify selection or final promotion.

Closest alternates: Easter Seals has a plausible child/caregiver pathway but historical cohort and public-insurance substitution; Lone Star has stronger matched accounting/activity but broader geography and overlapping legal categories; both remain valid replacements if the independent comparative challenge identifies higher expected learning/value. Santa Maria's funding cuts/closure merit scrutiny of restart capacity, not automatic exclusion because of its high partial-channel price. HAWC/Fort Bend/Daya have safety/resource mechanisms but overlapping populations and large unmatched scope; compare AVDA findings before declaring them inferior. Vecino/AccessHealth/San Jose offer additional clinical routes, but whole-recipient cost, insurance and old/current output mismatches need particular care. Repair charities, Brighter Bites, Avenue360 and Allies in Hope retain plausible wider benefits beyond the narrow initial channel; no claim their high partial-channel prices prove poor portfolio EV.

At acceptance, save the ten canonical IDs and review order in a durable selection artifact. Preserve all 25 initial diagnostics; each deep report explains source-driven changes or unchanged parameters, not a forced numeric revision. Record actual ~15-minute substantive intervals and independent review separately. No new count is credited by this proposal.
