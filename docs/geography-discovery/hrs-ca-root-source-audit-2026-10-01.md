The live original [HRS SSP page](https://hrssac.org/about-us/services-programs/) describes free confidential counter/mobile services in Sacramento, Narcan distribution and treatment navigation. It explicitly says Sacramento County does not fund the SSP or its drug-use supplies. General county support must not be treated as replacement funding for this mechanism without mechanism-specific evidence. Navigation is not observed additional treatment completion or household earnings.

The [HEALING primary trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2401177) reports an adjusted opioid mortality rate ratio .91, confidence interval .76–1.09, for a bundled community intervention over twelve months. It does not identify HRS's individual incremental naloxone effect, and does not justify assigning every kit a prevented death. Preserve zero/adverse effect scenarios and competing mortality.

The overdose-program search index mentions nasal Narcan out of stock, but the direct page returned HTTP429 during this audit. Treat present stock status as unverified; do not turn an indexed undated statement into a verified current capacity constraint.
