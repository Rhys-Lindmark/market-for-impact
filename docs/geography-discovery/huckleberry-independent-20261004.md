## Required causal correction

The proposal defines additionality as genuinely additional offered courses after displaced care/funding. That is causal exposure. Its multiplier must apply to every signed effect caused by the additional course, including travel burdens and negative clinical outcomes. Positive-only overlap/independence credit is a separate judgment and must never discount negative rows. The current implementation conflates these two ideas: it scales only positive health by additionality while assigning course burdens even when no course is caused. At additionality=0 and no independent donation-caused harm, both course health and household effects must equal zero. A finite positive price remains null because impact is zero; a negative impact is not implied by zero exposure.

Required equations: health = additionality × editionShare × (trialQalys > 0 ? trialQalys × positiveTransferRetention : trialQalys) / delayDiscount. For each exposed household, first credit positive cash rows and retain full negative rows, sum once, calculate the household logarithm, then multiply the resulting signed equivalent by additionality × editionShare. Multiplying additionality inside cash before logarithm falsely spreads a smaller burden across all households instead of modeling a fraction exposed to full course burden. If additionality is not a homogeneous exposure fraction, use separately specified counterfactual branches. Do not invent their probabilities.

Independent gift-caused losses (for example, a separately evidenced household administrative cost incurred whether or not a course is additional) belong in a separate ledger with their own causal exposure, not in course travel. Define the unit as one nominal donor-funded course budget versus what happens without that budget. The numerator stays $2,400 spent per nominal course, not $1,200, while half the nominal courses are assumed genuinely additional. A cost per verified incremental offered course would require a different numerator/estimand.
