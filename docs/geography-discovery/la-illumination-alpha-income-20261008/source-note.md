# Illumination Health + Home initial resource assessment

Fresh original sources: FY2024–25 impact report https://illuminationhealthhome.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Illumination_2024-25_Impact-Report.pdf and healthcare services https://illuminationhealthhome.org/service/healthcare-services/, retrieved October 8, 2026 UTC. Impact PDF printed page 4 reports 2,329 recuperative-care clients, 1,719 housed and 6,826 overall clients; these potentially overlapping person counts are not unique ratepaying households or measured private-payment savings. Printed page 5 describes one client's SNAP/general-relief access and pending Social Security benefits, establishing a mechanism but no award amount or annual beneficiary cohort. Family housing, navigation and employment assistance are further resource pathways.

Decision: targeted assessed-unknown resource incidence, retaining the valid partial clinical reference. No operational household dollar anchor links this respite cohort to additional retained benefits, avoided private payments or net rent/food savings. Hospital/public savings and retail service value are not client income. Future net resources may be positive or negative after actual rent, benefits withdrawn, medical payments, access/time costs and alternative CalAIM/hospital/provider funding. Full incurred burdens must not be discounted by clinical response. No absent-data zero or 90% health conclusion is made. The matched current household payment/cohort bridge, not absence of an RCT, is the unresolved step.
