## Controlled evidence versus selection

[2019 randomized crossover](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6420590/): improved diet, food insecurity, reported hypoglycemia and secondary mental-health-days; short24-week crossover without washout. No clinical utility mapping or persistent QALY estimate is identified. It supports plausibility, not.01GLWDutility calibration.

[Maryland pilot randomized trial](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11606-024-09248-x):74 randomized, three-month12-meal+produce dose and six-month dietitian calls. Clinical/diabetesQoL and costs did not differ; food security improved. Delivered86% differs from consumption: only23% of26 respondents reported eating all/most meals at visits2/3. Main complete-case sample57; eligibility/exclusions differ from NYC mixed severe disease. Delivery is not consumption, and conditional70%use is judgment rather than trial estimate.

[2019 instrumental-variable cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6547148/) found lower claims utilization/cost using distance instrument and matching. Instrument exclusion, unmeasured selection and transported provider/population constrain causal interpretation. Claimed insurer savings are neither charity expense reductions nor client disposable income.

[2026 Medicaid cohort](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04407-5) uses propensity-weighted difference-in-differences; receipt is not randomized. Random comparator start dates are not treatment allocation. Primary>90-day enrollment selects persistent recipients; broader secondary cohort partially addresses that concern. Authors acknowledge selection bias, bundled counseling pathways and unavailable post-service outcomes. No individual GLWD donor effect or QALY conversion follows.

[FAME-D original conference publisher](https://diabetesjournals.org/diabetes/article/75/Supplement_1/1249-OR/167139/1249-OR-Medically-Tailored-Meals-Lower-Hemoglobin): newer randomized abstract was discovered; original-page full retrieval403. No clinical coefficient or numerical result imported from secondary reproductions. Its existence means the pilot null is not described as the entire current randomized literature.

## Welfare crosswalk and boundaries

[Coefficient Giving methodology](https://coefficientgiving.org/research/cost-effectiveness/) uses50,000referenceincome, log proportional income welfare, and100,000CG per healthy year. Local shared calculator supplies exact discount and signed-gain arithmetic. It is a normative comparison and does not turn consumption into clinical QALYs/DALYs. No CG endorsement or grantmaking-bar conclusion is implied.

Counterfactual providers, SNAP/in-kind food, purchased food, caregivers and insurers remain. Different clinical nutrition, consumption completion, donor capacity and independent welfare overlap steps are explicit. Gross meal retail value, capital fundraising demand, payer savings and household income are never conflated. Missing costs, benefits/taxes and displacement are uncertain; conditional reference values do not establish measured zeros.
