# Transportation Alternatives alpha correction

Decision: **withdraw the $71.4 million per ten New York MSA QALYs as a current central estimate; retain it only as a historical conditional diagnostic.** The organization remains active and has a plausible street-safety mechanism. This correction is neither a finding of zero benefit nor a completed in-depth review. Donor readiness for a quantified unrestricted gift remains HOLD; NYC stays 25/25 initial and 3/10 deep.

An author source-gate check and an independent compact audit both reproduced all six old scenario outputs exactly. The problem is identification, not arithmetic. The original model assigns four additional corridor equivalents, a 25% incremental coalition effect, a 30% Transportation Alternatives share and a 50% donation response without source calibration. The 31st Street's 26 killed-or-seriously-injured people over five years is a selected corridor, not a representative campaign portfolio. The [NYC DOT treatment evaluation](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/downloads/pdf/safety-treatment-evaluation-2005-2018.pdf) reports an 18.1% before/after change across 146 protected-bike-lane treatments and 36.7 centerline miles; it does not isolate this recipient or the effect of another gift. The [April 2026 31st Street announcement](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/pr2026/nyc-dot-redesign-of-31st-st-in-astoria-queens.shtml) describes a proposal and multiple advocates, not an additional implemented project purchased by a donation.

Three original March-ending [returns for FY2023](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202420609349301452/IRS990), [FY2024](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202540159349301174/IRS990), and [FY2025](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202543449349300149/IRS990), with their Schedule D reconciliations and the [FY2025 audit](https://transalt.org/s/FY25-Transportation-Alternatives-Inc-Financial-Statement-FINAL-1.pdf), support recognized gross expenses of $4,447,542, $5,135,283 and $5,186,682. The current IRS status and recipient donation route were checked.
