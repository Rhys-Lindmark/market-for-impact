## Identity, reach, delivery

Official [giving](https://www.cbwchc.org/give) instructs checks payable to Charles B. Wang Community Health Center, 268 Canal Street; the recipient is the operating center, not Chinatown Health Clinic Foundation. Current [internal medicine](https://www.cbwchc.org/service/internal-medicine) provides chronic disease management, preventive care, immunization, cancer screening, hepatitis B care and specialty referrals; staff speak 12+ languages and provide interpretation. [Mental health](https://www.cbwchc.org/service/mental-health) integrates psychiatry/therapy but requires an existing primary-care patient and referral. These describe service mechanisms, not extra slots per donation or causal outcomes. Clinical locations in Manhattan/Queens do not establish beneficiary residence; g=.95 remains a judgment with .8–1 bounds. General health education can reach outside the MSA; it receives no separate modeled benefit absent residence/outcome data.

## Clinical and consumption interpretation

[Oregon randomized Medicaid study](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMsa1212321),2013: insurance expanded service use, reduced depression and financial strain, but had no significant effect on measured blood pressure, cholesterol or glycated hemoglobin at two years. Its insurance intervention/population does not estimate CBWCHC's clinical utility, donation elasticity, or savings. [IMPACT trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/195599),2002:1,801 depressed older adults across 18 US clinics,12-month response 45% versus 19%; quality-of-life advantage 0.56 on 0–10 scale. This is not a QALY scale, not this provider's treatment protocol, and cannot be assigned to every patient. It supports a possible mental-health mechanism, with utility/duration left as judgments.

[Operator hepatitis-B program](https://www.cbwchc.org/community-health/hepatitis-b-program) provides screening, vaccination, treatment and coordination. [Liaw randomized antiviral trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15470215/),2004,651 advanced-fibrosis/cirrhosis patients, median 32.4 months: progression 7.8% versus 17.7%; resistance developed in 49% of treated participants. Selected severe disease, old drug and composite endpoint prevent importing this as deaths avoided across 70,000 annual patients. Current incremental eligible starts, completion, drug/care costs, baseline progression and alternative treatment are missing; survival central credit remains zero.

[Current insurance/payment page](https://www.cbwchc.org/health-insurance) offers enrollment help, sliding discounts and partner-pharmacy discounts; examples state annual physical as low as $20 for eligible patients. A discount versus posted charges is not measured household saving. More treatment can increase medication, copay, specialist and travel costs. Any employment benefit must be net disposable pay after taxes, benefits withdrawn and work costs among people alive in both worlds; gross wages and ordinary earnings of additional survivors excluded. No measured CBWCHC household balance exists, so central signed consumption is zero as an unresolved neutral placeholder, with explicit positive/negative/break-even/unknown tests. This is not evidence of no financial effect.

## Remaining decisive uncertainty

Request gift-specific staffing/capacity plan, actual net public reimbursement displacement, patient ZIP/deduplication, completed treatment by diagnosis, controlled utility trajectories/adverse outcomes, survival baseline/finite timing, common-alive disposable income and net medical/transport costs. Annual deficit, awards, testimonials and visits cannot replace these. Positive clinical, residual wellbeing and material welfare must be kept distinct; no extra cash value for the same purchased/free medical care already represented in health.
