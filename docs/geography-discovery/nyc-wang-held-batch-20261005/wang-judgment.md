# Clinical and household judgment review

The positive reference remains conditional; it is not a posterior expected marginal gift effect. The initial alpha model and exact outputs are retained in the diagnostic JSON and historical namespace. The new primary source review does not identify a defensible replacement numerical center for the clinical coefficients, so the unchanged central price does not signify unchanged understanding or increased certainty.

- Effective care share .20: 8% uninsured is not an upper bound because insured people can face linguistic/continuity barriers; 70%+ non-English language need is not an additional-care share. There is no observed comparison with alternative clinics or specialists. The .20 value remains a testable prior. Alternative-equivalent care or additionality zero are material possibilities.
- Utility .02: Oregon randomization challenges assuming physical utility from access alone; IMPACT supports depression treatment among selected older adults, but supplies neither a provider-wide QALY nor an all-age average. Mental-health entry requires an existing primary-care patient. No measured depression treatment count, remission contrast or age/severity mix identifies the portfolio mean. Dental symptom relief, acute illness and chronic management are plausible pathways contained within this one clinical term, never added again.
- Half-year duration: unique annual patients do not identify full treatment completion or sustained improvement. Symptom relief can be shorter; prevention can have delayed benefits. The model truncates every clinical/residual/harm stream at its specified window, tests shorter exposure and delayed discounted benefits, and does not multiply visits into repeated outcomes.
- Gift factor .30: patient-service/public funding dominance, fee rules, reserves and alternative donations provide strong reasons to model substitution, but no elasticity estimate. The latest deficit does not prove a binding capacity margin. Public reimbursement may complement or displace gifts; neither is assigned a measured ratio. A restricted incremental staffing plan is needed to replace this average allocation with actual marginal gift production.
- Harm/survival: the selected [SPRINT trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1511939) demonstrates cardiovascular benefit alongside more hypotension, syncope, electrolyte abnormalities and kidney injury. It supports a reason to test net harm, not a CBWCHC adverse rate or universal mortality credit. Hepatitis-B progression trials likewise cannot identify donor-caused current starts. Central survival and harm zeros are unresolved placeholders. The finite survival sensitivity excludes those hypothetical survivors from clinical/common-alive residual utility and ordinary earnings, with no benefit beyond five years.
- Household welfare: fees, insurance help and pharmacy access make cash relief possible; increased medication/referral/travel exposure can create burdens. These can occur even when alternative providers deliver clinically equivalent care, so resourceShare is independent of clinical effective-care share. The .20 resource share, .5 households per patient, $25,000 annual household consumption and half-year exposure are scenario judgments. They prevent claims that every patient represents a separate household, but are not measured deduplication or baseline spending. Positive gains are limited to a .5 independent share; negative burdens count in full using sequential signed logarithmic changes. At share1 the two changes exactly recover the joint log of net resources. Gross wages, survivor ordinary earnings, insurer/public-budget savings and charges forgiven without actual household spending avoided receive no credit.

Central financial0 means unresolved neutral reference, not no financial effect. The explicit unknown-financial case has no combined price. The financial-only case provides positive conditional welfare without invented clinical benefit. The low-income/high-household-exposure case gives negative combined welfare under a $500 annual household burden; it is a stress test, not a measured loss or a confidence bound.

The final executable packet has 20 scenarios and assertions for signed log identity, full negative cost at zero positive overlap, financial relief without clinical increment, zero/null/adverse outputs, finite delay, original-model preservation and engine/report agreement. No promised donor slots or controlled provider clinical effect has been invented.
