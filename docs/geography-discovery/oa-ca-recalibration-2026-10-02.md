## Household resources and overlap

Use one first-year net resource amount per household-equivalent, with no later tail: .5 × households × log1p(net first-year resources / $30,000 annual household consumption), discounted at delivery delay + .5 years. This crosswalk is not actual household-income measurement. A cash event is consumed over the first year only, not perpetually, and is combined with that year's savings before taking the log.
