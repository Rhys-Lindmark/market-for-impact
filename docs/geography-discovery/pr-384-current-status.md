# P0: recalibrate existing in-depth reviews with health and income

Highest research priority, requested by Rhys. This PR now contains implementation and publication evidence, not just the original planning checklist.

## Verified status — October 3, 2026 UTC

- Published cohort: **40/40** reviewed, including the three prior USA policy revisions. These are conditional estimates, not verified marginal donation offers.
- Confirmed additional legacy models: **2/11** published (Pacific Hearing Connection; Clinic by the Bay). The original remaining-work queue is **39/48**: 37 subsequent cohort reviews plus two legacy models. The different denominators refer to different inventories, not eight invented reviews.
- Three further depth/provenance gates remain unresolved: legacy Vision To Learn, YMCA Greater SF portfolio, and New Door Ventures portfolio. Self-reported metadata is not sufficient evidence of independent acceptance.
- Operation Access's legacy US/Bay/SF portfolio is currently being re-examined. Its completed California review is a distinct scope, not automatic acceptance of the legacy model.

Latest publication evidence: Clinic by the Bay report/model Sites375, progress Sites376. Its conditional Bay cost is $17.49M per ten combined health/resource equivalents, versus the separate historical weighted $2.00M and unweighted clinical $7.98M. All 34 historical scenarios remain preserved; 39 current cases, independent clinical quadrature, signed household-resource reconstruction, focused tests and phone/tablet checks support the published integration. Actual dedicated sessions are recorded separately from deterministic QA, integration, deployment and waits.

## Required acceptance for every model

Explicit health and income/resource pathways; recipient incidence and actual net costs; transfers, alternatives, displacement and signed harms; coherent complete-gift cost boundary and separate external resources; sourced coefficients distinguished from judgment priors; no duplicate timing/discount/overlap; old-to-new explanation; unknown distinct from zero; zero/adverse sensitivities; independent review and reproducible arithmetic; report/API/list/shortlist price parity. Retaining a price is not independent validation of its original benefit envelope, and recalibration need not make an estimate better or worse.

The full geographic expansion remains active: eleven editions, each 100 researched discovery candidates, 25 initial reports, ten in-depth reviews and four source-vetted shortlist entries, preserving SF. Finishing this P0 queue is not completion of that goal. Geographic report/depth counts are not increased by same-report recalibrations.

Current continuity is the existing hourly automation, not a duplicate. Latest user reserve is **20% remaining**, with an operational **25% no-new-batch guard** and checks before dispatch/every 15 active minutes. Respect later pauses; do not consume resets automatically. Current model assignment is GPT-6.1 Sol; historical recorded identities are preserved. One helper at a time, root-only source integration/Git/Sites, reused checkout/dependencies.
