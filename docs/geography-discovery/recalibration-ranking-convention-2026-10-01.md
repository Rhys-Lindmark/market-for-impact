# Ranking statistic for the recalibration pass

Manager decision, October 1. The existing edition contract and `lib/geography-reports.mjs` select the `central` scenario for the comparison table. Keep that convention for recalibrated reports: **a declared central judgment case**, not a claim of empirically identified expected value or a confidence-bound estimate.

This avoids choosing a favorable statistic after seeing its rank. It also avoids introducing arbitrary probability weights into reports that contain only unweighted stress cases. Probability/attribution inputs inside a central model remain explicit; a scenario mixture must not duplicate those same uncertainties by adding another generic success haircut.

## Acceptance requirements

1. Define cost scope and the same health plus net-income welfare denominator before evaluating rank. Main price is `10 × cost / (local health years + local income-equivalent years)`. Preserve signed harm and economic losses. Nonpositive total benefit has no positive price.
2. State whether each input is observed, externally transferred or a judgment. A central case is not a median or expectation merely because its identifier says central. Policy models containing probability-weighted outcomes must disclose those probabilities and what is already integrated out.
3. Where a model already has a complete signed weighted mixture, retain it alongside the central result and show tail/weight sensitivity. State that weights are judgments unless independently calibrated. Compute its ratio from signed expected benefits and costs, never by averaging positive ratios or omitting null/harm cases.
4. For legacy reports ranked by a weighted result, do not silently relabel that result central. At their own acceptance checkpoint reconstruct both, document any change of statistic and source/parameter changes separately, and synchronize report, index, shortlist and API. No global migration of unreviewed models in this checkpoint.
5. Do not put annual-work accounting efficiency into a marginal-gift ranking without an explicit scope change. Funding room is a separate source-vetting question, not a discount arbitrarily applied to full annual benefits.

## ReCARES application

The current public central marginal health case is $74,720, while its original accepted weighted FY2025 result is $107,774. The earlier audit requested the weighted result; current integration selected central. This pass makes the selection explicit rather than pretending the integration obeyed that earlier instruction.

Use the accepted **central marginal** case for the common table after the substantive clinical/income challenge is resolved. Preserve its signed weighted result and adverse/zero/independent-income cases in the report/calculator. Keep annual-work results separate. Changing to a different clinical family, adding economic benefit, and changing the statistic are three different actions; the revision comparison must isolate them. This policy alone accepts no new ReCARES coefficients or replacement price.

The figures on these tables are model comparisons, not sufficient evidence for a ten-million-dollar donation. Native output, current operations, correct entity, financials, outcome evidence and additional funding capacity still determine recommendation strength. No extra endorsement is inferred from rank.
