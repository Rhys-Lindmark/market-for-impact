Affordability category is 79.10%; “none above” is .839%. | None-above is not a measured purchaser group. Device mix includes more high-value chairs than ReCARES' site model. |

For a positive transfer illustration, use **purchase share .01**, rounded from the latest national none-above share as a weak proxy; **$45 avoided personal expense once**, an illustrative 20% of the $225 daily-living device price gap; **$50,000 annual consumption/income baseline**, the published crosswalk reference rather than a claimed local income. [Medicare's primary DME coverage rules](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/durable-medical-equipment-dme-coverage) supply the 20% coinsurance mechanism, but the national price gap is not a Medicare-approved amount, some devices are not covered, and supplemental coverage may eliminate personal payment. Thus all three transfers must be labeled judgments; this is a much narrower and externally interpretable bridge than the original 30% / $50 / $20,000 guess, not newly measured ReCARES savings.

Cash savings require an equivalent purchase that would actually occur without reuse. Free equipment for a person who would forego acquisition may create health/access but **no avoided purchase spending**. Purchasers are disjoint from otherwise-unmet health recipients; maintain `purchaseShare + weightedUnmetShare <= 1`. Timing-only access improvements, agencies/insurers, and replaced public benefits must use their own incidence ledger. Do not add baseline earnings because health is better, or monetize ordinary survival as earnings.

The cash bridge is `incomeEquivalentYears = .5 × N × BayShare × [p × log1p(savedOOP / baseline) − acquisitionLossTerm]`, where acquisition loss is entered by its exposed fraction and signed log change, and any counterparty welfare offset is separately disclosed. A payer-loss/transfer adjustment should apply to positive **welfare** credit with its own incidence/baseline, rather than silently classify revenue as a full social loss. Count only incremental pickup costs relative to the comparison purchase; retail acquisition may also require travel. Donor dollars stay in the numerator, without an unexplained second subtraction as a donor-income loss.

**Why recommend zero net income centrally:** the anchored small-purchaser case produces only about $0.45 of gross saving per unique recipient on average. The true purchaser fraction, payment exposure, pickup costs and counterparty incidence could readily overturn that amount. Neither local data nor the external reuse ledgers identifies its net sign. Use zero as a disclosed provisional net judgment, retain positive and negative component estimates, and name the unresolved wage/caregiver/environmental/public-budget channels as unquantified. This does not assert exact cancellation or zero economic benefit. It avoids representing an unidentified price-gap gain as local consumption while still testing the economic channel explicitly.
