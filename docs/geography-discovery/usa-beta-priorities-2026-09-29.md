# USA beta priorities — 29 September 2026

Decision basis: compare the 25 accepted USA initial reports for a large plausible domestic health effect, a tractable cost and native-outcome bridge, an identifiable recipient, and a useful next diligence question. The alpha dollar figures are **conditional research estimates**, not observed returns or recommendation order. A deeper review can worsen or withdraw a number. This ten-report cohort is a research plan, not a donor shortlist; alternatives can replace a member with a dated reason if source evidence changes.

| Organization | Status | Why a deeper review is high value | Decisive beta question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| End Overdose | Accepted beta | Scalable overdose-rescue pathway; direct health outcome. | Does unrestricted cash create additional nationally attributable rescue capacity? |
| Surgery on Sunday | Accepted beta | Observable completed operations and sizeable clinical health opportunity. | Can current treatment mix, donor-funded extra slots, and patient counterfactual be verified? |
| Dental Lifeline Network | Accepted beta | Measured donated-treatment reach and plausible pain/function health gains. | Are the clinical gains sustained, additional, and accessible through new funding? |
| Center for Science in the Public Interest | Accepted beta | National sodium-policy leverage offers scale unlike small direct-service cohorts. | What part of future implementation changes because of the next gift, after other advocates and regulators? |
| American Nonsmokers’ Rights Foundation | Accepted beta | Smoke-free policy has a direct exposure-to-cardiac-health mechanism. | Does recipient activity accelerate additional protection versus an active policy baseline? |
| Help America Hear | Next | Approx. $689K per ten alpha QALYs; direct fitted-hearing pathway and small recipient. | FY2025 narrative repeats “over 120” people; obtain a truly annual completed-fitting denominator, sustained use, and costed extra slots before trusting the price. Re-audit the original return's Schedule L related-party disclosure rather than inferring its significance from an aggregator flag. |
| Kids and Car Safety | Next | Approx. $920K per ten alpha QALYs; potentially national child-safety leverage and a small cost base. | Recheck 2026 NHTSA rear-seat-alert rule stage, current vehicle adoption, unique advocacy contribution, and whether funding can move timing. |
| Remote Area Medical | Next | Approx. $1.3M per ten alpha QALYs; direct clinics with countable visits. | Link visits to incremental completed care and durable health, separate donated resources, and resolve national beneficiary allocation. |
| Institute for Safer Trucking | Next | Approx. $1.6M per ten alpha QALYs; commercial-vehicle braking policy could change many road-user outcomes. | Recheck live regulatory status and transport of crash evidence; the subjective policy acceleration and recipient contribution dominate the alpha result. |
| Cribs for Kids | Next | Approx. $3.7M per ten alpha QALYs; infant safe-sleep mechanism with potentially serious mortality stakes. | Separate commercial crib distribution from additional charitable households and verify induced safe-sleep behavior and net mortality. |

Selection deliberately includes multiple policy/systemic mechanisms (CSPI, ANRF, Kids and Car Safety, Institute for Safer Trucking) alongside clinical, hearing, dental, overdose, and infant-safety paths. The remaining alpha cohort was not discarded. Headstrong, Rx Outreach, NHeLP, housing/environmental and other mechanisms can replace a priority if their updated evidence or funding margin becomes stronger. No top-four slot follows automatically from this table; current donor-ready count remains zero.

Source of the alpha prices, caveats, and current stages: `data/geography-reports.json`, `docs/geography-progress.json`, and each linked report's primary-source appendix. The 2026 [Unified Agenda search for RIN 2127-AM49](https://www.reginfo.gov/public/Forward?SearchTarget=Agenda&textfield=2127-AM49) lists the rear-designated-seating-position alert in the prerule stage, which is a live-status check for the Kids and Car Safety review, not proof of charity attribution or a final rule.
