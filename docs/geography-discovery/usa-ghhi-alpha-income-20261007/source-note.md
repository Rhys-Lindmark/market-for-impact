# GHHI targeted household-resource assessment
Author opened original Maryland direct-services and About Us pages on October 7, 2026.
https://www.greenandhealthyhomes.org/about-us/ reports an analysis-to-date Baltimore mean $306 energy-cost saving for clients in the first 12 months after intervention. It supplies no current marginal cohort, study date, counterfactual, unique resource-sharing unit or joint net-cost baseline. Its 20–25% integrated-delivery savings concern contractor/process efficiency, not automatically household cash. Cumulative workforce training and stated wage changes are not causal retained net income from the next gift.
https://www.greenandhealthyhomes.org/services/maryland-direct-services/ identifies mixed assessments, remediation, energy/weatherization, family advocacy and legal services, plus intake/referral. These do not make all completed interventions asthma child courses.
Decision: targeted assessed unknown. Preserve original clinical and cost reference/all six worlds. Do not assign $306 to every modeled asthma child, invent household costs, or count public/insurer grant savings as cash. A source-reported historical saving is evidence of materiality, not sufficient current signed-resource bridge. Need gift-responsive unique households, energy intervention mix, tenant/owner bill incidence and actual alternative/private payment baseline, rent/rate/maintenance changes, unmet utility demand/rebound, incremental unreimbursed disruption/time/access costs, taxes/benefits, duration and public/partner real costs. Positives alone receive overlap discount; all incurred negatives independent of clinical response. Broad workforce/resource portfolio unknown, not zero or 90%-health.

Root independently verified the July 2026 original affordability report, https://www.greenandhealthyhomes.org/wp-content/uploads/GHHI_Home-Electrification_Affordability_FINAL.pdf, printed pp31–32: four retired homeowners had both positive and negative actual and weather/rate-normalized annual bill changes. Grant-braided whole-house projects with no upfront client charge are not the modeled $8,000 child-asthma bundle. This supports signed uncertainty, not transferring those cases to the asthma cohort.
