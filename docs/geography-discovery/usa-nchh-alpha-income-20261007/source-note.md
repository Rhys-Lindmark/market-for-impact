# Targeted household-resource assessment — National Center for Healthy Housing

Author opened both original NCHH research pages on October 7, 2026.

- https://nchh.org/research/dc-green/ describes historical Wheeler Terrace rehabilitation: 44 enrolled households, 25 followed up about one year later, and reported energy/water cost reductions of 16%/54%. This supplies a plausible utility-cost mechanism, not bill levels, tenant-versus-landlord incidence, current unrestricted-gift delivery, or a conversion from the asthma child-course cohort to renovated households.
- https://nchh.org/research/nys-hnp-a-cost-benefit-analysis-of-a-state-funded-healthy-homes-program-for-residents-with-asthma/ describes a historical state-funded evaluation. Its asthma-event group had estimated medical encounters/medication savings of $1,083 per visit and program cost $302 over 12-month follow-up; the active-asthma group had $613 savings. These are utilization/program economic values, not measured household disposable-resource gains. Page last updated April 24, 2018.

Decision: assessed unknown, not zero or no-material-income. Preserve the original annual full recipient cost and conditional asthma-health model. Do not replace 52.5 attributed child-course-years with renovation households, or allocate historical medical savings to uninsured private payments without evidence. Missing data: unique resource-sharing households; actual baseline utility/private medical bills and payer mix; additional current delivery attributable to a gift versus publicly financed or equivalent alternatives; rent/rate changes, unreimbursed access/time/disruption costs; tax/benefit incidence, implementation cost scope, finite delay/duration, and joint net resources. Positive overlap applies only to gains; full incurred negatives must remain independent of clinical response. Lead/IQ earnings and gross salary are not an income bridge.

Resource discipline: reuse existing Node and shared validation modules; no installations, servers, or generated dependency copies. Filesystem had 23 GiB free at setup.
