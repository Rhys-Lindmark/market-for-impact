# Year Up United — USA in-depth review evidence notes (2026-10-18)

Author: claude-opus-usa-beta-author (Claude Opus 5.5). Organization: Year Up, Inc. (EIN 04-3534407), branded Year Up United since September 2024.

## Sources read

- Fein and Dastrup, *Benefits that Last: Long-Term Impact and Cost-Benefit Findings for Year Up*, Abt Associates, OPRE Report 2022-77 (2022). Read directly from the report PDF.
  - Randomized: 2,544 young adults (1,669 treatment, 875 control) at eight offices in nine metros (Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, New York, Providence, SF/San Jose, Seattle, Washington DC), enrolled 2013-14. Ages 18-24; 54% Black, 31% Hispanic.
  - Exhibit 2-1 (NDNH wage records), treatment / control / impact: Y1 $3,964 / $9,742 / -$5,778; Y2 $19,200 / $13,978 / +$5,222; Y3 $24,330 / $17,320 / +$7,011; Y4 $27,858 / $20,277 / +$7,581; Y5 $31,032 / $23,243 / +$7,789; Y6 $34,439 / $26,363 / +$8,076; Y7 $35,589 / $27,338 / +$8,251. Years 1-7 +$38,152.
  - Year 1 stipends averaged $7,142 per treatment member, more than offsetting the forgone earnings.
  - Six-year survey: personal income +$5,374 ($35,708 vs $30,333); household income +$3,164 (p=.076); public-benefit receipt 32.3% vs 39.3%; no effect on health insurance, self-assessed health, psycho-social wellbeing or longer-term credentials.
  - Cost: $28,280 average per participant; $27,158 per treatment member after 4% no-shows ($20,273 staffing/facilities plus $6,885 stipends). Net cost to society $23,135. Employers bore $16,118 and private philanthropy $9,960 of net cost per participant.
  - Participant perspective (Exhibit 5-4, 5% discount): earnings +$30,056; taxes -$8,324 combined; public benefits -$3,903; work costs -$391; stipend +$6,885; nonmarket time -$4,135; fringe benefits +$12,473; total $30,661.
  - Society: benefits $57,019, net $33,884, about $2.46 per dollar.
- Year Up PACE research page (yearup.org/research/pace): restates the 30% / "over $8,000" persistence through year 7 and the $2.46 return.
- Arnold Ventures summary of the Professional Training Corps (college-based) RCT: no significant earnings or college-enrollment effects in years 2-3. Abt calls these small early studies.
- ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API, EIN 04-3534407: total functional expenses CY2021 $140,469,714; CY2022 $162,709,291; CY2023 $194,225,512. CY2023 contributions $90.3M; program service revenue $93.5M (mainly corporate internship fees).
- Annual participants: secondary sources only (about 3,769 in the one-year program in a recent year; about 4,000 a year as of 2022). Treated as a judgment of 4,000; the Form 990 Part III count was not machine-readable here.

## Decisions

- Health: no QALYs credited, because the trial found no effect on self-assessed health or insurance. Income is converted with the shared log-income comparison; it is not added to a health valuation.
- Net welfare share 0.5 of gross earnings. After taxes, lost benefits and work costs, about 58% of the earnings gain remains; subtracting nonmarket time leaves about 44%. Fringe benefits are excluded centrally.
- Transfer 0.6: today's whole-organization budget funds both the evaluated core model and the college-based PTC model, which had an early null result. Displacement 0.85.
- Cost boundary: whole-organization CY2023 expense per judged participant ($48,556). This exceeds the inflation-adjusted PACE cost ($38,461), because the organization now runs more than the evaluated program.
