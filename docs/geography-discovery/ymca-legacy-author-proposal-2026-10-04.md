## Signed net household resources

Child clinical incidence and household resources are separate. Fee relief for someone who otherwise purchases the same service can create consumption gain with **zero new clinical access**, so resourceAdditionality is distinct from clinical additionality. It means causal net household change relative to no gift, not insurance/provider receipts. Alternative public coverage, other charities, lost transfers and service displacement must reduce that change.
