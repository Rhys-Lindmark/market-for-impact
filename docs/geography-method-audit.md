## Scope and counting

Eleven new geography cohorts (California, USA, nine cities) imply 1,100 discovery memberships, 275 alpha memberships, 110 beta memberships, and 44 final shortlist slots. These are not counts of unique organizations, completed unique research reports, or endorsements. Preserve existing SF/Bay work as a separate accepted baseline; do not claim it was newly researched.

Maintain canonical `organizationId` (EIN where applicable, explicit sponsor/project relationship otherwise), `programId`, `reportId`, and `geographyId`. A cohort membership is a unique `(geographyId, organizationId)` unless the user explicitly changes organization counting to program counting. Multiple programs cannot fill several of the same cohort's 100 organization slots. Separate branches with the same legal recipient need explicit identity treatment. Shared research/financial source records belong to the canonical entity/report and are reused by memberships. Report both per-geography membership progress and global distinct organizations/reports; never add cohort totals and label them unique research.

Each stage entry records evidence artifact, version, decision, reviewer/model, UTC start/end, prior-stage membership, and acceptance status. Reuse counts as prior evidence plus a recorded geography-specific transfer review; never create synthetic research time. Same worker intervals may not overlap; shared effort is charged once, with geography allocations totaling at most 100%. Separate unique labor minutes from wall-clock elapsed time and cohort coverage.

## Geography and estimates

Before discovery, lock each city's city-proper or metro definition with a stable boundary version and an explicit county/municipality list; display the chosen scope. A headquarters address alone does not establish beneficiaries in scope. California means California beneficiaries, USA means US beneficiaries; record treatment of territories and cross-border beneficiaries. Existing SF branding currently denotes Bay Area benefits, so SF cannot silently become a city-proper baseline.

Store every estimate against `(reportId, geographyId, costBoundary, fundingRoute, fiscalPeriod/modelVersion)` with total effect, geography-attributed effect, numerator, uncertainty and attribution source. Recompute from underlying effect/cost inputs. Do not reuse Bay-diluted prices for California or USA, and do not automatically call an all-geography value California-only. National totals exclude unmodeled overseas benefits. Nested geographic views may overlap, but portfolio impact totals must not sum CA, USA and constituent city estimates. Preserve nulls when geography allocation is unsupported. Proportional allocation is a labeled assumption requiring evidence, not a default based on population share.

## Stage gates

- Discovery 100: identifiable operating organizations, primary-source activity and beneficiary-geography evidence, deduplicated IDs, program description, eligibility notes and source dates. Broad enough cause/program coverage to avoid filling the list from one convenience directory. Existing leads qualify only after current-scope screening.
- Alpha 25: completed, documented comparable screen of the 100 with finance/source availability, causal pathway, plausible additional-funding use, operating/geographic fit and explicit inclusion/exclusion reasons. Alpha is a research-priority choice, not a numerical impact league table. Requested worker configuration: gpt-6-astra low.
- Beta 10: completed deeper dossiers selected from accepted alpha, source-linked model or justified non-estimability, beneficiary and cost boundaries, three-year finance state, uncertainty/sensitivity, additionality and funding bottlenecks, legal recipient and donation-route evidence, double-counting review, and resolved publication contradictions. Discovery and beta requested configuration: gpt-6-astra medium. A justified null is a completed review but not a quantitative ranking claim.
- Top 4: comparative synthesis of all ten accepted beta reports with documented readiness reasons and caveats, review date and clear provisional research-lead labeling. Rank cannot be an automatic lowest-price slice. If four leads cannot meet the publication gate, leave the cohort incomplete and continue evidence work; do not silently lower standards or fabricate endorsements. No verified funding capacity means no claim that a large grant earns the modeled return.

Acceptance checks must enforce nested membership (4 ⊆ 10 ⊆ 25 ⊆ 100), canonical uniqueness within cohort, target counts versus accepted counts, source and report resolution, no unreviewed cross-geography estimate fallbacks, null preservation, fiscal-sponsor boundaries, exact configured model/reasoning evidence, and separation of planned/running/completed/accepted/published states. Final page acceptance includes correct geography labels/metadata, all 100 discoverable entries with honest stage status, full beta reports, four qualified shortlist entries, source-linked financials and usable navigation. Exact target counts do not excuse poor evidence.

## Concrete reuse and risks in current source

- `app/page.tsx` is the accepted SF homepage: reuse its shortlist layout and copy structure with a geography configuration. `app/san-francisco/page.tsx` merely re-exports that homepage. Its masthead, metadata, photos, four editorial objects, September 11 date, root URL and Bay-specific cautions are hardcoded; copying it directly would publish stale SF content elsewhere. Reuse `app/sf-home.css` and `app/givebetter.css` styles.
- `app/research/page.tsx` supplies the research table, expense component and caveat structure. Its import, captions, geography semantics, links and metadata are SF/Bay-specific. Parameterize data and route scope; do not just change its heading.
- `components/ResearchExpenses.tsx`, `lib/research-expenses.mjs`, `data/research-expenses.json` offer direct financial reuse. They require three consecutive verified fiscal years for an average and expose sources and accounting scope. Preserve organization annual expenses versus program/marginal costs; do not use fiscal sponsor total as project budget. Key expense facts canonically and let geography memberships reference the same record.
- `lib/unified-research-index.ts` and `lib/local-research-estimate.mjs` deliberately select Bay/SF values and null semantics. They are unsafe CA/USA adapters. `lib/expanded-geography-research.ts` already references California, US and international indexes and whole-scope model outputs; useful source leads and adapter patterns, not accepted new-cohort discovery or proof that the output matches a requested boundary.
- `data/donor-readiness.json` already separates conditional leads from recipient/financial issues and owns `homepageSlugs`; make this geography-specific while sharing entity-level warnings. `SfDeepReview.tsx` provides source/uncertainty patterns but hardcodes a non-estimable initial-review narrative, so it cannot be a universal beta template unchanged.
- `scripts/lib/sf-research-funnel.mjs` and `scripts/sf-research-funnel.test.mjs` supply nested-funnel validation ideas, but the old funnel is 6,688 universe / 1,000 screen / 100 priority / 25 reviews and explicitly zero completed CEA, not the requested 100→25→10→4. Version a new cohort schema; do not rename old counts into new stages.
- `scripts/research-session.mjs`, `lib/research-effort.mjs`, `data/research-effort.json` offer UTC intervals, model provenance, duplicate-session and worker-overlap checks. Current phases are research/modeling/source-audit and records are organization-keyed; add stage/geography/report references in a compatible separate ledger or deliberate version rather than violating its validator.
