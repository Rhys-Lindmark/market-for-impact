Prepared 2026-09-09 UTC. Research asset only; no publication, ranking authorization, or organization-count increment. This ledger supersedes the v1 *coverage claim*, not its independently verified arithmetic. A central scenario is not yet a probability-weighted expectation.

## Current service anchors and anti-double-counting facts

FY2025 reports 620,513 meals; 56 MAT enrollees; 96 behavioral-group attendees and 63 case-management enrollees; 369 Women's Center clients; 272 families and 142 children; 317 rental-assistance people and $965,254 transferred. These are throughput, not causal additional outcomes. The same person can receive several services. The report's 10% family multi-program statistic cannot stand in for adult-wide overlap. [GLIDE impact report](https://www.glide.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/GLIDE-Impact-Report-2024-2025.pdf), PDF pages 3, 5–7 (printed 2–3, 6–11). Service-count page 3 and women/behavioral page 6 visually inspected; remaining counts checked in extracted primary text.

Current HEAT advertises low-threshold medication access with SFDPH, partner-clinician wound/vaccine services, and infectious-disease testing/linkage. Existing public clinicians and treatment budgets remain in the no-gift world. The Orlando Chavez peer HCV-navigation program is explicitly paused; broader testing/linkage is still listed. [Current official HEAT](https://www.glide.org/heat/), retrieved 2026-09-09 UTC.

An unrestricted gift does not create another independent life for someone receiving rescue plus MOUD. Let N_R and N_M be incremental rescue-package and effective-MOUD patients after capacity and funding gates; shared=min(N_R,N_M)*overlap. Compute rescue-only, MOUD-only, and both-treatment survival separately for N_R-shared, N_M-shared, and shared. Both-treatment hazard applies the medication hazard ratio to the rescue-adjusted hazard while both are active; after either ends, remove only that intervention's effect. After all active care ends both arms face the same baseline hazard, with the existing survival gap decaying to a fixed horizon. Do not add two all-cause mortality effects, opioid mortality on top of all-cause, or reversals times lifetime QALYs.

Nonfatal groups use *condition/person/time* exclusions, not a claim that clients never overlap. Baseline utility on extra survivor-years does not itself duplicate symptom relief during years lived in both arms. Exclude shared symptom-time on extra survivor-years and depression/safety effects credited by housing, CBT, advocacy or parenting from one another using explicit unique-health fractions. Those fractions are priors needing linked data; test zero uniqueness. All external resource costs are gross associated activity, not demonstrated net induced societal cost.

## Clinical anchors: what they do and do not identify

- MOUD: post-overdose Massachusetts cohort found buprenorphine all-cause adjusted HR 0.63, methadone 0.47; observational, 2012–2014, not causal GLIDE effects. Use 0.63 with explicit confounding/local transfer rather than transplanting both. Treatment exposure was monthly and typically short. [Larochelle 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29913516/). ED initiation increased 30-day engagement versus referral, but does not establish GLIDE retention or mortality. [D'Onofrio RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25919527/).
- Behavioral groups: BRIGHT used sixteen two-hour depression sessions in residential substance-use treatment, with nonrandomized alternating site-period assignment. GLIDE's outpatient mixed groups are not proven equivalent. The model needs selected depressed share and delivery transfer, not a CBT effect for all attendees. [Watkins primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21646576/).
- Women: intensive community advocacy in a randomized study improved violence/quality-of-life outcomes; a separate 12-week community advocacy RCT did not yield clinically meaningful depression or HRQoL improvement. Central short utility gain must be weak and permit null/harm, not a universal violence-prevention effect. [Sullivan/Bybee](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10028208/), [Tiwari](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20682933/).
- Parenting/children: a 12-week Incredible Years trial selected preschool children above a conduct-problem cutoff and found short-term behavioral improvement; not proof GLIDE offers that curriculum or that ordinary childcare has the same effect. Another randomized trial did not sustain parent-reported differences to one year. Child and parent clinical utility conversions are separate analyst judgments, not source QALYs. [Hutchings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17350966/), [one-year counterweight](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33573694/).
- Meals: a small diabetic-food-insecurity crossover trial of medically tailored delivery found fewer self-reported hypoglycemia/mental-health-interference days. GLIDE communal meals differ. A larger intensive food-as-medicine trial did not improve HbA1c relative to usual care. Thus no generic meal-to-diabetes-mortality coefficient; model only finite relief among people whose inadequate intake actually changes. [Berkowitz trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30421335/), [Doyle trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38147326/).
- HCV: colocated low-threshold care improved cure relative to facilitated referral in an RCT of RNA-positive recent injectors. GLIDE's current broader referral pathway resembles aspects of the comparator, not necessarily the enhanced intervention. Use a small explicit earlier-cure probability difference; the trial's 44.2-point difference is not a coefficient for tests. [Eckhardt trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35285851/). Clinical/coordination costs in that setting were $4,867 per participant before treating reimbursement as equivalent to actual resources; use local full clinical cost prior, not that number as a current GLIDE quote. [Primary economic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36644226/).

## Missing variables and cheapest disconfirming requests

1. Foundation-only marginal budget and grant restrictions; current gift tranche, unspent restricted balances and fixed staffing: could eliminate assumed ordinary-gift additionality without changing clinical efficacy.
2. Linked unique-person program roster and entry/exit dates: resolves rescue/MOUD and mental-health overlap. Reported volume alone cannot do this.
3. MAT initiation, 30/90/180-day retention, no-GLIDE accessible alternatives, public clinician costs: a 56-enrollee count is not 56 extra year-long courses.
4. Behavioral group curriculum, attendance dose and baseline symptoms; women's advocacy intensity; parenting/child program fidelity: broad participation with little clinical content could null these branches.
5. Meal consumption and replacement survey, severe shortfall prevalence, actual meals per regular client and incremental kitchen capacity: high substitution or fixed production could null modeled meal health.
6. New positive RNA diagnoses, earlier completed treatment, downstream funded drug/lab costs; do not extrapolate paused program plans.
7. Partner-clinic wound patient counts, usual time to alternative care and full provider cost; symptom-care prior is not an observed treatment effect.

Version-2 model JSON is a transparent prior specification for these branches. It is not independently accepted, and neither a report schema nor a probability-weighted whole-organization expectation has yet been established. Policy effects, infection prevention/secondary transmission, and affiliate flows remain genuine named omissions requiring signed stress tests before an expected-health headline.
