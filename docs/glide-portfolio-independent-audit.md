## Clarification resolved during audit

The formulas discount service delay but do not apply mortality before service starts. The author clarified model/review/report consistently: expected OD and housing caseloads are conditioned alive at service start; delay shifts that service and its health stream relative to the gift, with mortality hazards applied afterward. If a future version instead defines caseload as alive at gift date, it must account for survival to care. No numerical adjustment was needed for the clarified current unit.
