## Finite overdose survival

Timing clarification: both OD and housing caseloads count expected people alive at intervention start, not people enrolled alive at the gift date. Expected service-start caseload already conditions on reaching care alive. Delay is time from gift to that service and only discounts health; mortality operates after service starts. A cohort fixed at gift date would instead require pre-service survival adjustment. This packet does not claim that different cohort.

Initially alive unique people, not doses or rescue episodes. Baseline total fatal hazard = .06 other mortality + .5 recurrent overdoses/year × .1 fatality without effective rescue × (1−.8 baseline rescue) = .07. Supported hazard is .065 for one year from a .1 rescue-probability increment, then returns to .07. Integrate the survival difference, utility .7 and 3% discount, over maximum 15 years after .25-year delay. People remain exposed to future overdoses; no permanent lifetime saved is credited. External post-overdose cohorts establish recurrence and competing-mortality relevance, not these current local rates. No independent MOUD/HIV/HCV/mortality gains are stacked.

## Separate housing morbidity bridge

[The VA model](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2825636), Table 1, uses .112 stable-at-discharge probability difference for TFA versus no TFA in homelessness prevention. This is an observationally grounded input, not a GLIDE randomized trial. We retain .5 for local transfer, assume the resulting .056 probability difference lasts one year, and multiply by .1 morbidity-only utility gap with common .01 mortality hazard and 3% discount after .1-year delay. The local .8 baseline-stability probability is an illustrative prior and validates support probabilities, not a claim that it equals the VA .821 comparator. Persistence, utility, local baseline and transfer are all priors. The source's two-year .144 QALY result, stable-state utility of 1 and mortality advantage are not imported. This removes the old opaque .072 whole-health transfer rather than merely scaling its denominator.

Only one adult-equivalent per household is eligible for credit. A .9 disjoint share excludes adults in the OD cohort from housing benefits; all housing costs and shared harms remain. No child/caregiver or other household multiplier. Signed housing worsening is permitted; health duration is at most two years, central one. Housing and OD harm inputs are gift-date PV per additional offer; independent gift-date PV harm survives zero activity and excludes deterioration already counted by signed clinical effects.
