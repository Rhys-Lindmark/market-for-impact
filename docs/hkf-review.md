# Healthier Kids Foundation: whole-gift research review

September 8, 2026. Conditional research estimate; independent acceptance pending. No Site or checkout changes, outreach, gift, or report-count increment.

## Result and decision

An ordinary $100,000 gift gives **0.268367 overall/US QALYs**, **0.265683 Bay QALYs**, and no identified SF-city QALYs centrally. Central donor price is **$3,726,243 overall / $3,763,882 Bay per 10 QALYs**. Modeled gross associated resources are $167,601.71, giving **$6,245,247 overall / $6,308,330 Bay**. SF positive ratios are null, not zero cost. These are not exhaustive/net societal-resource estimates.

Joint favorable assumptions produce 22.295970 overall / 22.073010 Bay QALYs: **$45,304 donor / $147,744 associated resources per 10 Bay QALYs**. This is upside, not a confidence interval or available offer. The cautious bundle is slightly negative. No threshold retuning: full research materially weakens the earlier $375,000 screening hypothesis by accounting for the complete pipeline, lower vision allocation, treatability and earlier-care timing. Unquantified work may still add substantial benefit.

## Funnel and clinical evidence

Cash buys the full pipeline: $30 per screen plus referral navigation$50/$50/$75 for vision/dental/hearing. These are direct program-cost priors; general operations are paid separately once. [VisionFirst](https://hkidsf.org/our-programs/visionfirst/) reports14% needing care and around20,000 screens/year; cumulative glasses recipients cannot be divided by that annual denominator. [DentalFirst](https://hkidsf.org/our-programs/dentalfirst/) reports29% needing follow-up, and its testimonial explicitly closes paperwork before all fillings are complete. [HearingFirst](https://hkidsf.org/our-programs/hearingfirst/) reports4%; an ear-cleaning example warns against treating every referral as permanent hearing impairment. Those rates are undated operational yields, not causal treatment effects.

Funding additionality50% limits new capacity; caps1,000 extra screens per pathway are absorption priors. Completion70/65/60% means reaching an appropriate clinical examination/treatment episode, not closing a referral. Treatable shares80/35/25% restrict positive health further; dental selects symptomatic disease rather than all painless concerns. Clinical severity/completion data are missing. Central additional completed episodes are19.8649/38.1236/5.0909, before these treatability and overlap filters.

Vision utility .025 is a judgment, not an imported trial estimate. The [Zambia cohort](https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/1604317/1/1478-7547-12-6.pdf) had an uncontrolled .075 EQ-5D improvement among41/113 followed recipients and substantial age/setting mismatch. Weak-source scenario scales .075 by41/113 and an additional .25 transfer factor; this is a diagnostic assumption, not an unbiased correction for missing data. The [SF preschool wear cohort](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaophthalmology/fullarticle/2776359) supports plausible sustained use, but its133/188 meeting a school wear threshold is not a measured fraction of all-day benefit; effective-use .70 remains a prior.

Dental utility .08 applies only to the symptomatic treatable subset. The [primary child pre/post study](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/169642/1/The%20impact%20of%20dental%20caries%20and%20its%20treatment%20under%20general%20anaesthetic%20on%20children%20and%20their%20families.pdf) supports clinical plausibility but has attrition and no untreated control. Its CHU9D summed score change is not preference-weighted utility and is never mapped mechanically. Hearing .01 is especially speculative: [randomized pediatric OME evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11124783/) reports no significant additional QoL improvement. The abstract was available through indexed primary-source text, not full-page verification. No-hearing-health scenario tests this directly; central hearing contributes only .0000895 net QALY.

## Finite counterfactual and cost boundary

[DHCS](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/medi-cal-resources/medi-cal-for-kids-teens/member-information/) already provides relevant covered care and access assistance. Coverage is not actual uptake; absence of a charity gift is not absence of medicine. Among clinically eligible cases, no-equivalent-care-at-start fractions .6/.6/.5 decay at alternative-care hazards .7/2/2 per year. Separate correction-loss/recurrence hazards .15/1/1 erase benefit even without alternative care. Horizons are two years for vision and six months for dental/hearing after treatment, not lifetime. All are labeled priors, tested widely.

Expected cohorts are alive at intervention start. Delays .15/.15/.2 years discount to the gift date only; mortality .0002/year then applies during the finite window, alongside3% discount. This is earlier morbidity relief, not lives saved. Amblyopia acuity improvement does not establish permanently prevented blindness from earlier HKF access; no separate lifetime stream is credited.

Vision then dental then hearing form a primary-person ledger, with nonoverlap1/.9/.85. No second generic utility credit is assigned to excluded overlapping people. All costs and episode harms remain. Harms .0001/.0005/.0002 per additional completed episode are gift-date PV losses outside lasting signed utility, charged before treatability/alternative filters. Independent harm remains at zero activity. Negative/zero health is not floored; positive price ratios are withheld.

The $167,601.71 associated-resource envelope keeps every gift dollar plus nominal partner/insurance clinical and screening resources and a$10,000 allowance elsewhere. It deliberately does not disappear when the gift replaces other financing. This is gross associated resource use, **not net induced societal spending** or exhaustive accounting of the expanded organization. No insurer payment, donated work, or patient time is automatically free. Nonquantified program health retains cash cost; the calculator does not assert those programs have no effects.
