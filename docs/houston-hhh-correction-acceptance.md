# Healthcare for the Homeless - Houston — corrected alpha (29 September 2026)

The original Houston alpha incorrectly treated 659 reported depression screening/follow-up cases as a treated depression cohort. Independent review of the [official 2025 HRSA UDS workbook](https://data.hrsa.gov/DataDownload/StaticDocuments/H80-2025.xlsx) confirmed Table 6B cell AY788 is **screening and follow-up as appropriate**, not depression treatment; its separate remission cohort is 55 eligible patients and its outcome numerator is suppressed. The depression QALY pathway is therefore unestimated, not zero benefit. The workbook's Table 5 cells BE788/BG788 identify 1,499 dental clinic visits and 595 dental patients, while the [organization's annual report](https://www.homeless-healthcare.org/s/FINAL-2025-Impact-Report.pdf) lists 3,219 dental visits under an unreconciled scope. The corrected exploratory model uses the narrower defined UDS series without silently combining scopes.

The corrected whole-expense, partial-benefit illustration is **$55.6 million per 10 Houston-metro QALYs centrally**, or $30.3 million from the signed scenario mixture. Both retain the unaudited $7,844,806 whole-recipient expense and uncalibrated dental relief, counterfactual, geography and funding-response priors. Five scenarios were independently recalculated. The model is not a current marginal donation offer or complete organization valuation. The site retains a donor **HOLD**.

[Harris County file 26-5142](https://harriscountytx.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8167289&GUID=9F12FC50-645B-4A78-B668-6049CC4D835E&FullText=1) records termination of a separate $70,000 dental project after HHH could not implement it because of staffing and priorities; the [termination attachment](https://harriscountytx.legistar.com/View.ashx?M=F&ID=15756863&GUID=E060471F-2059-41E2-8A54-E101BF76C76E) records no reimbursement as of May 31, 2026. This does not establish that all dental care stopped, but it contradicts an assumption that another gift alone would create capacity.

The additional author pass was 22.11 actual minutes of research plus 3.46 minutes of modeling on GPT-6 Astra Light. The independent GPT-6 Astra Medium audit was 1.76 actual minutes. Exact session times, workbook cell map, source SHA256, scenario arithmetic and acceptance are preserved in `outputs/houston-hhh-followon/` in the task workspace. The corrected report and two dedicated regression checks passed the local geography validation suite.
