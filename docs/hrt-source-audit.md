Do not present$36 as a September2026 quotation. It can remain a labeled historical cost anchor or conservative prior, with a current-price sensitivity. Thus24 packs means48 single-use sprays—not24 sprays or48 distinct rescues. Its restrictions prevent profitable resale, insurance billing or end-user charges by customers. These are issuer descriptions, not an independently audited marginal-cost ledger.

The [December2023 shipment announcement](https://www.harmreductiontherapeutics.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/HRT-First-Shipment-Release-Dec-18_FINAL17.pdf) used92%/8% and$36, versus95%/5% on current pages. ## Actual use and clinical evidence

The latest primary [2025 Community Feedback Report, released April2026](https://www.harmreductiontherapeutics.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-RiVive-Community-Feedback-Report.pdf) reports19 convenience interviews at two Pennsylvania programs, with compensation and self-reported experience. Several statistics use smaller denominators: six of15 report a product preference, not six of19 or40% of all supplied recipients. This is qualitative acceptability/experience evidence. It does not identify the probability a shipped spray is used, dose-to-unique-person conversion, comparative survival or unbiased withdrawal incidence. Product preferences and reactions must not be converted directly into QALYs. Manufacturer wording about lower-dose superiority is not a randomized3mg-versus4mg mortality result.

Required funnel: new donor-funded capacity → genuinely incremental delivered stock → delivery to appropriate responders → still available at an overdose → nonduplicated response → survival/health difference versus other timely rescue → finite, nonoverlapping person-time. Inventory, shipment, distribution, carried stock, use and recorded reversal are distinct units. Multiple sprays at one event cannot receive separate full rescues; repeated rescues of one person cannot each restart a full lifetime tail. Expiration, repeat supply, other brands/IM availability, rescue breathing and EMS remain part of baseline. Counterfactual product substitution is not automatically additional survival.

## Finite-survival and causal-use verification

The primary [Olfson recurrence cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30005310/) reports 295 repeat overdoses per 1,000 person-years and 1,154 fatal opioid overdoses per 100,000 person-years. The [companion mortality analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6143082/) reports 778.3 all-cause deaths per 10,000 person-years. These historical Medicaid claims cohorts establish serious recurrent risk, not current HRT recipient hazards or an observed rescue increment. Separate other-cause and modeled opioid mortality; do not add an unpartitioned all-cause rate to a second overdose hazard.

[Weiner's discharged emergency-department cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6920606/) similarly conditions on surviving discharge: 5.5% died within one year. It is not acute untreated-overdose fatality. A finite benefit model should integrate the difference between the same initially alive people's survival curves once; repeated nonfatal responses must not restart a life tail. HRT's current draft does this, ending extra protection after one year centrally while allowing the survival difference to persist only to a ten-year horizon.

[Walley's community interrupted time series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23372174/) is observational implementation evidence, not a per-pack randomized mortality estimate. The [2025 Pittsburgh response study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0315026) ends data collection in January 2023, before RiVive approval: its 1.63 reported doses per response is not a RiVive pack-to-unique-person conversion. The current draft appropriately treats placement, repeat-pack/person capacity, effective-rescue improvement and marginal funding as explicit priors rather than importing recorded reversals as lives saved.

The [August 31, 2026 SSDP partner announcement](https://ssdp.org/blog/carry-naloxone-know-your-rights-save-a-life-ssdps-hope-puts-the-tools-in-your-hands/) independently confirms donated RiVive in HOPE kits, beyond Remedy. It establishes an active distribution mechanism, not linked additional use, local residence or a causal product advantage. The [current DHCS FAQ](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/individuals/naloxone-distribution-project/ndp-frequently-asked-questions/) confirms April 2026 generic nasal substitution, continued free public supply, and a CalRx retail alternative. No current cheapest-product assertion is warranted from the historical HRT launch price.
