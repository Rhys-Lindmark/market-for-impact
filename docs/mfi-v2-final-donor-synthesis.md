# What the ten deeper reviews imply for giving

September 11, 2026. Decision synthesis from the accepted V2 packets, not new research or a verified offer. A “better life” means **10 additional quality-adjusted life years (QALYs), potentially spread across people**. Prices below are central modeled donor costs, not observed returns. The frozen ten were a research cohort, not the ten best charities across the full library. FUF's final independent audit accepts transparent exploratory publication, while retaining a hold on a giving recommendation.

## Bottom line

**ReCARES is the strongest first diligence candidate in this cohort. Project Homeless Connect is a plausible second, with a more concrete funding disruption but greater current-capacity uncertainty. HOPE Pacifica merits a focused financial and delivery check rather than an immediate high-confidence recommendation.** These are comparative judgments about where additional investigation seems useful, not claims that any organization has offered extra health at its modeled price.

ReCARES combines a small whole-accounting cost base with a direct equipment-access mechanism. Its central $74,720/10 Bay QALYs is encouraging, but depends on unmet need, safe sustained use and donor-created throughput. The FY2025 filing reports $253,225 in cash/savings/investments and $60,000 in grants receivable; that contradicts a simple cash-starvation story, not the possibility of useful expansion. Ask which staffing, collection, storage or matching constraint an added gift would remove and why current resources will not cover it. [Original FY2025 filing](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/full_text/202621259349200012/IRS990EZ), [current receiving rules](https://www.recares.org/receive).

Project Homeless Connect's central $774,408 retains the entire sponsored-project gift while quantifying glasses, hearing and dentures. Unlike generic appeals, its public record identifies a real historical funding loss: the city contract ended June 30, 2025 and its sponsor announced a Bridge Campaign. That strengthens the case for asking about replacement funding, but does not establish an unfilled clinical tranche now. Confirm present project budget, money already raised, partner treatment slots and completed care—not only event contacts. Community Initiatives' whole expense is not PHC's expense. [Adopted city minutes](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/HOC__June_5th_mtg_minutes_adopted.pdf), [sponsor's September 2025 campaign notice](https://communityinitiatives.org/blog/project-news-september-5-2025/), [Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/).

HOPE's central $554,660 is promising but substantially less financially anchored: the annual $20k/$40k/$100k operating envelopes are priors, and cumulative naloxone units are not annual unique people protected. San Mateo County offers free stand-box hardware/shipping; the potentially useful private increment is access, placement, outreach and reliable restocking beyond that baseline, not automatically another purchased box. Obtain a current expense ledger and deduplicated risk-network/restocking account before inferring a cheap expansion. [HOPE locations](https://www.hope4change650.org/narcan-locations-information), [county access program](https://www.smchealth.org/post/overdose-prevention-coalition-launches-naloxone-program-expands-free-access-county).

## Shared reasons to be cautious

- **Additional useful care is the missing unit.** Devices, visits, screenings, naloxone packs, trees and policy passage are intermediate outputs. Health requires appropriate recipients, completion, sustained use and a better counterfactual.
- **Public alternatives matter without proving access.** Medi-Cal vision/hearing benefits, OTC hearing aids and free county naloxone hardware can reduce the donor increment. Eligibility does not prove timely usable care. Model the delay or access gap actually closed, and include outside clinician/public/volunteer resources. [Medi-Cal vision benefits](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/providers-partners/pharmacy-benefits-division-and-vision-care-program/medi-cal-vision-benefits/), [hearing benefits](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/hearing-aid-benefit-cap-benefits-frequently-asked-questions-for-members/).
- **Finite health and optimistic tails change comparisons.** A hearing-sensitive utility instrument can show larger gains than generic measures; rescue models can obtain most benefit from a finite post-intervention survival tail. Neither is repeated annual treatment. Weighted results must remain labeled and paired with central/null/harm cases.
- **Whole cost is not complete benefit.** Several reports retain every donor dollar but quantify only named health pathways. Unquantified mission value is not zero. Conversely, a conditional program result cannot become an ordinary-gift result by changing its label.

## What a donor can reasonably do now

General mission support through an identified official recipient is a legitimate decision, but it is different from buying modeled health. For an effectiveness-led gift, start with the few concrete questions above: the extra activity, full cost, existing commitments, available capacity and likely alternative. No inspected packet establishes a priced marginal health offer. Do not let that common uncertainty erase the comparative evidence—but do not hide it behind a precise dollar figure either.
