# NYC first three acceptance

Transportation Alternatives, OnPoint NYC and Bergen Volunteer Medical Initiative are accepted initial reports for the official22-county MSA. Each was selected from the independently accepted100-recipient pool. They add three initial reports and zero beta reviews or giving picks.

The independent audit beside this file reproduces all19 scenarios and checks original expense evidence, active delivery, clinical/road-safety sources, counterfactuals, geography and actual researcher intervals. Root reviewed the corrected reports and retained all central values. The models are conditional health estimates using full recipient resources, not complete portfolio valuations or verified marginal donation offers.

Transportation Alternatives retains a finite corridor-injury calculation and induced public-cost stress. OnPoint uses one trajectory per modeled person, explicitly approximates annual hazard change and carries competing mortality after the intervention year. Its historical intervention count is not observed deaths averted; its merger-period accounts cannot yet support a comparable three-year mean. BVMI uses distinct patients rather than repeat visits, includes audited donated services and goods once, and tests additional partner resources.

All actual author sessions are retained once (846 seconds across the three reports); interrupted prior TA work is uncredited. Independent audit and integration are not author time. Functional spending and native-unit explanations remain in the reports and model detail. No numerical assumptions were changed to improve rankings.

Release checks cover scenario arithmetic, all source/session links, exact selected cohort membership, responsive report rendering and canonical publication. Deployment must be terminal before claiming these three are live.
