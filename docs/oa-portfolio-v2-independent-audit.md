## Primary comparator checks

- [CTS primary economic trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1660539/): utility measured at 12 months, not an integrated lifetime quantity; substantial comparator crossover. The model's utility is explicitly independent.
- [Ms AUB trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/198438): expanded medical care, not ablation, is the comparator. Short-term improvement and later crossover do not establish permanent gain.
- [PEOPLE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36538310/): pessary versus surgery with crossover/attrition; failure to demonstrate noninferiority is not proof of a generic surgery-versus-no-care effect. Report does not make that claim.
- [Torkki trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11368700/) and [follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12807332/): painful bunions, not every tendon/orthopedic condition; waiting followed by treatment permits catch-up. Broader case mix remains a judgment.
- [TURP trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7527493/): symptom and composite treatment-failure findings are not mortality effects. Modern medication alternatives remain a transfer limitation.
- [NAIROS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38477237/): septoplasty versus nasal medical therapy, not a SNOT22-to-QALY conversion.
- [REACTIV primary report](https://njl-admin.nihr.ac.uk/document/download/2001311): 0.054 discounted QALY is integrated over two years, not an annual utility. The independent model prior is not that coefficient repeated.
- [C-GALL](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38943314/): conservative management is a meaningful alternative and the overall QALY contrast is uncertain. Small signed net benefit with procedural harm is coherent; no broad lifesaving claim.
