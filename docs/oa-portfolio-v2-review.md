Research assets only, September9,2026 UTC. v1 preserved as historical whole-cost/partial-health calculation, not the expected organization return. No report-count change or Site edits.

## Result

Under explicit diagnostic case-mix and clinical-transfer priors, expected whole-organization health for $100,000 becomes0.513674QALYs: national donor$1,946,762/10Q, Bay$3,244,603, SF$24,334,519. Associated resource ratios are$7,185,600/$11,976,000/$89,819,997. The old three pathways supplied only0.064065QALY; named additions supply0.449609 net QALYs. Joint favorable assumptions give2.150969QALYs ($464,907 national donor/10Q), not a confidence bound. No thresholds were used to choose inputs.

The correct claim is **expected whole-organization health conditional on the inspectable case-mix prior**, not measured OA value. It is no longer three selected services divided into the entire gift. Every specialty's nominal services are allocated exactly once to a named clinical pathway or the explicit2%other-assessment null. Actual modern within-specialty diagnosis frequencies are unknown; therefore this is still a highly uncertain research estimate, not release acceptance or a donation recommendation.

## Primary clinical comparators and transfer limits

- CTS: [Gerritsen2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12215131/) compares open release with wrist splinting. The [primary economic paper](https://research.vu.nl/ws/files/2213413/195347.pdf), Korthals-de Bos2006, Table2 and results, gives utility0.85vs0.81 at12months, difference0.04, CI−0.004to0.08. This is not a measured integrated QALY increment and is not multiplied by years. The model's0.03utility-point successful-person-year value is an independent transfer prior with finite catch-up. Dutch tariffs and the authors' personnel-cost discussion are not imported as current OA costs.
- AUB: [Ms trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15039411/) is hysterectomy versus expanded medical treatment in women dissatisfied with prior medication, NOT hysterectomy versus ablation. Six-month quality-of-life outcomes favored surgery; substantial later hysterectomy crossover reduced long-run differences. Its SF36 outcomes are not converted directly to QALYs. Model0.1utility gain/.8success and .015acute burden are explicit priors for appropriately selected refractory disease; fertility preferences, recovery and harms matter.
- Pelvic prolapse: [PEOPLE trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36538310/) compares pessary and surgery, with large crossover and attrition. It prevents assuming zero benefit from conservative care. Model0.07utility prior is not a direct score conversion or established surgery-vs-no-care effect.
- Foot: [Torkki2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11368700/) supports earlier relief for painful hallux valgus, not all orthopaedic cases. [Two-year follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12807332/) finds catch-up after later surgery. Extrapolation to the separate tendon/trigger-finger portion is an explicit mechanistic prior, not trial evidence. Degenerative knee surgery has zero benefit centrally, consistent with [sham-controlled evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24369076/); its recovery burden remains.
- Nasal obstruction: [NAIROS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38477237/) compares septoplasty against steroid/saline medical therapy, not no treatment. Symptom improvement supports a pathway, not SNOT22-to-utility equivalence. The0.04utility prior and short waiting contrast are independent assumptions.
- BPH: [Wasson1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7527493/) compares TURP against watchful waiting and finds symptom/treatment-failure benefit. Modern medication options change the comparator. The model uses only finite symptom utility, not death prevention from its composite failure endpoint. Stone and scrotal rows have separately labeled clinical/utility priors, not a borrowed TURP effect.
- Veins: [REACTIV](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16707070/) supports a modest integrated two-year health benefit from surgery versus conservative care. Its0.054discounted QALY estimate is not annual utility. The model's0.04utility-point prior passes through response, catch-up, failure, mortality, and a finite horizon; it is not an unchanged trial quantity.
- Gallstones: [C-GALL](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38943314/) finds no significant overall QALY difference and substantial conservative-arm later surgery. Central0.01utility is a small uncertain selected-patient prior, with harm retained; it is not a strong surgery benefit claim.
- Cyst/skin, anorectal, ear, benign diagnostic, stones and scrotal rows are explicitly weaker clinical-indication priors. No trial effect from a different organ is assigned. Diagnostic rows require management linkage; negative tests alone do not cure disease. No unverified breast/skin/gynaecologic cancer-survival benefit is inserted to improve the estimate.

## Finite health, waiting and costs

Added rows use distinct people = nominal specialty services×diagnostic share×common additional-activity factor÷services per episode×disjoint fraction. Expected gross health/person = success/linkage×signed utility gain×discounted implementation delay×finite annuity(catch-up+loss+death+discount,H). Central additionality remains0.5, cap100services; new rows use3year horizon and .7/year counterfactual catch-up, not actual treated waiting time. Failure/recurrence.1and mortality.02further limit benefit. These are hypotheses about incremental earlier appropriate care; primary medical/orthotic/pessary alternatives are reflected in the modest signed utility contrast, not ignored.

The delay0.25year is donor-to-treatment lag; the no-gift care clock starts at enabled treatment. Expected caseload is frozen as people alive at service start, so no additional pre-service mortality is applied to that already-conditioned count. New acute harm is gift-date PV per unique episode and includes recovery/complications regardless of success. It is not removed when benefit is set to zero. No later comparator surgical harm is credited back, a conservative simplification. All horizons/rates/signed gains are bounded; no repeated surgery earns another whole-person award.

The foundation already charges coordination cash, outside clinical resources, support and patient time across ALL nominal services. Adding health does not add a second hospital resource bill or erase old costs. Six diagnostic/follow-on pathways receive an extra$200per nominal service planning allowance for further treatment not in the initial specialty encounter. It is not a real quote; no insurer or public payment is subtracted. Waived charges still are not opportunity costs. Only gross-associated resources are reported; this is not net induced societal cost. New utility and wait estimates are not derived from dollars, and there is no inverse allocation uplift.

## Remaining work

The crucial missing evidence is the actual2025procedure/diagnosis crosswalk, medically appropriate no-gift treatment and wait distributions, treatment linkage after diagnostic services, patient-level repeat/cross-specialty overlap, and clinical opportunity costs. The case-mix prior intentionally exposes these uncertainties rather than pretending broad specialties are observed diagnoses. Independent review is required before replacing the published estimate.
