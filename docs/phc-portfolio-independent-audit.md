## Sources and finite clinical transfer

[Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/) confirms annual prescription glasses, partner hearing aids and follow-up, and full dentures through a lottery. These are actual services, not proof of marginal capacity or unrestricted allocations. Existing [Medi-Cal vision coverage](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/providers-partners/pharmacy-benefits-division-and-vision-care-program/medi-cal-vision-benefits/) supports an equivalent-care baseline; coverage is not treated as realized access. The report does not infer that every PHC client lacks or has particular benefits.

Independently inspected [Zambia primary study](https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/1604317/1/1478-7547-12-6.pdf), abstract and Table 4: 113 recruited spectacle patients, 41 six-month follow-ups, utility .850 to .925. Packet correctly avoids calling .075 a randomized effect or full-cohort result, and exposes an attrition stress. [Denture trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/bdj.2007.569) confirms 133 older edentate adults, immediate versus waiting-list care, three-month oral daily-performance outcomes—not generic QALYs. The hearing and denture utility coefficients are explicit priors; no oral/communication/dementia/depression scores are separately stacked. Primary hearing page returned no body on direct access during this audit; indexed primary record was available, and no numerical hearing effect from that trial is imported.

Each pathway has at most one year of credited use after delayed care. Common mortality and discount operate over delay plus benefit time. Fraction with equivalent alternative care receives no incremental durable utility. Financial replacement is separately applied. Clinical caps are illustrative, not verified open slots. The patient-year ledger prioritizes glasses, then nonoverlapping hearing, then nonoverlapping dentures; discarded overlaps keep all costs and episode harms. Signed durable worsening and distinct gift-date PV acute/independent harms remain possible.
