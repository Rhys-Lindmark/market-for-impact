## Resource and counterfactual boundary

All donated dollars remain priced, including other clinical care, education/research, facilities and central activities. The$1,000 direct-course research budget retains referral/noncompletion costs; separately budgeted central activities are not charged twice inside it. Outside$2,500 resources include only clinical/facility/lens inputs not already in donor cash or intercompany transfers. Public inputs are zero only in the central unpriced narrow-route ledger, with a separate$500 sensitivity—not a factual claim of no public support anywhere.

Gross associated resources retain all nominal-course outside inputs even when the donor replaces funding. Net resources count financing-additional outside inputs and subtract an explicit assumed otherwise-later outside-care cost at its discounted time. Central offset$12,130.57 is the present value of five otherwise-later$2,500 resource courses, not an observed saving. A zero-offset case exposes this uncertainty. No speculative offset is applied to the full donor gift or the unquantified portfolio; participant time and additional non-surgical costs remain unresolved.
