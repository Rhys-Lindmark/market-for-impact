# Featured four follow the research-list ranking

User direction on 18 October 2026: the featured four and the four lowest positive $/DALY references must be the same in every city, California and USA edition.

`featuredReports` now shares the full research list's sorting and `researchListPrice` definition. It includes accepted initial reports and retained historical references in active editions, while labeling review stage and historical status on the landing cards. Missing annual expenses do not remove an organization from the cost ranking. Images and reviewed native-unit denominators are optional presentation data; their absence must not suppress the four cards or invent a unit price.

The prior independently accepted editorial slates and progress registry remain historical research provenance. This display-policy change does not alter reports, models, accepted review stages, financial data, or research-effort records. The new `displayPolicy` metadata makes the distinction explicit. Dynamic display selection prevents future drift as ranked prices change.
