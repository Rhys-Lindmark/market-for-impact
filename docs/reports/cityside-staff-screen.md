## Two concrete mechanisms checked

1. **Vaccine access information:** Cityside's [historical2020–2021 impact account](https://citysidejournalism.org/report/stories-of-impact/) describes bilingual flyers and more than3,000 vaccine-text registrations. This is a real service-journalism channel, but not3,000 additional vaccinations. The missing quantities are current annual eligible users, vaccination acceleration versus other information, risk-specific clinical benefit and donor-created continuation. Reusing a2021 cohort for2026 would be a period error. The report also describes wildfire guides without measured behavior or exposure change.

2. **Food-safety accountability:** a council agenda search surfaced references to Berkeleyside reporting, but a separate [public auditor/council process](https://dev.berkeleyca.gov/city-council-regular-meeting-eagenda-july-30-2024) already requested inspection reforms. The [primary July2025 status report](https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/legislative-body-meeting-agendas/09-25-25-CHCFinalAgendaPacket.pdf), pp10–14, reports new policies, inspector/supervisor hires, and the division's statement that previously missed high-risk facilities were inspected. It does **not** identify Cityside's causal share or illnesses prevented. A $100,000 consultant budget referral had **not** entered final budget proposals as of that report; referral is not committed funding. Existing inspectors, fees, auditor work and other media are a substantial counterfactual. This is not a basis for charging a current gift with the whole reform's health effect.

The historical impact account includes police-accountability admissions, but admissions are not a quantified reduction in subsequent injury. No mortality award is inferred. Other civic, cultural, information and democratic benefits remain unquantified, not zero.

Large reach can clear either threshold only if the behavior/policy effect is genuinely additional; tiny invented attribution fractions are not evidence.

The exact missing variables are (a) a representative annual opportunity denominator, (b) journalism-caused implementation difference beyond existing oversight, (c) nonoverlapping exposed people/risk change with finite health duration, and (d) cash response. A future candidate portfolio could be modeled with explicit subjective scenarios once these have at least topic-specific anchors. No observed outcome need be perfect, but current sources do not define a representative prospective portfolio.
