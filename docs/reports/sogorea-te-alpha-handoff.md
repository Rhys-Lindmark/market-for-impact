## Audit attention

1. Every people, disruption, unsupported-share, ARR, utility-duration and funding coefficient is an unvalidated analyst judgment. Four hubs alone does not statistically identify a health model. Audit must retain this limitation rather than promote a weighted number to empirical evidence.
2. Capacity/hub is a partition of unique network people; do not sum overlapping visitors. Annual disruption probability aggregates repeated events; no rescue-count or recurring-person survival bug.
3. Response jointly covers ordinary-gift allocation to readiness and net additional capacity despite baseline funding. Do not add a second readiness budget fraction. Public alternatives also affect otherwise-unsupported share, which is a different boundary from financing.
4. No cultural/communal QALY coefficient is invented. Numerous core mission benefits remain unquantified. No full-return or zero-total-value claim.
5. Finite years are conditional morbidity-equivalent duration; not a death or life-expectancy claim. A hypothetical harm world is explicit, not observed harm.
6. A donor decision needs current hub capacities/activation logs, alternatives and a priced additional spend plan explaining reserves/capital commitments. Unknown marginal room alone is not a reason to forbid exploratory modeling; here it is a reason to HOLD giving.
