September8,2026. Frozen read-only snapshot: `work/market-for-impact-hpp`. **50 unique indexed reports:8 whole-gift partial-health models;42 program-only models.** Every indexed slug has an actual report renderer. This is neither50 new organizations nor a count of screening memos. The current49/50 ambiguity resolves to50 in this snapshot; earlier felton snapshot has49 because HPP was not yet added.

The CSV contains exact absolute proof file/line, index line and renderer for every row. This inventory checks the modeled spending unit, not causal validity, deployment status or current donor room. A $100k illustrative program budget, full offered-course cost, included overhead or audited program ratio is **not** a whole-organization unrestricted allocation. Demographic/geographic allocation is not the missing organizational allocation.

## Revision groups

- G0 (8): preserve whole-gift boundary; health remains partial and allocation judgmental. No new scope revision needed merely to standardize a dollar figure.
- G1 (4): highest presentation priority—SFAF,PHC,GLIDE,Breathe. Their legacy low headlines invite ordinary-gift misinterpretation. PHC has a new tmp portfolio packet, but remains program-only in this indexed snapshot.
- G2 (23): clinical/access programs. Determine unrestricted marginal allocation and shared-provider/public financing; do not re-label targeted courses as whole gifts. Hearing center needs recipient clarification and PHC deduplication. This group is not a recommendation to redo all trials.
- G3 (6): housing,policy and legal unit models. Separate ordinary giving from specific advisory projects,turnover crews,cash packages or assisted households/cases.
- G4 (9): workforce,food,social and education units. Distinguish program/school accounting from organization gift scope; revise when decision-relevant,not to inflate completion counts.

Groups indicate scope-revision workflow,not expected-impact ranks. No estimate was recalculated or changed. Tmp accepted/pending packets are not treated as already indexed. In particular PHC's historical glasses model is not overwritten by its new draft in this inventory.

## Boundary caveats

“Program-only” is not a judgment that the program is ineffective or the organization inefficient. Some providers concentrate on the modeled activity, but a present explicit whole-gift allocation and complete other-cost treatment are still required before assigning the specific program result to ordinary general giving. Rebuilding Together's direct-resource budget,Five Keys' schoolwide total and Farming Hope's job-training expense retain their actual narrower scopes. Shared pathways (PHC/hearing center; PVF/Lions/CPMC; SFAF/DOPE/HYA/HRTC/public partners) must not become additive independent QALYs simply because organizations have separate report pages.
