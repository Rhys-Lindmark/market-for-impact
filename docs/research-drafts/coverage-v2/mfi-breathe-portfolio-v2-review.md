## What the missing share means

The v1 marginal allocation is a judgment ledger, not observed spending. Of its unmodeled 60%, only central management/fundraising 10% is an enabling cost already retained for the modeled services. CPAP 25% is a distinct clinical pathway and is now represented. Senior/COPD 10%, clean-air/youth 10%, and TB/other education 5% remain separate activities with unresolved incremental effects. They cannot be silently renamed overhead or assumed to have zero expected health. Adult asthma was additionally omitted inside the original 25% asthma allocation.

## Added causal calculations

CPAP: $25,000/$500 = 50 nominal complete equipment episodes; 0.5 financing additionality = 25 additional episodes. Both are unverified priors, including donor cash after any recipient fees. No equipment price claim is used as a full delivery cost. PREDICT randomized 278 adults aged at least 65 with symptomatic OSA to CPAP plus supportive care versus supportive care. Its [primary economic report](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK299286/) estimates one-year EQ-5D gain 0.005 QALY (95% CI −0.034 to 0.044); SF-6D gives 0.018 (0.003 to 0.034). The central uses EQ-5D, not selectively the favorable instrument. Both and signed CI cases are exposed. These are integrated one-year ITT effects, NOT annual utility to multiply over device life. They already contain trial adherence, survival and within-year comparator access. Relative transfer 0.8 is an explicit local service/population contrast prior, not an extra adherence probability. Discount only the 0.25-year implementation delay. No further within-year catch-up or mortality discount is stacked. No benefit after year one without another explicit funded treatment model. All source confidence intervals are trial uncertainty, not a confidence interval for the charity estimate.

[SAVE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27571048/) found no cardiovascular-event prevention in its established-CVD/minimally sleepy OSA population. We give no CPAP survival, stroke-prevention or accident credit. Aggregate trial QALYs include routine treatment burdens; no duplicate generic adverse-effect haircut. The signed outcome inputs allow worsening. Neither SAVE nor PREDICT proves effectiveness of any particular refurbished machine; clinical calibration and safe usable inventory remain prerequisites.

Adult asthma: existing nominal all-age episode count, existing financing additionality and existing public-provider outside cost remain unchanged. The 40% adult fraction is partitioned into 80% home-visit-equivalent and 20% workshop/other, explicit priors. [HomeBASE primary trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25419871/) randomized 366 low-income adults with uncontrolled asthma; about 4.9 home visits over one year yielded 2.02 more symptom-free days per fortnight versus usual care. Urgent care did not improve between groups. The calculator uses 2.02/14 × 0.5 local transfer × 0.1 symptom-day utility gap × at most one year, discounted at 3%. Utility mapping and the constant first-year symptom contrast are priors; endpoint symptom-free days are not empirically integrated QALYs. The transfer covers lower intensity and timing; no separate within-year catch-up haircut is added. Existing asthma shared harm applies to ALL added adult/child offers already; do not charge it twice.

CPAP and asthma benefits concern distinct symptom dimensions. Coincident patients are allowed, but no second survival reward is added and only small additive symptom improvements are modeled centrally. Extreme clinical effects are stress tests, not a valid joint patient utility-state forecast; no population-level extrapolation is claimed. Future full-person modeling would need overlap data and bounded joint utility states.

## Costs, geography and missing expectation

Whole gift stays $100,000. CPAP adds $400 per nominal episode for external clinical setup, real donated-equipment alternative use, patient time/shipping and supplies outside the $500 donor-funded episode. This is an unverified combined real-input prior, not retail donated value or free public care. Participant fees are financing transfers, not themselves additional resources; resource costs they finance belong in the outside envelope, not both donor cash and outside. No recipient-fee subsidy leverage is assumed. Every nominal episode retains outside costs even if financing displaces other funders. The two original pathways retain their original outside costs; adult asthma is not a second purchase. Resource figure remains a GROSS PARTIAL envelope: outside inputs for unresolved activities are missing, and no medical savings are netted away.

CPAP Bay/SF shares 0.8/0.08 are future-residence priors within the actual eligible service counties, not observed client counts. Adult asthma inherits independent 0.8/0.08 shares. US is the modeled overall geography; all new episodes are assumed US residents. A no-SF scenario correctly returns null local prices.

The calculator intentionally returns whole_organization_expected_qaly:null. Scenario central is a conditional clinical-subset estimate, not a probability-weighted organization expectation and not a strict health lower bound. No generic residual QALY/$, inverse-allocation uplift, or threshold fitting is used. Resolve marginal budgets, CPAP actual episodes/fees/stock and prior alternative access, adult service mix, overlapping recipient IDs, and education-to-completed-care effects before offering a whole-organization return. Further refinement of unresolved activities is not necessary to preserve this honest status.
