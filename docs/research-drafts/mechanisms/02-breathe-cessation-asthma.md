# Breathe California: cessation, with pediatric asthma comparison

Research 2026-09-07. All scenarios exploratory; no verified marginal quote.

## Service/entity and eligibility

https://lungsrus.org/quit-smoking-services/ and https://lungsrus.org/ash-kickers-smoking-cessation-program/ verify Ash Kickers: six adult group sessions, trained facilitators, quit planning and relapse support. Provider says >60% tobacco-free at six weeks; no denominator, comparator, biochemical validation or long-term follow-up published. DO NOT use60% incremental sustained cessation. https://lungsrus.org/san-francisco-county/ is predominantly a resource directory, not proof of a current SF Ash Kickers cohort. The entity is Breathe California of the Bay Area, Golden Gate, and Central Coast; do not confuse separately incorporated regional Breathe organizations. Exact current SF cessation schedule, paid capacity and restricted donation route remain unverified.

Asthma SF delivery is better verified: https://www.phi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/AHVP-Directory-by-County-8-2025-Update.pdf (RAMP/PHI directory dated8/5/2025, indexed SF row inspected) lists Breathe countywide, all ages with poorly controlled asthma and Anthem Blue Cross or Kaiser Medi-Cal coverage, providing self-management education, trigger assessment and remediation. https://lungsrus.org/asthma/ confirms initial visit plus2–3 follow-ups and equipment. DO NOT substitute a summer camp or school class for this home intervention.

## Cessation evidence and arithmetic

Primary randomized/economic trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3751449/?pdf=1 (2012 Txt2stop full relevant text read):58 additional six-month quitters/1,000 enrolled, with lifetime Markov gain29Q/1,000, therefore .5Q per incremental six-month quitter. Already includes21%relapse6–12months,30%lifetime relapse after12months, age distribution and3.5%discount. This is a UK text intervention, NOT Ash Kickers efficacy. Do not apply an extra generic relapse haircut unless explicitly changing the model's relapse assumption. Do not transfer low £16.12 text cost to a six-session group.

Primary counterfactual-rich Start2quit RCT economic analysis https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5873401/ : validated three-month prolonged abstinence measured at6months150/2635(5.7%) vs60/1748(3.4%). Short-run adjustedQ difference.002, CI−.001to.004; not immediate demonstrated largeQ. Its letters+taster+usual services differ from Ash Kickers, but establish plausible low-single-digit incremental quit rates.

Model unit: one enrolled SF adult offered an entire six-session program, INCLUDING noncompleters. Q = incremental6monthquit probability × modeledQ/6monthquitter × population/clinical transfer × funding additionality. No separate attendance multiplier if increment is ITT per enrollee.

## Pediatric asthma comparison

Primary matching trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2810206/ (2009):309 low-income children3–13, all received nurse education; adding homeCHW yielded24.4additional symptom-free days/year (0.94days/2weeks, CI.02–1.86), caregiver asthmaQoL+.22 scale points; urgent-use difference nonsignificant. Caregiver scale is NOT preference-basedQ. Another primary trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1449237/ found nonsignificant between-arm symptom-day effect (p=.138) and historical additional cost$1,124/child for high-intensity7visits vs1visit. Breathe's3–4visits different dose. More recent https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapediatrics/fullarticle/2825197 (2024,626children) found asthma-control improvement in all arms with no between-group differences. This is important disconfirmation.

Transparent health-only asthma bridge: Q = incremental symptom-free days/365 × assumed utility difference symptomatic-vs-free day × duration-in-years × transfer × fundingadditionality. Utility difference .10 is a JUDGMENT, not conversion of the asthmaQoLscale and not measured trialQ. Public funding limits donation leverage. https://www.hpsm.org/provider/hpsm-provider-news/healthmatters-md/healthmattersmd/2024/09/05/breathe-california-is-hpsm-s-new-asthma-program-partner verifies contracted CalAIM/Medi-Cal education and remediation in SAN MATEO (not itself SF proof). RAMP verifies SF payer eligibility. Need distinguish unpaid navigation reaching eligible families from services already reimbursable. A donor should not buy insurer-funded equipment twice.

Bottom line: cessation can cross threshold only with a genuinely additional low-cost SF delivery offer; current SF cohort remains unverified. Asthma home services have stronger local availability evidence but weaker health-only price under cautious assumptions; pursue uncovered high-severity families only with payer/claim ledger, dose and preference-based outcomes. Under5 eligibility exists from age3 in the cited RCT, not evidence in infants.
