# SFFC broader vaccine opportunity screen

2026-09-07 follow-up to 01-sffc-vaccination.md. The healthy-adult acute-flu baseline is NOT an adequate verdict on targeted vaccination. Recommended next hypothesis: an explicitly proposed shingles-vaccine patient-assistance/administration pathway for eligible uninsured older patients; retain high-risk flu as a serious alternative requiring age/risk-specific model inputs.

## Local delivery boundary

[SFFC primary care](https://sffc.org/primary-care) verifies vaccinations generically. [SFFC educational sample progress note](https://sffc.org/samplenote) includes flu, shingles, HepB and pneumococcal histories. This is a SAMPLE NOTE, not an actual patient record, current inventory, or proof those products are administered locally. Do not use it as a delivered-dose source. Current SFFC vaccine menu, patient ages, current PAP participation and unmet series completions remain unknown. Shingles pathway is a proposed expansion, not an established local service.

## Strongest inspectable donor hypothesis: shingles PAP throughput

[Current GSKPAF vaccine page](https://gskpaf.org/gsk/vaccines-patient-assistance/) includes SHINGRIX and ENGERIX-B. Adults without third-party vaccine coverage can apply through a registered prescriber, subject to income rules; Medicare patients are ineligible. Current webpage lists single-person income maximum $47,880; confirm at application because rules change.

[Currently linked application, April2024](https://gskpaf.org/content/dam/brs-pharma-us/gskpaf-v2/en_US/pdfs/GSK-PAP-Vaccine-English.pdf) establishes a specific operational gap: REPLENISHMENT, not free inventory up front. Clinic uses purchased vaccine for approved patients, then must accumulate 10 approved doses within 12 months or forfeit replenishment; limit 200 doses/product/site/year. Vaccine must be free to patient. GSK does not reimburse administration, and provider agrees not to seek public-payer administration reimbursement. Thus administration, applications, recall and initial inventory financing are possible philanthropic functions. No evidence SFFC lacks these resources or can add this throughput yet.

[2026 manufacturer price](https://gskforyou.com/gsk-pricing-information/shingrix/) $234.69/dose, $469.38/two doses before administration. Actual acquisition can differ. The English January2026 price is current; Spanish text on same page appears stale/lower. Never label list price a provider quote. Donated replacement vaccine still consumes real resources; donor cash and full-resource costs must be reported separately. Initial inventory needs working capital; final residual inventory, expiration and uncompleted batches cannot be silently treated as reimbursed cash. PAP cap corresponds at most to 100 two-dose series annually per site, before partial courses and wastage.

### Source-based health bridge

[Primary independent decision model, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6602903/) inspected full relevant tables by direct fetch: Table2 estimates discounted net lifetime QALY gain per subunit-vaccine initiation versus no vaccine .001220 at age50, .003052 at60, .005392 at70. US immunocompetent population; model to age100, 3% annual discount; shingles/PHN/ocular complications, admissions/death and vaccine adverse effects included. Trial efficacy input uses total vaccinated cohort and already incorporates 95.5% second-dose adherence. Do not multiply a second 95.5% factor or add duplicate mortality/pain effects. Waning originally assumed similar to live vaccine; not new long-term empirical follow-up. Historical acquisition/cost offsets in article are NOT current donor costs. This is modeled lifetime health, not QALYs directly observed in an RCT.

### Transparent conditional scenarios

Unit one initiated series for an otherwise unvaccinated eligible adult. Q = published age-specific net Q × local clinical/completion transfer × counterfactual/funding additionality. Clinical factor is relative to trial dose performance, not a second raw completion rate. Additionality covers both alternative free access and later vaccination; use a timing model if only acceleration rather than permanent omission is established. No separate harm subtraction for effects already included, but unmodeled extra harm can be signed.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Favorable | $20 | .005392 (age70) | .9 | .9 | .00436752 | $45,793 |
| Central | $50 | .005392 (age70) | .75 | .5 | .002022 | $247,280 |
| Unfavorable | $600 | .003052 (age60) | .5 | .25 | .0003815 | $15,727,392 |

Every local price/factor is JUDGMENT, not an offer. Central $50 cash assumes donated clinician capacity, shared clinic overhead, successful PAP replenishment and modest paid applications/recall/admin; favorable $20 requires near-marginal shared visits and very low additional paperwork. Neither cost includes buying a fresh unreplenished series. Unfavorable $600 approximates two current list doses plus delivery. For central health assumptions, donor cost must be below $20.22/initiated series to pass $100K/10Q. At favorable assumptions, below $43.68. If most eligible uninsured patients are60 not70, recalculate; age70 PAP eligibility and adequate cohort size cannot be presumed.

Null: no incremental vaccination beyond usual care, no eligible untreated patients, full existing clinic/PAP capacity, or no clinical gain. Harm: adverse reactions exceeding modeled benefit; retain signed Q. Do not use an already fully vaccinated person, a referral or successful application as a new protected series. This hypothesis is potentially attractive only in donor-cost perspective with verified supply leverage and a small genuinely marginal cost. Full-resource performance may not pass.

## High-risk influenza: serious counterweight to acute-only baseline

[DeLuca etal2023 primary economic-study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37291022/) includes severe/permanent outcomes and high-risk strata, vaccination versus no vaccination. High-risk adults≥50 modeled cost-saving; healthy18–49 $194K/Q; other groups<$95K/Q. These are NET SOCIETAL/HEALTHCARE ICERs, not donor$/Q. Full outcome/input tables could not be inspected (publisher403); cannot infer Q per dose by dividing the clinic acquisition cost by these ICERs. Obtain full age/risk-specific hospitalization/death probabilities, vaccine effects, discounted survival/utility, and net-vs-gross costs before making a local severe-flu central model. This is more relevant to older chronic-disease SFFC patients than the healthy-adult baseline.

[IAMI2021 primary trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34459211/), [inspected primary manuscript](https://cci-cic.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/IAMI.pdf): 2,571 randomized; 99.7% immediately after MI, not stable outpatient cardiovascular risk. Vaccine/placebo one-year all-cause death2.9%/4.9%, HR.59 CI.39–.89; primary death/MI/stent composite5.3%/7.2%. Trial stopped early because pandemic. This supports severe health potential but NOT a 2pp death reduction for any older/diabetic clinic patient. No invented remaining lifetime Q per death. Immediate inpatient delivery may already be financed; SFFC post-discharge gap and matching treatment window unverified.

[IVVE2022 primary trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36400089/): heart-failure placebo-controlled vaccine trial found no significant overall mortality/co-primary cardiovascular benefit; all-cause hospitalization HR.84 and pneumoniaHR.58 improved. It prevents treating IAMI as universal cardiovascular vaccine effect.

[DANFLU2 primary author abstract](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/publications/high-dose-influenza-vaccine-effectiveness-against-hospitalization/):332,438 randomized, high-dose versus STANDARD vaccine, not none. Flu/pneumonia admission .68%/.73%,p=.14; influenza-specific .06%/.11%; all-cause death .67%/.66%. These are annual seasonal effects. Do not add its effect to a vaccine-versus-none model or claim a demonstrated mortality gain from buying higher-dose product. It argues for access to indicated vaccination rather than assuming premium dose alone is a major donor opportunity.

## HPV and hepatitisB alternatives

[Primary Chesson2020 HPV transmission model](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/131723/cdc_131723_DS1.pdf): whole established adolescent/young-adult program <$10K/Q versus no vaccination; expansion through45 roughly$653,300/Q. Current catch-up clinic opportunity is NOT equivalent to starting the entire national program before exposure, and models include herd effects. SFFC young/previously unvaccinated population, product supply and dose completion unknown. Do not select a low national average then assign all benefits to adult catch-up. Remains a plausible younger-patient audit, not the best currently inspectable local model.

[Hall2022 primary adultHBV vaccination model](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35260904/): universal adult expansion3dose$152,722/Q;2dose$155,429/Q versus risk-based baseline. [CDC primary workgroup evidence](https://www.cdc.gov/acip/evidence-to-recommendations/hepb-adults-etr.html) identifies very different high-risk subgroups. SFFC opportunity would require susceptible (not chronically infected, previously vaccinated or immune), high-incidence adults, completed vaccination and incremental PAP access. ENGERIX-B is in current GSKPAP, but adult immigrant HBV burden often reflects existing chronic infection—vaccination cannot cure it. Separate from NEMS diagnosed-CHB retention model and perinatal prophylaxis. No defensible local incidence/eligible denominator yet; do not use universal ICER or chronic prevalence to imply high acquisition risk.

## Recommendation / next decision

First clarify SFFC vaccine inventory and existing assistance program, number of eligible uninsured adults by age, series completion, free alternatives, and cost of increasing PAP batches. If a near-$20–$40 truly incremental age70 series is feasible, shingles PAP administration deserves a conditional research flag. Simultaneously acquire DeLuca severe-flu tables: high-risk flu may be stronger for actual patients but cannot be ranked from net societal cost-saving alone. No verified funding offer for either. This screening changes the provisional recommendation from healthy-adult flu-only to a targeted assisted-vaccine access inquiry, not an assertion that SFFC already delivers the proposed program.
