# NEMS Hep B Moms and Wu Yee home visiting

Accessed 2026-09-07. All monetary/QALY estimates below are analyst judgments to answer the donor decision, not measured program ICERs. Restricted SF grant capacity and quoted marginal budgets have not been confirmed. Clinical service decisions belong to clinicians; this is a donation model.

## NEMS Hep B Moms

**Central best guess $500,000 per 10 incremental QALYs**. Model cost per newly offered mother–infant care-management episode c=$500; a=.5 truly additional episode fraction after overlap with funded NEMS/public/hospital care; d=.005 infant infections averted per truly incremental episode; L=4 discounted lifetime QALYs per infection averted. Q per paid episode=a×d×L=.01; cost per10=10c/Q=$500k. d decomposes to 20% receiving a clinically meaningful correction who otherwise would miss it ×2.5-percentage-point absolute transmission-risk improvement in that corrected subgroup. Both components and L are priors, not NEMS measured inputs. L already averages chronicity/prognosis; do NOT add a separate 90% chronicity multiplier or credit whole infant life.

Optimistic c=$250,a=.9,d=.03,L=8=>.216 QALY/episode,$11,574/10. Pessimistic c=$1,000,a=.2,d=.0005,L=2=>.0002,$50m/10. Null if all enrolled families already receive effective prevention or donation replaces existing care-management money. Harm scenario: at central values subtract >.01 QALY/paid episode for inappropriate care changes/burden to cross net zero; no evidence that NEMS causes such harm, but more intervention is not logically always beneficial. Central <$100k/10 requires a×d×L>.05, or d>.025 at central a,L; central d=.005 misses threshold fivefold. Maternal antiviral treatment benefits could add value only if genuinely additional long-term care is purchased, modeled separately without double counting adult HBV programs.

Actual program: NEMS' Liver Health Program states screening in all pregnancies and Hep B Moms care management covering appropriate treatment, monitoring and infant immunization: https://www.nems.org/programs/liverhealth/ . This is a real local clinical pathway, though NEMS also serves outside SF, so require SF mother–infant allocation. Donation page https://www.nems.org/support-us/ identifies nonprofit support and directs checks to **North East Medical Services Foundation**, mailed to its Daly City office; general care is the stated destination. Do not conflate foundation gift with an already offered Hep B Moms restriction or infer all NEMS patients are SF residents.

Primary clinical basis: Jourdain et al., NEJM 2018, original trial DOI **10.1056/NEJMoa1708131**, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5895092/ ; author reply confirming interpretation https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8628567/ . Thai randomized double-blind trial with prompt infant prophylaxis found 0/147 vs3/147 transmissions, statistically nonsignificant; maternal tenofovir increment atop strong prophylaxis is not the entire untreated transmission risk. High-viremia enrollment and Thailand limit direct SF transfer. CDC 2025 perinatal clinical guidance estimates 90% chronicity after perinatal infection and requires linkage to public prevention case-management: https://cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/perinatal-provider-overview/clinical-testing-guidelines.html . This supports consequence severity and substantial overlap, not d. CDC vaccine efficacy page (2025-08-27) reports combined HBIG/vaccine efficacy ~94% versus untreated exposure: https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/perinatal-provider-overview/vaccine-administration.html . Applying 94% to every NEMS referral would grossly overstate incremental benefit.

Local payer/counterfactual: California PHBPP explicitly includes case management, prenatal screening and hospital coordination: https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CID/DCDC/Pages/Immunization/Perinatal.aspx . Routine clinical services can be insurance-funded, NEMS already screens, and hospitals deliver newborn prophylaxis. Highest plausible charitable margin is language/transport/lost-follow-up retrieval for currently missed families, not purchasing all existing HBV care. Need SF annual HBsAg-positive births, timely prophylaxis/series/PVST completion, high-viremia antiviral uptake, actual unserved queue, additional case-manager cost and whether eligible costs already reimbursable. Completion/PVST are process metrics, not infections prevented. No outreach undertaken.

## Wu Yee Children's Services — home visiting add-on supplies

**Central best guess $857,143 per 10 incremental QALYs** for an incremental $300 targeted health/basic-supply bundle delivered through existing home visitors. Formula Q=a×h=.7 additional share ×.005 discounted child-plus-caregiver health QALY per genuinely additional bundle=.0035. Cost/10=10×300/.0035. h equals roughly 1.8 fully healthy day equivalents shared across child/caregiver from minor morbidity relief and stress relief; entirely judgmental, not a claim of clinical measurement. Developmental scores, parenting confidence and books do not automatically become QALYs.

Optimistic c=$100,a=.9,h=.03=>.027 QALY,$37,037/10. Pessimistic c=$500,a=.2,h=.0005=>.0001 QALY,$50m/10. Null if families would obtain supplies anyway, items are not used or benefits are entirely nonhealth. Harm if items impose burdens, substitute better support or inappropriate equipment causes injury: h=-.001 at central a gives -.0007 QALY/bundle. Target requires a×h>.03 at c=$300, hence h>.0429 at a=.7; central h=.005 misses by ~8.6x. A serious-injury prevention-only bundle might have rare large benefits, but no Wu Yee attributable injury incidence reduction establishes that upside.

Why this donation unit: the primary 2026-04-22 interview provides concrete marginal scope: **110 participants including around20 expecting families; 11 bilingual home visitors; 90-minute weekly visits**; donations can buy basics constrained by existing funding including nursing supplies, carriers, toothbrushes, cream, locks and plug protectors. Source https://wuyee.org/insights/building-strong-foundations-home-visiting-at-wu-yee-childrens-services/ . Quoted output counts and supply need are program-manager self-report, not outcomes evaluation. Regular service page identifies **Early Head Start**, birth–36 months, free visits and family groups: https://wuyee.org/for-families/home-visiting-services/ . Direct nonprofit donation route https://wuyee.org/donate/ . No separately priced bundle or guaranteed restriction is published; $300 is the analyst's proposed unit, not a Wu Yee solicitation price.

Do not equate Wu Yee with Nurse-Family Partnership or import nurse-home-visiting trial mortality/maltreatment effects. Federal EHS evaluation randomized 3,001 families at17 sites and found early developmental/parenting benefits with narrower persistence by fifth grade: https://headstart.gov/programs/article/early-head-start-benefits-children-families . Those outcomes are not QALYs and full EHS-vs-control is not the effect of extra supplies atop an existing home visit. Core visits are publicly funded EHS; charitable gifts may purchase small extras and not create additional slots. Donor-useful next evidence is itemized unmet needs, donated replacements already available, observed use and costs, injury/skin/dental or parent mental-health endpoints rather than beneficiary counts. Existing 110 is a ceiling context, not 110 extra families per gift. No outreach undertaken.
