## Causal evidence, and why no prescription-to-QALY coefficient exists here

[CLEAN Meds RCT, Persaud et al., May21,2021](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pmed.1003590), DOI10.1371/journal.pmed.1003590:786 Ontario primary-care patients reporting cost-related nonadherence; free essential medicines versus usual access for two years. All-appropriate-medication adherence was38.7% versus28.6% (difference10.1pp, CI3.3–16.9). No statistically significant improvements in measured diabetes, blood pressure or LDL endpoints at two years. It was not a donated-stock trial, not SF, and no preference-based QALY endpoint is used here. Its adherence outcome is binary across all prescribed medicines: **.101 is not .101 medication-covered person-years**, and dividing a future health benefit by this endpoint would require a causal mediation model. Healthcare-payer savings do not refund the donor.

## Fully labeled conditional planning model

Unit: two-year delivery-support episode for one SF patient with documented affordability-related gaps and clinically indicated usable donated medicines. Cost includes donor-side logistics plus downstream dispensing/coordination to avoid pretending SIRUM transport alone completes treatment. Donated drug purchase value is excluded; report the in-kind opportunity cost separately, not inflated list price.

`Q = expected net incremental preference utility from the actual medication mix × equivalent benefit years within two years × funding additionality`

`USD/10Q = 10 × episode cost / Q`

| Null | positive | 0 or no additional access | — | — | 0 | No finite positive ratio |

**All numeric scenario inputs are judgments, not SIRUM quotes or CLEAN Meds effect estimates.** Central$240 allowance is$60 collection/logistics+$120 pharmacist/technician allocation+$60 follow-up across two years. It needs a time-and-motion check; unpaid labor isn't literally free. Central.005 utility is a modest expected symptomatic-health allowance given unknown mix and mixed surrogate evidence; a single equivalent year allows partial/late coverage. It already embeds nonadherence, usable stock, disease mix, transfer and adverse drug effects; do not multiply these again or imply .005 is experimentally estimated. This simple sketch is less defensible than a diagnosis-specific model and must retain that label.

At central Q, episode cost must be below **$25** to beat$100K/10Q; with$240 cost, one benefit year and.5additionality, expected utility must exceed **.048**. A lower upstream donor cost can cross the threshold only if downstream dispensing is genuinely secured and additional; publish full-resource and donor-only views side by side.

## What would make this a decision-ready bet

Get SF-specific accepted usable drugs, expiry/wastage, prescriptions actually dispensed, patients' baseline gaps, incremental coverage over insurance/other free assistance, and clinical monitoring. Select a narrow class/condition before claiming future complications avoided. Longer-term statin benefits, immediate symptomatic medicine benefits, and rare lifesaving treatments cannot be averaged without a documented mix. Null/harm if donated stock substitutes for existing procurement, does not match prescriptions, expires, fragments supply or introduces medication errors. No verified restricted SF funding gap or expandable pharmacy-hours offer. A volunteering request demonstrates demand for skills, not automatically unmet philanthropic cash need.
