## Evidence versus judgments

- [Current PHC donation page](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/donate/) says$100 provides two pairs, a **fundraising benchmark** not audited marginal accounting. Central$100 per completed dispense doubles that benchmark for navigation/overhead, including unsuccessful referral effort allocated to completed recipients. It excludes donated professional and insured resources: **donor-only**, not full resource cost.
- [Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/) offers an annual prescription pair at monthly Optical Day with a valid prescription; screening and dispensation are not equivalent. Repairs/reading glasses are distinct services. [Historical February2025 Optical Day](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/events/optical-day-26/) did not itself provide eye examinations.
- [Griffiths et al.2014 Zambia paper](https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/1604317/1/1478-7547-12-6.pdf) provides an uncontrolled six-month EQ-5D improvement .850→.925 among41 spectacle recipients at follow-up. Central.0375 halves the.075 change for confounding and severity/population transfer; this is subjective shrinkage, not an estimated causal adjustment. Do not describe all refractive error as blindness.
- [SF free-clinic follow-up study, Hennein et al.2021](https://www.thieme-connect.com/products/ejournals/pdf/10.1055/s-0041-1726288.pdf):40referred/14presented is referral attendance, not sustained glasses wear. Do not multiply by14/40 again when cost already allocates failed referrals.
- .75effective years assumes loss/breakage/nonuse within one year; .5additionality jointly addresses substitute funding and otherwise obtainable correction. [Medi-Cal vision benefits](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/providers-partners/pharmacy-benefits-division-and-vision-care-program/medi-cal-vision-benefits/) establish a counterfactual coverage route, not guaranteed access.
