These are historical visits/referrals, **not incremental completions**. Neither$14,000/48 nor32/48 is a causal cost-effectiveness estimate.

[Current campaigns page](https://allmaysee.org/campaigns) retains the shelter project and equipment/operations giving pathway. [UCSF's current clinic feature](https://ophthalmology.ucsf.edu/ucsf-eye-clinic-brings-vision-care-to-people-experiencing-homelessness/) independently confirms the shelter service. The2024request being online does **not** establish that it remains unfunded in2026.

## Bounded counterfactual model

Unit: a hypothetical$14,000 **incremental clinic-support package** producing completed new correction within a maximum two-year delivery window. Judge resulting completed episodes directly, including failed referral effort; do not infer all referrals get glasses. Add any induced downstream costs not already secured separately—current donor-only central excludes partner/in-kind clinical resources.
