## Primary utility evidence

[Kaur et al., December1,2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-020-05977-x), DOI10.1186/s12913-020-05977-x: Singapore community-center delayed-start study of previously unaided adults with≥40dB loss, mean age69. Community centers randomized; **439 eligible/willing,12 excluded for missing cost/utility,427 analyzed (264fitted/163waiting)**. Reported three-month HUI3 utility difference .12, **not .12 three-month QALY**. Fitting included rehabilitation at2weeks,1month and3months. Waiting-group HUI3 scoring was modified; this and short, unblinded follow-up limit causal certainty. One-year follow-up responded66%;71.4% of respondents reported use—not71.4%ofallfitted. Five-year persistence in published CEA is extrapolated, not measured.

[Joore et al.2007 instrument comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2039846/), PMID17647093:70 fitting follow-ups showed HUI3+.12 versus EQ-5D approximately0–.01, uncontrolled. Sensory-sensitive versus generic-instrument choice materially changes rankings. [China randomized CEA2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37306959/) reports .017QALY but its annualization should be checked before using it as a coefficient. Do not import cochlear-implant utility or claim dementia/mortality prevention.

## Decision tests and portfolio accounting

Request a priced extra fitting-or-battery-support tranche, confirmed in-kind device availability, audiology capacity, prior coverage and device use, and three-/six-/twelve-month use/preferences. A supplies-only marginal model can be much cheaper but needs **additional sustained use over already-owned aids**, not the entire first-fitting effect. Do not credit PHC and the Center each with the same full gain; allocate joint navigation/clinical costs and beneficiaries. No verified unfilled financial gap. Child hearing has different developmental/coverage pathways and is outside this adult model.
