## Effect evidence and limits

[2019 SF one-visit pilot](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/19424396.2019.12220799), DOI10.1080/19424396.2019.12220799, primary search-index text: CitySmilesConnect was aSFDS/PHC partnership; eight patients were invited for evaluation along with providers/administrators. Full PDF failed retrieval, so do not invent completion/retention or improvement magnitudes. Oral-health impact measures are not generic QALYs.

[Kawai et al.2005 RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16139697/), DOI10.1016/j.jdent.2005.01.005, compares traditional/simplified dentures in122edentulous adults age45–75 with satisfaction/function follow-up at3/6months. Both arms receive dentures, so it cannot identify dentures-versus-no-treatment health gain. [Mini versus conventional implant pilot](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5310054/) reports generic EQ-5D/SF-6D analyses but compares implant retention techniques—not this intervention; it must not be repurposed as evidence that conventional free dentures cause a particular QALY increment. No defensible empirically measured .03 utility coefficient was found in this bounded pass.
