## Evidence and disconfirmation

[Bessone et al.2021, The Economic Consequences of Increasing Sleep Among the Urban Poor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8242594/):452Chennai adults in a one-month paid-work field experiment. Night-sleep interventions added27minutes without broad short-run benefits; half-hour nap opportunities yielded about14minutes daytime sleep and improved some wellbeing/cognitive outcomes, while partly displacing night sleep. It did **not** measureQALYs, and its healthy-worker/private-nap setting is unlike homelessness/sanctuary. It demonstrates why extra timeinbed cannot be assumed to equal restorative sleep or health improvement.

[2017 JAMA InternalMedicine homeless-sleep survey](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2594280) documents sleep problems, not the causal effect of providing a church cot. Medical-respite and permanent-housing QALYs are **not transferable** to a nonclinical daytime nap space. Longitudinal associations between short sleep and mortality do not license a mortality-averted estimate here.

## Explicit service-day model

Unit: one additional occupied daytime-rest opportunity, counting at most one per individual per calendar day. Formula:

`Q = expected net preference-utility gain on that calendar day / 365 × funding additionality`

`USD/10Q = 10 × donor cost per occupied service-day / Q`

**All scenario inputs are judgments.** Central allowance could correspond to$900extra-day cost divided by60occupied opportunities:2staff×8hours×$35loaded=$560 plus$340facility/cleaning/admin/supplies. These are hypothetical staffing and occupancy assumptions, not Gubbio accounting or a staffing recommendation. The.03 expected day utility is a modest subjective allowance for rest/fatigue/anxiety relief, already incorporating probability/quality ofsleep, displacement of other rest and clinical transfer. It is not derived from a wellbeing zscore or minutes ofsleep. No additional sleep-hour, response or use factor should multiply it. Fundingadditionality separately addresses replacement of existing supported days.

No benefit after that calendar day is credited centrally; do not multiply by a year of improved sleep or claim that hours asleep equal full-health hours. Repeat service-days can accrue repeat modest utility but cannot exceed one daily increment per person. The model excludes clinical treatment, naloxone rescue, housing and mortality effects to prevent overlap with providers operating onsite.

At centralcost/additionality, required day utility is **1.095** to beat$100K/10Q—larger than a full0to1health change. At centralQ, cost must be below **$0.41096 per service-day**. This makes the strict health-only threshold difficult even before evidentiary uncertainty; sanctuary may still have substantial non-QALY value.
