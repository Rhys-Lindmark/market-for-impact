## Primary clinical evidence and transfer limits

[Little et al.2015 PRIMIT randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26256072/), DOI10.1016/S0140-6736(15)60127-1:20,066English adults,16week follow-up completed by84%; tailored internet handwashing promotion reduced the proportion reporting≥1RTI from59%to51% (adjustedRR.86,CI.83–.89). It was behavior change with existing household facilities, **not a bag of toiletries**; outcome is people withanyRTI, not episode counts. Minor skin irritation increased. Do not use the commentaryDOI displayed elsewhere inPubMed as thetrialDOI.

[Sandora et al.2005 household RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16140697/):292families with childcare-age children, sanitizer plus education forfive months reduced secondaryGIillness (IRR.41) but not respiratory illness (IRR.97,CI.72–1.30). [2010 crowded-urban-household randomized trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2821845/) likewise found no significant multivariate infection-rate differences across education/sanitizer/mask groups. These conflict with a universal large kit effect. NoSFkit randomized clinical or preference-QALY outcome found. Do not import intensive hospital hand-hygiene/MRSA mortality models or low-income-country diarrheaDALYs as SFQALYs.

## Explicit judgmental infection pathway

Unit:one completed one-month supply episode, not a quarterly shipment automatically lastingthree months. Include downstream distribution/education; facility/water supply must be genuinely available. Formula:

`Q = baseline relevant infection episodes/person-month × incremental relative reduction × symptomatic days/episode /365 × symptom-state utility loss × funding additionality`

`USD/10Q = 10 × episode cost / Q`

Central$20 doubles it for storage, sorting, delivery and partner contact; not an audited quote and excludes donated product opportunity cost. Favorablehighincidence/effect/severity are demanding joint assumptions. No mortality, transmission multiplier, dental treatment, housing or employment bonus.

## Payer/counterfactual/funding room

Privately funded supply does not imply100%additionality: receiving partners may already buy or receive products, and donors may replace an existing budget. The schoolwaitlist claim on the organization's social profile is not a verified SF capacity quote and not used as funding-room evidence. Need exact SFreceiving-site product shortage, incrementalrecipient-months, productuse, facilityaccess, deliverycosts and clinical comparison. Hygiene supplies accompanyingSFCCCvisits must not each receive the fulloutreachclinical benefit. Null if products replace existing supply, are unusable without facilities, do not change hygiene or do not prevent infection. Irritation/unsafe products/waste can harm; quality guidance rejects opened, expired or irritating products. No verified current restricted expansion amount or unit-cost offer.
