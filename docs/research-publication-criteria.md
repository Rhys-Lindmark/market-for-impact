Current checkpoint: 68 unique published organizations, verified at Sites v173 on September 10, 2026. Target: 115. The 49 category slots and two permitted overlaps remain specified in sf-progress.md.

## Publication criteria

A completed research report may conclude accept, hold, or reject for donation. An estimate above $100,000 per 10 QALYs is not a reason to withhold an otherwise sound report. The threshold guides candidate selection before research; findings determine the conclusion afterward. Publication acceptance concerns evidence, scope, calculations and clear presentation, not an attractive result.

A screen does not count. Each counted report needs a distinct organization, a substantive account of its activities and major potential benefits, inspected sources with dates, a versioned inspectable model, costs and attribution for an ordinary gift, explicit uncertainty and material omissions, local beneficiary geography, funding-room assessment, and independent review. Unknowns must remain visible. A model skeleton alone does not count as a finished quantitative report.

Do not multiply historical annual outputs by gift/annual expense and call that marginal gift impact without an explicit additionality and capacity argument. Do not equate absent SF-residence data with an observed zero. Cash cost, public reimbursement, donated resources and associated implementation costs need separate justified boundaries. Negative or null scenarios must remain possible where supported.
