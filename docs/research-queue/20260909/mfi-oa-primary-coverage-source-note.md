Historical 1994-2008 data, not current case mix. Table1: 4,201 interventions; 1,103 evaluations without procedures, 1,218 non-OR procedures, 1,880 OR procedures. OR general surgery1,204; within that hernia646, cyst/lipoma270, anorectal140, cholecystectomy80, breast68. Other OR: orthopaedic154, gynecology131, ENT122, ophthalmology121, urology66, vascular60, plastic10, dermatology7, other5.

Table4: 2008 median referral-to-first-appointment68days, operation83days; means78/98. These are actual treated waits, NOT counterfactual waits avoided. Follow-up surveys6-10weeks used yes/no improvement questions, not EQ5D utilities. 96% reporting better quality of life cannot be converted to .96QALY. Waived-charge leverage is not resource opportunity cost.

Model implication: named omitted surgical pathways warrant explicit priors; a three-procedure denominator is incomplete. Use current2025 specialty totals where available, historical within-specialty mix only as an explicitly weak sensitivity. No new estimate is authorized by this descriptive study alone.
