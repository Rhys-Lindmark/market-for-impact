# Five specialist prevention candidates — bounded discovery screen

Screen date: 2026-09-08 local. Research only; no publication or report-count increment.

## Decision

Rank **SIRUM first for a targeted evidence follow-up, not yet a full priced report**. It has an actual Bay dispensing partnership and a plausibly cheap coordination mechanism. None of the five yet survives the stronger gate of a credible ordinary-gift, Bay-resident sub-$100,000/10-QALY estimate. Do not turn absent drug mix, marginal capacity, or local allocation into favorable defaults. A national SIRUM report could be substantially more promising than a Bay-only headline.

Deduplication: searched the latest accessible `work/market-for-impact-via-heart/data` and its research backlog. None of these five has a report there. SIRUM is already an unreviewed discovery lead, so is new to the report cohort, not newly discovered by this screen. Excluded existing naloxone-chain reports, Via, BACC, broad medical centers, and the parallel maternal/child screen. No numerical policy-passage pathway used.

## Ranked candidates

### 1. SIRUM — redistribution of clinically useful surplus medicine

Exact lead: Supporting Initiatives to Redistribute Unused Medicine–SIRUM, EIN 27-1103057. The [California Pharmacy Board](https://www.pharmacy.ca.gov/online/surplus_med_intermediary.shtml) lists its intermediary license. Actual Bay delivery is corroborated by [Santa Clara County, February 20, 2024](https://news.santaclaracounty.gov/innovative-county-program-provides-residents-free-prescription-medications): SIRUM connects more than 150 institutional donors to the county's Better Health Pharmacy; county staff inspect and dispense. This is not a SIRUM-owned free-standing clinical intervention.

The current [fundraising page](https://sirum.org/support-our-work/) says $1 sends one prescription to a partner. It does not specify quantity, treatment duration, full organization costs, marginality, or final dispensing. Its [2024 impact section](https://sirum.org/impact/) reports 250,000+ patients reached by partners, not additional adherent patient-years attributable to SIRUM. Current linked partners include public and charitable pharmacies outside California. The separate Good Pill $6/90-day patient fee is neither SIRUM's complete cost nor evidence of California eligibility.

Why potentially strong: off-patent cardiovascular drugs can have large benefits in high-risk untreated patients, and software/logistics can mobilize otherwise wasted stock. The [MI FREEE randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22080794/) supports an access mechanism but constrains transfer: adherence rose 4–6 points; the primary event/revascularization outcome was not significantly improved. A secondary first-major-vascular-event rate fell from 12.8 to 11.0 per 100 person-years. Do not apply a full drug-efficacy effect to every donated prescription, or add multiple drug effects on the same person.

Cheap break-even, explicitly illustrative rather than an estimate: a $100,000 gift, 50% health-bearing logistics allocation, $1/dispensed-equivalent prescription, 12 prescriptions per patient-year, 50% additionality and 20% high-risk trial fit yields 417 relevant additional patient-years. At a hypothetical 0.054 finite QALY per year, that is 22.5 QALYs nationally. The 0.054 is a speculative 0.018 secondary event-rate difference times 3 finite QALYs/event, not trial-measured QALYs. Bay needs >44% of this health to reach 10 QALYs; a 2% Bay share yields 0.45. Null and negative effects remain possible. Prescription units and trial fit could each invalidate this entire calculation.

Resource/baseline gate: [county December 2023 reporting](https://publichealth.santaclaracounty.gov/news/news/these-programs-put-unused-prescription-drugs-hands-patients-need) describes roughly 40,000 prescriptions/year and just over $1 million annual pharmacy operations, about $25 per prescription before recognizing differences in fill duration. This public resource is not free, and existing stock, Medi-Cal, generics, patient payment, or other donors may replace SIRUM supply. Do not equate drug retail valuation with real resource cost or health. [FY2024 IRS extraction](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/271103057) reports $32,354,215 total expense and $29,049,476 revenue; the primary return's cash/in-kind reconciliation has not been extracted in this bounded screen and no unit-cost ratio is inferred from it.

Next cheap disconfirmation: reconcile the primary 990's in-kind flows and ordinary cash expenses; obtain documented actual fills by drug, quantity and geography, deduplicated annual patients, and public dispensing capacity. Confirm whether additional cash buys incremental high-risk completed courses rather than donated inventory already awaiting funding elsewhere. SF residence is unverified; Santa Clara service establishes Bay presence, not Bay share. **Best next lead, conditional hold before authoring.**

### 2. Harm Reduction Coalition of Santa Cruz County — last-mile overdose prevention / treatment navigation

The [current official site](https://www.hrcofscc.org/) verifies material distribution, street outreach and overdose education; its [contact page](https://www.hrcofscc.org/contact) gives a MAT navigation channel. It is distinct from National Harm Reduction Coalition and from the [county-operated program](https://santacruzhealth.org/PublicHealth/InfectiousDisease/CAReTeamIntegratedServices/SafeUseandOverdosePreventionProgram.aspx). The EIN lead 84-5048993 needs current primary filing/status verification before any recipient-level report.

Why potentially strong: specialist last-mile access to high-risk people can cheaply increase availability at a lethal event, or treatment retention. At a strictly illustrative 10 finite QALYs per net additional survivor, one net additional death prevented per $100,000 clears the overall threshold. That is a break-even condition, not evidence that this provider achieves it. Current attributable unique recipients, kit quantities, rescue use and operating costs were not found in primary material reviewed. Navigation is not prescribing, treatment initiation is not retention, and reported rescues are not additional lives.

Public supplies and existing county outreach are the comparator; donated naloxone, participant time, transport, clinician/MAT costs and shared HRT/Remedy/NEXT supply-chain contribution must be counted without duplicate health. Santa Cruz is outside the nine-county Bay; no positive Bay/SF share established. **Reserve California candidate, defer full model until current cash/quantity and additionality anchors.**

### 3. Central California Asthma Collaborative — home trigger remediation

[Current CARES page](https://www.centralcalasthma.org/community-health/cares/) verifies actual home-based assessment, education and remediation, with CalAIM Medi-Cal contracts in 13 counties. EIN lead 45-3599201. This is a clinical environmental-exposure mechanism, not a numerical advocacy model. Historical [CARB April 2021 project list](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2021-04/available_seps_04.20.2021_r.pdf) proposed $79,077.31 for 50 clients: $1,581.55/client, not a current quote or awarded marginal tranche.

The [Seattle randomized home-visiting trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15798126/) compared seven visits/full resources against one visit/limited resources over one year. Urgent use and caregiver quality of life improved; between-group symptom-day difference was not significant. No measured mortality effect should be assigned.

At that historical price, 50% whole-gift allocation and 50% additionality require approximately 0.633 incremental QALY per newly additional client to get 10 QALYs/$100,000. This is demanding for a finite asthma-morbidity pathway. Current public coverage further narrows philanthropy's uncovered population. Bay/SF coverage not established by statewide language. **Cheap screen failure for a high-probability strong ordinary-gift report; revisit only a much cheaper uncovered severe-asthma tranche.**

### 4. Give an Hour — donated specialist trauma therapy coordination

[Current approach](https://giveanhour.org/approach/) confirms national professional support alongside peers, education and contract products. [Provider FAQ](https://giveanhour.org/provider-faq/) asks for sustained weekly care, not a single curative session; insurance or sliding-scale arrangements may also occur. A [Ventura County 2023 resource guide](https://venturacounty.gov/human-services-agency/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2023/08/ENGLISH-OTV-Mental-Health-Resource-Guide-CONDENSED.pdf) is an actual California lead, not sufficient proof of current Bay access or incremental completed courses.

Why initially promising: one coordinator could unlock multiple clinically effective courses. At a hypothetical 0.1 incremental finite QALY/completed course, 100 additional courses per $100,000 are needed. With 50% allocation and 50% additionality, the coordination cost would need to be at most $250/course before Bay dilution. These are gates, not measured effects. Clinical modality, course completion and baseline alternative care are unknown; cumulative volunteer hours, referrals and satisfaction do not supply them. Donated qualified therapist time is a real outside resource. **Defer: low donor cost remains possible but current specialist course and California throughput premise insufficient.**

### 5. Suicide Prevention and Crisis Services of Yolo County — actual SOS school curriculum, not assumed Caring Contacts

[Official current site](https://www.suicidepreventionyolocounty.org/) verifies SOS school education, crisis lines and bereavement support. No current primary source reviewed establishes a standardized post-attempt Caring Contacts service. Thus the attractive low-cost suicide-followup hypothesis fails activity verification here; do not import another operator's protocol or mortality effect. SOS's attempt endpoint cannot simply be multiplied by an average attempt fatality risk as if every prevented self-reported attempt had that risk.

At a hypothetical 20 finite QALYs per net prevented death, 0.5 additional deaths per $100,000 would suffice overall, but neither that life expectancy nor a causal mortality change is established for the current portfolio. School and crisis-system funding, screening follow-up clinical costs and existing 988 access are baseline resources. Yolo is outside nine-county Bay; no Bay/SF delivery evidence. **Defer; only reopen for documented protocol, costs, unique high-risk entrants and completed follow-up.**

## Release rule

No full model authored from this screen. Highest-value next work is the narrow SIRUM cash / high-risk-course / geography reconciliation, not five new reports. Any later calculator must retain finite duration and later-treatment counterfactuals, nonpositive-health null prices, whole gift in every regional denominator, explicit external resources, and a zero or unknown SF pathway unless verified.
