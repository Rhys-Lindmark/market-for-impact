## Decisive clinical source and resource caveat

[Hanna et al.](https://www.bmj.com/content/371/bmj.m4087) studies associations between cancer treatment delays and mortality by cancer/treatment type. It supports urgency, not a causal effect per grant or ride; month-scale initial treatment delay is not interchangeable with a missed/rescheduled appointment. A report would need stage, curative intent, actual delay avoided, baseline prognosis, finite survival and no-gift alternatives, with signed adverse/null cases. Do not multiply a relative hazard by every recipient or transplant the paper's national death extrapolation.

Medical resources can dominate donor leverage. For an illustrative BACC $100k gift, 20 nominal cases and one genuinely additional completed course at $20,000 real clinical cost would raise gross associated funding to at least $120,000, making the same .20Q component at least $6m/10Q; actual cost depends on treatment and what was merely accelerated. Grants and other cash transfers should be distinguished from provider resource costs without pretending medical bills are opportunity cost. No current funding tranche or resource ledger was verified in this bounded screen.
