## Causal evidence: the closest comparison is not reassuring

1. [Rich etal2015 randomized continuation vs forced withdrawal](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26028120/):283randomized,60excluded as ineligible,leaving114continuation/109withdrawal. This tests preserving methadone during incarceration, not adding navigation to SF's already-funded guest dosing. Its large linkage effect cannot be assigned to a new gift. Neither a first clinic visit nor one guest dose equals retained medication-time.

2. **Closest incremental-navigation test:** [Schwartz etal2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31753737/),225Baltimore detainees randomized to interim methadone+navigation, methadonealone, or enhanced usual care. Methadone groups improved30-day entry compared with usual care, but navigation+medicine did not significantly outperform medicinealone.12-month estimated enrollment0.40/0.27/0.29was not significantly different; opioid tests also not significantly different. [24-month follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33187759/) does not establish a durable navigation gain. [Study team's qualitative follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34134868/) distinguishes improved first guest dosing from lack of significant enrollment/opioid-use benefit. Thus positive added-days assumptions below are **judgments despite a relevant null comparison**, not attenuated trial effect estimates.

3. Mortality bridge: [Sordo etal2017 primary meta-analysis of cohorts](https://www.bmj.com/content/357/bmj.j1550) reports unadjusted pooled all-cause mortality difference24.9/1,000person-years off versus on methadone(CI13.6–36.1); rates11.3/36.1. This is association, not randomized navigation mortality. Induction and discontinuation risks vary over time; annual pooled rates are not measured first-month SF fentanyl hazards. [Degenhardt etal2014 linked prison cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24612249/) finds adjusted mortalityHR0.25(CI0.12–0.53)with OST exposure in four weeks postrelease, supporting the mechanism but not an SF absolute risk or navigation effect. Do not multiply thisHR by the already computed Sordo rate difference.

## Proposed unit and boundaries

One consenting SF releasee already prescribed methadone receives a90-day continuity offer: prerelease plan verification, warm handoff to **their appropriate OTP**, transportation/phone support and follow-up after missed dosing. No prescribing/dosing advice or coercion; clinician determines safe restart. Appropriate alternative MOUD is not failure. Linkages back to other OTPs count only if they create new medication-time, not merely redirect existing treatment.

Model additional **medication-covered days within90days**, averaged over every offered releasee including nonengagement. Central7days is not7days for each retained patient; it is a population-average difference. No extra uptake or retention multiplier. No jail days, housing, naloxone reversals, nonfatal morbidity, avoided arrests or earnings gains. No extension past90days without evidence. Releasees may have repeat episodes: deduplicate overlapping90-day windows.

## Threshold, disconfirmation and duplicate accounting

Under$100K/10Q requires0.05Qat$500cost. With central mortality/survival/funding factors this requires293additionaldays **before funding displacement**, impossible within90days. Favorable requires20.85daysbeforefundingdisplacement;30assumed days clears donor threshold only while payer covers treatment. Expanded-resource favorable does not clear it. No probability assigned to this optimistic bundle.

Strongest disconfirmation: navigation-added randomized comparison was null; existing SF navigation and funded guest dosing may leave no additional service; known medication availability does not imply a staffing bottleneck; local covered-days effect could be zero. No measured Bayview baseline or new-dollar causal effect was found. A positive central value is a scenario, not the evidence-weighted claim that treatment benefit has been demonstrated for this gift.

Require before promotion: current payer/contract scope; release-to-first-dose and90-day covered-day records including transfers; actual failed-handoff causes; marginal navigator cost/caseload and after-hours capacity; clarification of September2025public navigation expansion; proof any private tranche does not reimburse alreadybillable/public work. Obtain outcomes for all offers, not only located/completing patients.

**Ledger:** HYA and HealthRIGHT360 medication-access models cannot claim the same incremental person-day; SF naloxone/DOPE/HRTC/GLIDE cannot also claim the same death reduction without a joint counterfactual. This model is marginal to existing naloxone and clinical provision; mortalitytransfer does not substitute for beneficiary/time overlap accounting. No additive portfolio claim from summing reportQALYs. Entity is net-new, but health opportunity is not automatically independent.

Readiness: useful **exploratory stress test**, not donation-ready and not a program-matched causal cost-effectiveness estimate. The key model choice is to center measured medication-time once available rather than import a forced-withdrawal effect into an already-treated setting.
