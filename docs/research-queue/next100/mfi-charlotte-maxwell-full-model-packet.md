# Charlotte Maxwell Clinic: aromatase-inhibitor arthralgia acupuncture

Research-only model packet; checked 2026-09-08. No Site work, outreach or donation commitments.

## Bottom line and unit

**Conditional central donor estimate: $3.70 million per 10 incremental QALYs; favorable $112,275; pessimistic $800m.** Null and harm remain possible. This is a narrow symptom model, **not a valuation of CMC's whole integrative-care bundle**. It does not establish an under-$100,000 opportunity or a currently available restricted grant.

Unit: one trial-compatible adult with early-stage breast cancer, currently taking an aromatase inhibitor and experiencing clinically relevant treatment-associated joint pain, **assigned/offered an 18-session acupuncture course over 12 weeks**, with a limited allowance for subsequent sessions within one year. SF referrals are the geographic target. Do not count all cancer patients, all clinic visitors, a single treatment, or a completed-course subgroup as this denominator. Exact CMC caseload matching these criteria and trial protocol delivery remain unverified.

## 2. Primary randomized evidence

[Hershman et al., JAMA2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6583520/), DOI10.1001/jama.2018.8907: 226 women randomized to true acupuncture110, sham59 or waitlist57; mean age60.7, predominantly White, early-stage disease. At six weeks, the adjusted worst-pain difference on BPI's 0–10 scale favored true acupuncture by **.96 points versus waitlist (95%CI .24–1.67)** and **.92 versus sham (.20–1.65)**. The roughly two-point within-arm improvement is not the incremental effect. Authors emphasized modest/uncertain clinical importance; the prespecified two-point between-group difference was not attained. Grade1 bruising occurred in47% true versus25% sham; one grade2 presyncope episode in each needle arm; no grade3+ events reported. No QALYs measured.

[Hershman et al., JAMA Network Open2022 follow-up](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9652759/), DOI10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.41720: full assigned dose was **12 sessions in weeks1–6 plus six in weeks7–12 =18**, not12total. At52weeks pain differences were .99 versus waitlist (.12–1.86) and1.08 versus sham (.24–1.91);191/226 supplied52-week follow-up. All arms were offered ten further sessions between24–52weeks; reported later receipt was13.2% true,11.3% sham,10.6% waitlist. Thus this is not pristine proof that18sessions alone sustain benefit for a year. AI discontinuation was12.1% overall and did not differ by arm; **no adherence-mediated cancer-survival gain** is credited. Worst pain persisted as a signal, but other52-week pain/quality-of-life domains were mixed.

The two papers are follow-ups of the **same trial**, not independent replications. Waitlist represents no additional acupuncture rather than no oncology or no analgesia. Sham comparison helps limit exaggerated needling-specific claims; do not add sham and waitlist effects. Use the .96 waitlist contrast for access to added acupuncture, with transport/shrinkage; a .92 sham-based sensitivity changes little. An active alternative pain-treatment comparator may shrink benefit further. Existing attendance/missingness are part of the trial estimate; do not apply another completion factor unless explicitly modeling worse local delivery.

## 3. Utility and duration bridge: judgments, not measured QALYs

No validated target-population mapping from the single BPI worst-pain item to preference-based utility was verified. Central **b=.02 utility per one-point BPI improvement** is an explicit analyst judgment. It translates .96 pain points to .0192 utility before transfer. It is not “.96QALY,” not a fraction of cancer survival and not a validated universal linear mapping across0–10. Worst pain and daily average pain differ, making this bridge particularly uncertain.

Central effective duration **Y=.75year** is an assumed area under the effect curve within the first year: allows for initial ramp-up, imperfect persistence and counterfactual treatment. The positive52-week endpoint supports investigating persistence but does not itself measure the integrated utility area. Favorable Y=1year assumes nearly full-year effect; pessimistic .25year constrains benefit to the course. No benefits after one year; repeat years require repeat costs/evidence. The transport factor **t=.75** further accounts for protocol, SF travel, population and comparator differences, not a separate measured adherence rate.

Central net QALY before funding additionality: `.96×.02×.75×.75=.0108`. Do not separately add pain interference, stiffness, well-being or reduced analgesic use without checking overlap; these may describe the same utility burden. No immunity, tumor response, mortality, future recurrence or caregiver benefit enters. The native symptom benefit can coexist with zero preference-based utility benefit; `b=0` is a real uncertainty case.

## 4. Costs, resources and payer counterfactual

**Constructed C=$2,000 donor cash per assigned course**: about20planned-equivalent encounters×$90 for scheduling/space/supplies/supervision/transport/access allocation, plus$200 intake and administration. This is a planning judgment, not CMC's quote. Twenty includes18protocol visits plus a two-visit allowance for later acupuncture; it does not mean the RCT delivered20to everyone. A rough13.2%×10=1.32 later-session allowance would be an upper-cap-based approximation, not the observed mean. Actual use/billing must replace this buffer. The six-month counterfactual late acupuncture is not automatically charged to the charity.

This donor budget assumes clinicians provide some donated time. An expanded **full-resource diagnostic adds $800** (20×.5hour×$80/hour clinical opportunity cost, all judgments) **plus$600 patient time/travel opportunity cost**, yielding$3,400 per assigned unit and **$6.30m/10Q** at the same central health estimate. Do not add the$800 if the$90cash allocation already purchases the same clinical labor. No donated professional time is intrinsically free. A resource ledger must distinguish sunk clinic capacity from additional paid/volunteer hours. These are gross resource scenarios, not net societal ICERs; no assumed downstream savings offset costs.

Favorable donorC=$600 requires very low marginal cash delivery (~$30×20) and adequate volunteer/room capacity; its full-resource ratio will be worse. PessimisticC=$3,000 allows more costly local care. No 18-session course should be priced as one$30visit. Baseline publicly/grant-funded courses are not newly created by an unrestricted donation.

**Payer overlap is unresolved and materially time-sensitive.** [Current DHCS benefits overview](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal/benefits/) lists acupuncture, while [DHCS budget materials](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/budget-information/) contain proposed adult-acupuncture eliminations. A proposal is not proof of enacted loss of coverage. [SFHP Care Plus2026 handbook](https://www.sfhp.org/wp-content/files/programs/care-plus/member-materials/Member_Handbook_EN.pdf) describes supplemental acupuncture access up to24visits/year; Care Plus is a specific Medicare+Medi-Cal product, not all Medi-Cal members. Coverage indications, provider requirements, referral/authorization and date must be checked for each modeled patient. Do not assert no insurance covers this course, nor that everyone can already obtain it. CMC's grants53% does not identify which services are publicly paid.

Central funding/access additionality **a=.5** means half the modeled benefit would be absent without the donor contribution, versus grants, insurance, other free providers or self-payment. It is not estimated from the grant mix. DonorC is charged in full despite possible substitution; resourceC is likewise a gross resources-used diagnostic, not automatically the incremental social cost if care would have occurred elsewhere. No payer contribution is added to donor cost and then subtracted again. If additional access is zero, attributed health benefit and new-treatment harm are zero.

## 5. Inspectable model and scenarios

`Q per assigned unit = a × (E × b × Y × t − h)`

`USD per 10Q = 10×C/Q` forQ>0.

E=incremental BPI worst-pain points; b=judgment utility/point; Y=effective years; t=transport; a=funding/access additionality; h=additional treatment-harm QALYs per genuinely additional assigned unit. Include both benefit and harm insidea. Yalready integrates time; do not multiply again by course weeks. No arbitrary cohort size or available offers are claimed.

Achieving that cash price does not establish cheap full-resource delivery or actual marginal funding. Present evidence does not support rounding$112,275 down to an under-$100k claim.
