# Ten new-to-coverage SF-serving nonprofit program leads

Discovery only; primary pages checked 2026-09-08. No cost-effectiveness scores, Site changes, outreach or claimed funding commitments.

## Scope and novelty

These are **ten distinct programs newly surfaced relative to the inspected modeled reports and conventional/unconventional briefs**, not ten newly founded nonprofits. Checked the `*cea*` files and `docs/research-drafts` in `work/market-for-impact-large-bay-impact` for the ten organization names/aliases; no matches. This is a program-level deduplication check, not proof their names never occurred in the 6,688-record universe. Most organizations are established. SisterWeb has a specifically documented 2026 initiative; Zen documents a 2022 extension. A current page or copyright date does not establish a recent launch. If the requested universe is strictly organizations founded in the last few years, this does **not** satisfy that narrower criterion.

“Shortlist” below means worth further clinical evidence/budget research, not an effective-giving endorsement. Mechanisms and possible health endpoints are hypotheses unless expressly identified as measured; no output counts are QALYs. No verified incremental funding tranche was located for any candidate.

## 1. SisterWeb — community doula delivery and SF Doula Hub

**Existence/local delivery:** [Current programs](https://www.sisterweb.org/programs) lists Kindred Birth Companions and a community-doula intake route at 1385 Mission Street. A [partnered process evaluation, 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10995-022-03373-x) documents actual SF delivery, originally supported by SFDPH. This is not the earlier HPP perinatal-depression prevention hypothesis.

**Newness:** SisterWeb's [own current announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sisterweb) advertised an August 31, 2026 SF Doula Hub session: help with Medi-Cal enrollment, credentialing, practice readiness, mentorship and client connections. This verifies a recent initiative announcement, not completed incremental births attributable to the Hub. Existing direct doula delivery predates it.

**Mechanism/bottleneck:** Additional supported births or postpartum visits could improve maternal experience and selected complications; price a completed care episode and incremental attendance, not training certificates. Separate Hub administration from payer-funded clinical services; no whole-infant lifetime gain. **Shortlist**, especially the concrete enrollment/credentialing bottleneck, with Medi-Cal reimbursement and existing SF grant overlap examined first. Do not revive the Latine Semilla Sagrada program from old pages: SisterWeb announced its closure in December 2023.

## 2. Maitri — Maitri Affirmation Center (MAC)

**Existence/local delivery:** [Maitri's current primary page](https://www.maitrisf.org/) describes specialized short-term nursing care surrounding gender-affirming surgery, at 401 Duboce Avenue; nonprofit EIN 94-3189198. This is postoperative recovery support, not Lyon-Martin's earlier-access clinical care and not generic Community Forward medical respite.

**Newness:** New to inspected coverage; exact MAC launch date not verified. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** Supported discharge, wound care and a safe recovery setting may reduce complications or permit otherwise-delayed indicated surgery. Need bed-days, eligible referrals, occupancy and a genuinely additional bed/staff shift. Count only the marginal recovery/access benefit versus hospital, family or other funded aftercare—not all surgical benefit. Include nursing and capital opportunity costs and check health-plan/public payment. **Shortlist**, with potentially expensive care and a strong possibility that extra donations do not expand fixed capacity.

## 3. San Francisco Women Against Rape (SFWAR) — medical accompaniment and short-term peer counseling

**Existence/local delivery:** [Primary service page](https://sfwar.org/programs-services/advocacy-counseling/) identifies certified rape-crisis counselors accompanying survivors at ZSFG emergency care and other SF facilities; individual peer counseling is ten sessions and support groups run eight–ten weeks. Address 3543 18th Street; page gives EIN 92-2756753. These services are distinct from general counseling programs already reviewed.

**Newness:** Established service, newly researched here. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** Timely trauma-informed care engagement and reduced distress/PTSD are plausible endpoints; neither ten peer sessions nor forensic attendance proves trauma-focused psychotherapy efficacy. Price incremental counselor coverage plus supervision, referrals and clinical treatment induced. Separate public/rape-crisis grants and alternative hospital advocacy. **Shortlist** for the defined dose and immediate clinical pathway, conditional on matching causal evidence; do not transplant a clinician-delivered PTSD treatment effect into peer support.

## 4. La Casa de las Madres — emergency domestic-violence shelter with survivor support

**Existence/local delivery:** [Current service-access page](https://www.lacasa.org/) explicitly says shelter, crisis lines and drop-in counseling remain active; [contact page](https://www.lacasa.org/contact) locates the organization in SF. [Program/report page](https://www.lacasa.org/what-we-do/) links the 2025 annual report. Do not confuse this organization with Michigan's LACASA Center or another city's Casa shelter.

**Newness:** Established, not newly launched. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** A genuinely additional safe placement may prevent repeat assault and improve mental health; relevant unit is an episode of shelter plus services, compared with actual alternative safety options, not all lifetime violence avoided. Needed: turnaways, occupied bed-days, length of stay, staffing and survivor-controlled follow-up. Public domestic-violence and housing funds may already pay capacity. **Shortlist** as a distinct violence-prevention mechanism; attribution and continued safety beyond discharge are central uncertainties.

## 5. Safe & Sound — Integrated Family Services (IFS)

**Existence/local delivery:** [2024 primary annual-report section](https://annualreport.safeandsound.org/strengthening-families-transforming-systems) reports 333 children and caregivers receiving IFS support, describing care coordination, therapy/parenting and practical support. [Current service description](https://safeandsound.org/what-we-do/) confirms SF operations. This is not Wu Yee home visiting or a second Center for Youth Wellness entity; CYW merged with Safe & Sound in 2021.

**Newness:** Existing program, new to this coverage. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** Caregiver mental-health improvement or reduced child maltreatment is plausible. Protective-factor improvement, reduced stress and avoiding entry into child welfare are not validated QALY equivalents and do not by themselves establish causation. Price a complete family episode with treatment/referral costs, not divide organizational revenue by children-plus-adults. Check SF family-resource and clinical payer funding. **Hold for evaluation retrieval**: a named local evaluation may make research decisive, but do not assume all prevented system entry is health benefit.

## 6. PRC — Emergency Financial Assistance (EFA) for HIV-related medical access

**Existence/local delivery:** [Current PRC programs](https://prcsf.org/what-we-do/) specifies grants to people with HIV/AIDS to prevent homelessness and continue treatment. [Primary 2022 brochure](https://prcsf.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Emergency-Financial-Assistance-Brochure-ENG-2022.pdf) identifies SF delivery and the 2017 merger of AIDS Emergency Fund into PRC. Do not count the legacy fund as an independent extra charity.

**Newness:** Established. **Distinct marginal mechanism:** Restrict a possible future study to a bill/payment that prevents a documented interruption in medical care; generic rental assistance would duplicate the existing housing mechanism. Cost is grant plus assessment/administration and induced resources; cash-transfer face value and social resource cost are different perspectives. Ryan White, ADAP, Medi-Cal, hospital assistance and existing EFA awards are possible substitutes, not proven exclusions. **Shortlist only the medical-access tranche**; reject another generic housing score without a new causal question. Viral suppression or mortality cannot be credited just because a grant was made.

## 7. Zen Caregiving Project — trained bedside volunteers at Laguna Honda

**Existence/local delivery:** [Current volunteer page](https://www.zencaregiving.org/volunteer) identifies ongoing Laguna Honda placements and a Cadillac Hotel extension in 2022. It provides specific April 2026 training dates, 40-hour initial training, and a five-hour weekly/one-year volunteer commitment. Application period currently closed. Training fees $250–$350 are participant contributions, **not full delivery costs**.

**Newness:** Established Laguna Honda service; 2022 hotel extension is newer but overlaps Curry's setting. Use the hospital bedside mechanism for distinctness. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** Less distress/isolation near end of life, possibly caregiver respite; duration is remaining supported days, not lifetime survival. Need completed incremental bedside hours, patient utility/distress evidence and nursing/hospice counterfactual. Volunteer time, coordinator cost and hospital resources are not free. **Hold**, because recruiting volunteers and obtaining placements may bind more than donations and the health bridge is unmeasured.

## 8. Family House Inc. — pediatric serious-illness treatment lodging

**Existence/local delivery:** [Current stay instructions](https://familyhouseinc.org/planning-your-stay/) locate the Nancy & Stephen Grand Family House at 540 Mission Bay Boulevard North and require UCSF social-worker qualification. [FY2024 report](https://familyhouseinc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/FY2024-Impact-Report-Digital.pdf) describes 80 rooms for families traveling over 50 miles for a child's treatment. Correct entity is **familyhouseinc.org**, not similarly named familyhouse.org.

**Newness:** Established. **Geography:** Real SF delivery primarily benefits families residing elsewhere. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** Lodging may prevent missed oncology/serious-illness treatment and caregiver distress. Need otherwise-missed treatment probability, room occupancy/turnaways and additional room-night cost; never attribute all cancer survival to accommodation. Hospital lodging alternatives and existing donated rooms matter. **Shortlist for SF-service geography; reject for a strictly SF-resident-only pool** unless a qualifying local-resident subgroup is documented.

## 9. Charlotte Maxwell Clinic — specific cancer symptom-support services for SF referrals

**Existence/local delivery:** [Primary service page](https://charlottemaxwell.org/about-us/what-we-do/) identifies Oakland-based free services for Bay Area women with cancer, telehealth, transport assistance and actual referrals from ZSFG and Shanti. Thus SF-serving is supported, but an SF clinic location is not. [Organization brochure](https://www.charlottemaxwell.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/CMC-Brochure-1022-DIGITAL.pdf) dates the founding to 1991; its filename is not evidence of a 2026 launch.

**Mechanism/bottleneck:** Investigate only a specified modality and symptom—for example acupuncture for a defined treatment-related pain syndrome—against appropriate usual or sham care. Do not accept promotional immune-system, energy-work or healing claims as causal evidence. Full licensed-practitioner time, trips, screening and herb–drug safety are material. Insurance/public supportive-care access could substitute. **Reject the undifferentiated integrative bundle for QALY modeling; retain a tightly specified symptom-treatment subprogram as conditional research.** SF-specific patient share remains unquantified.

## 10. San Francisco CASA — foster-youth health-service access through a sustained advocate

**Existence/local delivery:** [Current primary description](https://www.sfcasa.org/about-sfcasa) describes actual trained volunteers serving SF foster youth and helping access essential services; it links 2025 impact findings. Keep its overlapping/undated counters out of a denominator until reconciled. This is not generic youth employment or the already modeled youth counseling course.

**Newness:** Established, newly examined. **Mechanism/bottleneck:** A sustained advocate might secure needed mental-health or medical care across placement changes. Court outcomes, graduation and relationship continuity are not QALYs. Needed: incremental completed treatment, care effectiveness, advocate retention and supervisor caseload—not all matched youth assumed treated. Foster-care health entitlements, social workers and existing court services form the comparator. **Hold**, with a specific medical-access cohort required; broad CASA assignment effects may be confounded by selection and cannot be assumed to generate lifelong health gains.

## A genuinely new launch rejected on local verification

[Baby2Baby Golden State Start](https://baby2baby.org/goldenstatestart/) began summer 2026, but the participating-hospital list updated September 4 had **no SF hospital**. Fremont, Fairfield and Napa are not SF. [FAQ updated August 19](https://baby2baby.org/goldenstatestart/frequently-asked-questions/) states $6.2m state funding for 40m diapers, 400 per newborn: $62 per allocation including manufacture, warehousing and hospital shipping, not incremental health gain. Reject as a verified current SF hospital program; do not imply that a nationwide/SF-adjacent nonprofit footprint establishes local rollout. This is an eleventh screened lead, not part of the ten verified SF-serving prospects above.
