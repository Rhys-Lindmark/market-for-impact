## Trial transfer: explicitly conditional, not guaranteed conservative

[Weintraub et al., 2006 primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2257982/) confirms SF children, caregiver counseling in all groups, control/intervention OR3.77 and a center-adjusted analysis among280 followed children. The four intended visits were baseline,6,12,18 months; only one child received four active applications. The protocol error and incomplete follow-up make this an assignment-based anchor, not an observed completed-four-active-dose effect.

Using that effect without upward correction is a reasonable declared modeling choice, but **not a proven lower bound** for modern completers. Selection, baseline risk and usual care can change benefit in either direction. Say “borrowed assignment-effect calibration for a completed-course scenario,” not “conservative clinical efficacy of four completed doses.” Do not replace it with the nonrandomized actual-dose odds ratio. A center-adjusted odds ratio applied to a pooled judgmental p0 is also an approximation, not a reconstructed trial event risk.

Direct PMC access challenged this audit; indexed primary full-text passages were used. The source supports caries outcomes, not measured pain, remission, preference-based utility or anesthesia avoidance.

[Protecting Teeth@3 primary paper](https://d-nb.info/1220420751/34), DOI10.1159/000509680, supplies a useful numerical contrary anchor: worsening d3mft26.9% versus31.6%, OR.80 (95%CI.62–1.03), P=.078, on top of Childsmile care. This is not an identical outcome/population and should not replace the SF incidence estimate mechanically. It illustrates why an active preventive-care comparator can greatly reduce incremental benefit. Do not describe nonsignificance as proof of exactly zero efficacy.

## Utility bridge and horizon

The bridge `.25 symptomatic probability × .05 utility loss × .25 cumulative years` is explicitly judgmental. Its units reconcile; none should be represented as a measured pediatric utility coefficient. A prevented incident caries case is not necessarily a prevented pain episode; all three bridge terms require empirical refinement. Prompt access to restorative care can reduce counterfactual symptomatic time, while lack of access can increase it. The model cannot select high untreated-pain risk solely because that makes prevention look favorable.

Keep Y as total symptomatic time that would occur **within the two-year analytic horizon**, conditional on the prevented case becoming symptomatic. Do not count the full horizon again, or assign six months of symptoms to every late incident case without an incidence-time assumption. Benefit/cost discounting is currently absent: explicitly label this a two-year undiscounted exploratory model or add a dated timing convention. Do not invent exact present values with no visit/event timing distribution.

The donor-useful threshold cost at the chosen health assumptions is $1.07665 central or $35.90542 favorable per completed course. Those are much lower than the assumed $200/$75 navigation costs and are not provider offers. Even the favorable case remains above $100K/10Q.

## Complete-course cost, payer overlap and attribution

The $200 unit is **navigation spending divided by observed completed courses**, including all failed-referral work. It is not spending per attempted referral. Consequently do not multiply benefit by completion probability again. Conversely, a current navigation yield cannot be inferred from that costing convention: the price remains hypothetical until both total workload and completed-course denominator are observed. Retain the cost of failed attempts; do not claim those families receive no service or health effect unless measured.

[DHCS EPSDT](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/early-and-periodic-screening-diagnostic-and-treatment-services/) verifies medically necessary dental care for full-scope Medi-Cal members under21 without member cost. That is coverage, not appointment access, clinical eligibility or payment approval for this precise course. Public funding can enable an incremental navigation benefit; it does not automatically eliminate philanthropic additionality. Nor can NICOS claim to purchase clinical work already reimbursed.

Define `a` as the proportion of counted completed courses that this gift causes beyond **all existing navigation and alternative provider delivery**. Define `t` as transport of the clinical effect among those genuinely added courses. If equivalent varnish elsewhere makes a course redundant, put that redundancy in a or a specified active-care clinical contrast—not both. A half-effect t plus half-access a is not inherently double counting, but “modern comparator” language in t and “already served” language in a must be separated explicitly.

`C + a*clinicalCost` is a coherent conditional resource proxy if the two costs are nonoverlapping and clinicalCost is additional consumption for induced care. Its $300 input is an assumed resource cost, not a reimbursement tariff. The $80 support component must not duplicate the four $30 coordination allocations or publicly funded task-force labor. No actual overlap amount is known. Patient/caregiver time remains omitted, not proven zero.

If harms include burden on unsuccessful referrals or displacement of another child's treatment slot, they may occur outside the completed, additional-course factor a. Keep the present signed h term for harms conditional on truly induced treatment, and add an independent displacement/burden term if later evidence requires it. A pure replacement model should not erase genuine portfolio opportunity costs merely by setting a=0.
