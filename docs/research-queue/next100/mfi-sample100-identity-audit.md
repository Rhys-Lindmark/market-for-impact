## Three next models, with stopping rules

1. **Bayview Foundation — incremental postrelease methadone continuity.** Verified local medication and jail-service infrastructure makes the causal mechanism concrete. Model only additional medication-covered time caused by the proposed access/retention intervention; do not count referrals, existing jail dosing, or an entire treated lifetime. First obtain public descriptions of handoff operations, payer responsibilities and a marginal staff/transport unit. If no incremental treatment-time mechanism can be substantiated, publish an explicitly exploratory/no-offer model or stop. Existing HYA/HealthRIGHT model assumptions are not local evidence for Bayview.

2. **Family Caregiver Alliance — a defined caregiver counseling/skills course**, with respite as an alternative rather than a combined unpriced package. Existing SF-inclusive service plus own EIN is clear. Match actual delivered course to causal caregiver mental-health evidence, then estimate caregiver utility separately from the care recipient. Consultation, referral and respite eligibility are not completed treatment. County/state-funded baseline, waiting list, substitution for purchased respite, course uptake and incremental staffing are decisive unknowns.

3. **La Casa — structured trauma-focused counseling for domestic-violence survivors.** Start with the actual counseling protocol, dose, completion and public-contract cost boundary. Do not assume the provider uses a specific evidence-based therapy until verified. A transferable trial must match that protocol/population; shelter availability and safety outcomes cannot be converted mechanically into QALYs. If counseling is supportive rather than the tested protocol, use a broad/null exploratory model or choose the better-defined service instead.

These three are selected for distinct identity, actual SF clinical/support infrastructure and plausible narrow health pathways—not demonstrated superiority. APA Family Support Services is the strongest identity-cleared substitute for an explicit early-childhood focus; NICOS and On Lok should not leapfrog their unresolved delivery/gift mappings merely because external dental/falls trials are convenient.
