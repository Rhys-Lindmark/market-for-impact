Reviewed 2026-09-08. Research only; no outreach, Site changes or deployments. Current ranking in `work/market-for-impact-large-bay-impact` identifies ranks13–16 as DOPE, Huckleberry, RAMS and Lyon-Martin. The previous three funding briefs cover ranks1–12. Prices below identify the exploratory models, not verified donation opportunities or recommendations. For all four, **verified marginal funding room remains unknown (`null`), not zero**. No priced, restricted, dated next-gift tranche was verified.

## 13. National Harm Reduction Coalition — DOPE tenant-responder site-year (~$2.425M/10 QALYs)

**Actual modeled unit:** a hypothetical additional occupied100-resident site-year of tenant-responder coordination, not all DOPE naloxone distribution. The central$30,000 comprises four specialists ×$50/week ×52=$10,400; five coordinator hours/week ×$60 ×52=$15,600; and$4,000 other delivery expense. Historical$50 gift-card compensation is an observed anchor; the number of specialists, annual duration, loaded coordinator rate and overall budget are judgments, not a provider quote. Public naloxone, building services and later medical care are outside donor cash. Funding additionality0.5 is assumed, not measured.

[DOPE SRO project description](https://harmreduction.org/news/dope-sro-project/) documents the tenant-based intervention and its 2020/2021 development; it is historical program evidence, not an open2026 funding offer. [SF response page](https://harmreduction.org/issues/fentanyl/first-responders-safety-guide/san-francisco-response/) identifies SFDPH funding and distribution partners including SFAF, GLIDE and HYA, but its historical2017 reporting is not current output.

**Material current overlap:** [SFDPH FY25–26 performance objectives](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/PBH_Performance_Objectives_FY25_hwPpKPK.26_v2.pdf), created May2,2025/revised June22,2026, physicalp5, already assigns DOPE contract1000032403 a minimum50 trainings and onboarding two new organizations/programs as distribution sites. Physicalp2 separately describes DISH contract1000035812, *Peer Led Overdose Prevention Educators in PSH*: target12 unduplicated PSH sites, four outside DISH, with peer completers leading groups at five or more sites. These are existing contractual targets, not completed output or unfunded room. The DISH mechanism is particularly close to the modeled tenant-responder proposal: avoiding only duplicate naloxone supply is insufficient.

[NHRC giving page](https://give.harmreduction.org/) offers general national giving and illustrative giving levels. It does not establish a restricted SF SRO site-year offer or the remaining cost of one. Old activity references on the page do not establish current capacity.

**Decisive missing evidence:** a named uncovered building and occupied resident denominator; current DOPE/DISH/building responder coverage and executed obligations; publicly supplied stock availability; incremental coordinator and specialist hours with compensation/supervision; provider acceptance of an SF/site restriction; and a dated budget/remaining gap. Compare additional response coverage, not reversals treated as causal deaths prevented. If another contract already buys the same site coverage, donor additionality can be zero.

## 14. Huckleberry Youth Programs — adolescent anxiety counseling course (~$3.692M/10 QALYs)

**Actual modeled unit:** a proposed brief behavioral course for adolescent anxiety, not all SF/Marin counseling or shelter services. Central$2,400 per offered course is12 ×45-minute contacts plus three hours engagement/supervision, totaling12hours ×$200. It pays for unsuccessful engagement/noncompletion; it is not a verified local protocol or reimbursement rate. Model additionality0.5 combines alternative access/funding displacement.

[Counseling programs](https://www.huckleberryyouth.org/counseling-programs/) verifies no-fee full-scope Medi-Cal services and sliding-scale counseling down to zero, integrated with other programs. It does not establish an anxiety-specific waitlist, course dose, marginal price or new clinician capacity. Its reference to a new state contract expanding child-abuse-victim therapy is undated: do not describe it as a new2026 award. The page links a donation route, but the external form could not be inspected; no program restriction was verified.

[FY2025 audited financial statements](https://www.huckleberryyouth.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/101052_Huckleberry-Youth-Program_6.30.25-YE-FINAL-FS.pdf), year ended June30,2025, program-description section, reports228 SF counseling youth aged12–24 and their families. That is not228 anxiety-course completers. Note7 reports counseling restricted net assets: opening$100,000 plus$25,000 additions less$125,000 releases equals zero closing balance. This is a historical restriction balance—not proof of zero cash, exhausted current counseling resources or a marginal funding gap.

**Decisive missing evidence:** current SF anxiety-specific demand and wait time; clinician language, modality and available hours; authorization/denial and reimbursement rules; current state/city grant scopes; actual costs per offered course including failures; and a written donor restriction that buys additional treatment rather than replacing reimbursement. Free to patients does not mean unfunded. Do not infer counseling closure from separate clinical-service consolidation proposals.

## 15. RAMS — additional adult depression course (~$4.211M/10 QALYs)

**Actual modeled unit:** a hypothetical15-contact depression course within Adult Outpatient Services. Central$3,000 equals15 ×$150 loaded contact cost plus$750 intake, supervision and coordination. It is per offered course, including noncompletion, and not a verified standard RAMS CBT protocol. The external CoBalT evidence concerns CBT added to usual care for treatment-resistant depression; a generic RAMS psychotherapy gift is not automatically the same intervention. Transfer0.25 and funding additionality0.5 are judgments.

[Adult Outpatient Services](https://ramsinc.org/programs/clinical-counseling-services/adult-outpatient-services/) verifies SF delivery to low-income Medi-Cal, Medicare and uninsured clients, with counseling, case management and psychiatry. It offers multiple modalities, not a confirmed15-contact CBT expansion. No priced depression tranche, available clinician hours or dated anxiety/depression waitlist was found.

[Donation page](https://ramsinc.org/donate-now/) provides general organizational giving and check/electronic routes. It does not establish an accepted depression-course restriction; not all embedded form controls were inspectable, so this is absence of verification rather than a claim that restricted gifts are prohibited. [Impact page](https://ramsinc.org/about/our-impact/) describes organization-level activity and spending; direct-service spending share is not marginal funding room or health impact.

**Decisive missing evidence:** executed Adult Outpatient funding and billable-payer capacity; the specific clinical indication/modality proposed; whether therapists, recruitment or supervision constrain additional courses; unfilled versus unfunded positions; payer reimbursement for an additional patient; and a restricted incremental budget. Organizationwide funding pressure cannot be assigned to this depression mechanism without allocation evidence. The donor should not fund a nominal course already purchased by public contracts or insurance.

## 16. Lyon-Martin — earlier clinically indicated adult testosterone initiation (~$4.571M/10 QALYs)

**Actual modeled unit:** earlier testosterone initiation for clinically eligible adults seeking masculinization—not minors, estrogen, surgery, all gender-affirming services or lifetime primary care. Central$1,000 is three clinical/lab/medication encounter bundles ×$250 plus$250 navigation, including failed engagement. No reimbursement savings are assumed. Additionality0.5 refers to starts actually brought forward relative to existing access, not half of all clinic encounters.

[Healthcare services](https://www.lyon-martin.org/healthcare-services) verifies hormone treatment, injections, labs and self-injection training, with surgical referrals separately described. [Insurance coverage](https://www.lyon-martin.org/insurance-coverage) lists Medicare and full-scope Medi-Cal pathways and managed-care/referral conditions. Uninsured-only assumptions or universal inability to bill would therefore be wrong.

[Save Lyon-Martin campaign](https://www.savelyonmartin.com/) describes a permanent home and broader capacity expansion, aiming to serve an additional1,000 people annually across several services. This is a real organizational expansion aim, but neither1,000 earlier testosterone starts nor an operating-cost quote. Do not convert a total capital campaign target into an available hormone-treatment tranche or assume a gift accelerates starts immediately.

[SF HRC commission grant-approval request, February9,2026](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/HRC_RFP_100_Grant_Agreement_Commission_Approval_20260209.pdf), physicalp12, lists Lyon-Martin awardID BV-SI04-26-004, no fiscal sponsor, $202,000 annually/$404,000 over two years. Its proposed integrated clinical, mental-health, case-management, crisis and benefits-navigation scope spans adults/youth. It is not a hormone-only donor offer; a request for approval is not independently verified execution/payment.

**Decisive missing evidence:** a dated adult SF new-start queue and the actual cause of delay; incremental prescriber/lab/navigation capacity versus capital needs; insured/uninsured mix and payer denials; current grants' covered work; medications and follow-up financing; accepted restriction; and a written remaining budget with date-bounded expected acceleration. If treatment would occur equally soon without the donation, this mechanism's incremental benefit is zero.

## Integration recommendation

Keep all four verified-room fields `null`, with separate fields for actual existing service, model-matched intervention, publicly funded overlap, restricted-gift acceptance, remaining gap and date checked. Retain exploratory cost-effectiveness estimates as judgments while showing funding confidence as unverified. A general donation link is an operational giving route—not evidence that the modeled intervention is purchasable. Require a provider-confirmed scope, budget and counterfactual before turning any into an actionable next-gift recommendation.
