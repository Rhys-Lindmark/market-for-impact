## 21. St. Anthony Foundation — additional diabetes outreach/reconnection (~$10.667M/10Q)

**Unit:** proposed additional outreach offer, central$400, transferred external modeled0.0015QALY ×0.5 transfer ×0.5 funding additionality. It is not an entire diabetes treatment course or the separate peer-supported redesign. Cost and allocation are judgments; the health bridge requires ensuing care, not an appointment alone.

[Current medical-clinic page](https://www.stanthonysf.org/services/medical-clinic/) verifies SF Tenderloin delivery and actively seeks volunteers for patient outreach/operations. This offers a plausible nonfinancial capacity pathway, not proof of unfunded paid outreach positions. Whole-clinic visits and patients cannot price the diabetes tranche. [FAQ](https://www.stanthonysf.org/faq/) identifies the clinic as a CCS FQHC with sliding-fee eligibility and Healthy San Francisco support for visits/medications. Its older generic statement that everything is donation-funded conflicts with this more specific payer description; do not claim zero public support from the generic language. Current contracts, reimbursement and grant allocation need reconciliation.

The model's [January30,2025 diabetes-reconnection report](https://www.stanthonysf.org/how-st-anthonys-medical-clinic-is-enhancing-diabetes-care-rosas-journey-to-reconnection/) timed out twice in this audit; its prior-reviewed process results were not newly verified. The clinic page links general giving, not a priced diabetes restriction.

**Could overturn0.5:** outreach already included in QI staffing/grants, volunteers able to do the additional work without donor spending, or patients unable to obtain the visits/medications to which outreach reconnects them. Need a current eligible disconnected cohort, cost per offered outreach episode including failed contacts, existing funding, visit capacity, and diabetes-specific accepted budget. Donation dependence does not establish incremental health production.

## Material cross-cohort corrections from all25 funding checks

1. **Separate model estimates from purchasable offers.** All25 verified-room figures remain unknown. General fundraising, contract totals, restricted net assets and proposed cuts are not remaining marginal budgets; do not fabricate a numerical discount update from them.
2. **Five Keys and IOA need explicit donor-perspective handling.** Their native models lack a separate funding-additionality input. Existing public education/call support could make gross resource estimates poor donor proxies. Any new factor must be declared, not implied already present. IOA$1,040 arithmetic also contains no additional non-call allowance.
3. **Correct overlapping program ledgers.** SFAF's2026 proposal names HYA/HRTC/GLIDE subcontractors; DOPE site-year work also overlaps DISH peer-led PSH targets. Map recipients, sites, staff time and stock—not merely prime contractors. Do not add independently attributed full mortality benefits for the same people.
4. **Preserve cost and dose boundaries.** Openhouse$100 matching appeal and PHC giving levels are not verified full marginal courses; JCYC school-year wage is not a future summer quote; YMCA25versus26sessions requires course selection; Self-Help's observed short Sun-style course does not verify the modeled longer protocol.
5. **Drug and vaccine financing is conditional.** SFFC has unverified local Shingrix delivery; GSK PAP has eligibility/replenishment/site-cap boundaries, not cash refunds. SFCHC and HealthRIGHT zero donor drug share needs a real payer pathway. NEMS recurring retention costs are not a one-time annual gift.
6. **Do not misallocate campaigns/cuts.** Lyon-Martin permanent-home expansion is not1,000earlier testosterone starts; SFCCC$35K education-award appeal is not wound-care room; HRTC supply-policy cut is not an alcohol-therapy deficit; St.Anthony generic donation-only language does not override FQHC/HealthySF disclosure; IOA county proposal conflicts with a later longer agreement and is not SF-only.
7. **Dated restricted balances are not available cash or deficits.** Compass C-Rent balances and Huckleberry counseling releases require current commitment/allocation review. Neither proves next-gift room, positive or zero.

The audit changes confidence and the required counterfactual evidence, not automatically the central rankings. Provider-confirmed restricted scope, unit cost, existing commitments and a dated remaining gap are needed before actionable gift recommendations.
