# Funding-first audit: Hamilton, Compass, Clinic by the Bay dental, NEMS HBV

Completed 2026-09-08; sources checked September 7–8. Research only, no outreach. Model and corresponding report snapshot: `work/market-for-impact-large-bay-impact/data/san-francisco/` and `app/charities/`. This audits the modeled unit, not the whole organization's financing. No verified dated marginal private-gift offer or remaining dollar capacity found for any of the four. Unknown room must remain `null`, not zero.

## Hamilton Families — homelessness prevention assistance

**Actual model:** an additional eligible family avoiding recorded homelessness within six months because temporary financial assistance was offered. Central $10,000 per assisted family is an analyst assumption, not a provider price; the hypothetical $100K gift is not a tranche. Not shelter, rapid rehousing, long-term subsidy or post-rehousing basic income.

[Current prevention page](https://hamiltonfamilies.org/homelessness-prevention) verifies back/future rent support for San Francisco households at risk, income at/below 50% AMI and recent hardship. It sends applicants to [SF ERAP](https://sferap.com/) to select Hamilton. This is a concrete shared funding/application pathway, not independent private delivery. Current page does not specify the exact three-month back plus three-month future dose cited in the old model; do not present that package as a current guaranteed entitlement.

[Ways to give](https://hamiltonfamilies.org/give) identifies Hamilton Families EIN 94-3055602 and offers general gifts. [Stock-giving page](https://hamiltonfamilies.org/stocks) explicitly says gifts are flexible unless designated, and invites designation communication. Thus designation is publicly contemplated, but no accepted prevention restriction, additional family count, term or price has been verified. A designation is not proof of additionality. The older `/donate` page failed browser retrieval; use the verified giving page to explain available routes, not a claim that donation is unavailable.

**Counterweight:** [Hamilton Heroes](https://hamiltonfamilies.org/hamilton-heroes) says it seeks to house 200 families “this year,” without a clearly identified year or prevention-only budget. It is neither a dated current prevention plan nor 200 additional prevention cases.

**Required next-gift evidence:** prevention-specific restricted balances and commitments, SF ERAP reimbursement/award rules, current eligible-but-unfunded family count and arrears distribution, caseworker capacity, expected awards by date, and a reconciliation of alternate public/private funders. Separate assistance transferred from delivery resources. Overall city contracts, total families served and organization assets cannot provide this denominator.

## Compass Family Services — C-Rent prevention

**Actual model:** an additional at-risk family avoiding recorded homelessness within six months from C-Rent financial support. Historical audited C-Rent expense $2,008,658 / 207 reported prevention-classified families = $9,703.66 per reported family, not an incremental quote; the cohort/program mapping is not fully reconciled. Do not substitute SF HOME subsidies, hotel vouchers, childcare or basic income.

[FY2025 independent audit](https://www.compass-sf.org/_files/ugd/13331a_cd52c427b2074bb1ae6195c5aded399b.pdf), physical p12/printed9, confirms C-Rent program expense $2,008,658 including housing assistance $1,095,985. Physical p27/printed25 reports **C-Rent donor-restricted net assets $1,185,965 at June 30, 2025**, versus $510,710 at June 30, 2024. This is material existing program-restricted funding—not necessarily cash, uncommitted resources or the present remaining balance. Reconcile receivables, commitments, expenditures since year-end and restrictions before claiming room. Never subtract these net assets mechanically from an invented current requirement.

[Current eviction-prevention page](https://www.compass-sf.org/eviction-prevention) and [get-help page](https://www.compass-sf.org/get-help) verify rental-assistance access and provider selection, with SF residence and custody of a minor child for the public application route. Existing Compass clients outside SF have a distinct referral route. [General giving page](https://www.compass-sf.org/donate) and [give-today page](https://www.compass-sf.org/give-today) do not establish a priced restricted C-Rent expansion in retrieved text. Historical C-Rent restricted net assets prove program-restricted resources exist, not that a particular new restriction is accepted automatically.

[February 28, 2023 C-Rent article](https://www.compass-sf.org/post/support-c-rent) describes development and intended expansion of a program established in 2021. It is stale evidence for a September 2026 funding request and contains no present shortfall.

**Required next-gift evidence:** updated restricted C-Rent ledger, allocation across SF ERAP and private awards, applicant risk/eligibility funnel, operating bottleneck, additional awards enabled per dollar, assistance versus case-management cost, and written terms allowing the intended SF prevention use. Broader Compass fundraising or the January 2027 Holy Family Day Home operational expansion cannot be mapped to C-Rent effects.

## Shared Hamilton/Compass public funding and capacity warning

[HSH Director's Report](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Agenda_Item_8_Directors_Report_Eks6IVF.pdf), slide 15 “SF ERAP Policy Changes,” states eligibility changes effective **February 23, 2026** were intended to increase program spending. Returning-applicant score threshold fell 10→8, income cap rose 30%→50% AMI, and future-rent eligibility widened. This is evidence that eligibility/processing and use of existing resources matter, not proof that more private cash is the binding constraint. It neither establishes current surplus nor quantifies either provider's remaining allocation.

[May 7, 2026 Family Addendum](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/HBTB_-_Family_Addendum_Report_-_May_2026.pdf), p18, cites roughly 20% of FY23–24 SF ERAP families reapplying within a year (data accessed April 10, 2026), and proposes flexibility/complementary supports. Reapplication is not homelessness and cannot be converted into causal failure or QALYs. Avoid crediting identical rent payments or household outcomes to Hamilton, Compass, GLIDE and other partners simultaneously.

## Clinic by the Bay — earlier simple extraction relieving severe pain

**Actual model:** one completed clinically indicated simple extraction for an SF resident, not generic dental visits or all clinic patients. Central $350 is constructed from dental-director salary and assumed allocated time/overhead, plus assistant/admin and supplies. Full cost of failed appointments is allocated across completions; volunteer opportunity cost remains excluded. Funding additionality .5 is a judgment, not a measured share.

[Current dental page](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/dental) verifies simple extractions, but eligibility also requires active medical-patient status. It prioritizes documented diabetes patients because demand is high. One emergency appointment each clinic day is for examination/x-rays, not guaranteed same-day treatment. Root canals, dentures and surgical extractions are excluded. Service days are Wednesday/Thursday; those existing hours are not additional donor-funded capacity.

[Eligibility](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/eligibility) specifies uninsured adults without Medi-Cal/other insurance; the clinic says it accepts neither government funding nor insurance. Therefore a blanket direct Medi-Cal-reimbursement displacement story is inappropriate for this provider. Alternative outside access, future eligibility, private-donor replacement and volunteer constraints still matter.

[Current dental-director recruitment](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/careers) gives $85K–95K annual salary for 20 hours/week, benefits eligibility, two-chair suite, and January 2025 dental launch. This is the strongest concrete capacity/cost signal among these four but is **undated and not evidence the position lacks committed funding**. It covers oversight/quality/volunteers as well as treatment; salary cannot all be assigned to extraction minutes.

[Donation page](https://www.clinicbythebay.org/donate), EIN 26-2593712, is general operating support. It still describes dental care/free pharmacy as future plans even though dental is now operational: do not present that language as a current dated expansion. No extraction-specific restriction or budget verified. Its pro-bono leverage statement is organization-wide, not marginal dental resources.

**Required next-gift evidence:** vacancy funding status, licensed clinician/assistant availability, an incremental session plan with filled slots, triaged eligible SF patients and expected wait reduction, supplies/sterilization/overhead, failed-visit allocation, and whether a dental restriction can be accepted. A funded salaried vacancy may be recruitment-constrained rather than cash-constrained. Count additional completed treatment, not examinations or referrals.

## NEMS — sustained adult HBV registry re-engagement

**Actual model:** closed cohort of diagnosed, initially inactive adult HBV registry patients, with repeated navigation and subsequently indicated monitoring/care. Central costs full recurring navigator/supervision/analyst capacity for **20 years**, panel 2,500, 1.5 local loading. It is not a one-year gift, new screening, an immediate antiviral start or Hep B Moms. Public tests/drugs/surveillance are excluded from donor cost, not free resources; loss to follow-up does not create salary savings or replacement patients in this model.

[NEMS liver program](https://www.nems.org/programs/liverhealth/) verifies monitoring/treatment and SF clinical sites, plus already existing screening and perinatal services. [CDA Foundation ReLink grantees](https://cdafound.org/relink/grantees/) lists NEMS HBV re-engagement as a **12-month project in progress**, including diagnosed untreated, missing-fibrosis-assessment, treatment-indicated or lost-to-follow-up patients. NEMS-specific award, calendar dates, staff allocation and remaining balance are undisclosed. Aggregate grant-round totals are not NEMS's allocation. Funder status is undated; do not infer expiry or renewal from the phrase 12 months.

[NEMS support page](https://www.nems.org/support-us/) discloses public grants/contracts as well as private support. Mailed checks are payable to **North East Medical Services Foundation**. This differs from casually naming the clinical operating entity as the gift recipient; confirm legal/tax entity, transfer to the HBV program and geographic restriction. No SF-only re-engagement offer or 20-year commitment is published.

**Required next-gift evidence:** current ReLink award dates/ledger and renewal likelihood, payer-financed navigation overlap, SF-only registry size and unreachable fraction, actual monitoring gaps, funded staff caseload, accepted restriction, multiyear budget and continuity financing. The 2026 economic paper's salaries are model inputs, not an NEMS offer. A one-year navigation gift cannot receive the 20-year scenario's health gains unless future costs and sustained intervention are explicitly funded or reliably financed. Existing Hep B Moms and adult screening budgets must not be counted as this tranche.

## Integration verdict

All four remain modeled research opportunities, not verified funding-ready tranches. Clinic dental has a useful hiring/capacity lead; Compass has particularly concrete existing restricted resources; NEMS has a directly overlapping grant; Hamilton has a current public application pathway but no prevention-only marginal price. Keep funding-room amounts unknown and request evidence before upgrading readiness. No outreach was performed.
