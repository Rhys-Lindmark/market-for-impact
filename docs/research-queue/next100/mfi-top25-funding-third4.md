## 9. San Francisco Free Clinic — proposed shingles-vaccine access (~$1.955M/10 QALYs)

**Actual model unit:** finite annual vaccine cohort, central 20 initiators/16 completed series; 20 seed doses purchased, 36 PAP-eligible doses assumed, administration/navigation/setup cash. It assumes age70, not all clinic patients, and has no terminal-inventory cash refund or future health credit. `verifiedLocalProductDelivery` is false: this is a proposed product-specific service, not confirmed existing Shingrix dispensing.

[Current primary-care page](https://sffc.org/primary-care) verifies free vaccination services for uninsured patients, not named Shingrix availability, eligible age70 volume, stocked inventory or provider enrollment in GSK PAP. [Donation page](https://sffc.org/donate) provides general clinic giving; no vaccine-specific dated campaign, accepted restriction or marginal budget was verified.

[Current GSK vaccine PAP](https://gskpaf.org/gsk/vaccines-patient-assistance/) includes Shingrix, requires no third-party vaccine coverage, excludes Medicare patients, and requires provider registration/patient approval. Thus the age70 cohort cannot simply consist of ordinary Medicare beneficiaries. Current mainland one-person income ceiling displayed is $47,880; eligibility is not established by uninsured status alone. No local eligible headcount found.

[Linked April2024 application, still current linked document](https://gskpaf.org/content/dam/brs-pharma-us/gskpaf-v2/en_US/pdfs/GSK-PAP-Vaccine-English.pdf), p1, confirms replenishment of previously purchased product, ten approved doses within12months, and cap200doses/product/site/year. It prohibits reimbursement for PAP product from any source and administration reimbursement from public payers. GSK does not reimburse administration. Do not treat approved replenishment as cash back or ignore other patients consuming the site's cap. Timing of shipments and seed inventory still need confirmation. The source was re-read; despite the generic enrollment-page wording, the linked PDF still states replenishment, not free advance stock.

**Decisive missing evidence:** a willing registered SFFC prescriber, vaccine storage/procurement capability, older uninsured non-Medicare eligible patients, actual invoice price and seed inventory, existing PAP use at the same site, approved-patient/second-dose plan, staffing cost and written restricted budget. A donor may finance administration/navigation/working stock, but modeled seed spending is not proof the clinic needs or wants it. Existing assistance is an enabling resource, not an additional health result attributable independently to both PAP and donor.

Internal FY23–24 headers conflict with filename and other FY25–26 content; verify executed dates/budget rather than claiming current award size. No matching program dollar amount was verified in this audit.

[Donation page](https://www.sfcommunityhealth.org/donate) identifies legal recipient Asian and Pacific Islander Wellness Center, Inc. dba SFCHC, EIN94-3096109. It offers general giving, not a verified HCV restriction/expansion. No current amount remaining for a donor identified.

**Decisive missing evidence:** executed HCV contract and remaining commitments; separate billable clinical care, publicly financed DAA and grant-funded outreach; eligible untreated SF patients who would otherwise receive referral-only care; funded clinician sessions and downtime; startup only if genuinely required; actual new treatment starts and confirmed cures. Reconcile with SFAF/DOPE/HYA services and avoid counting the same cured patient twice. Zero assumed donor drug cost requires an actual payer pathway, not merely FQHC status. If public funds already purchase the added access, funding additionality may be zero.

## 11. YMCA Greater SF — additional adult diabetes-prevention offers (~$2.095M/10 QALYs)

**Actual model unit:** an additional offer of a yearlong adult DPP; $600 central cost is a constructed allowance, not an observed YMCA fee or completion cost. Benefit is incremental weight-associated health, not automatically lifetime diabetes prevention or children. External offer effect already includes take-up/nonparticipation: do not multiply attendance again without changing denominator.

[Current provider program](https://www.ymcasf.org/program/diabetes-prevention/) advertises English virtual start September22 with enrollment through October13, plus Spanish virtual/in-person plans October/November2026. Cantonese August enrollment is already closed. In-person English/Cantonese currently unavailable. It specifies25one-hour sessions across a year, groups8–15, and possible employer/insurance coverage. These are real dated service plans, but no prices, funded-place balances or donation gap. Waitlist/pre-registration language does not quantify unmet demand.

[SF Health Service System](https://sfhss.org/dpp) offers an already sponsored free YMCA DPP for qualifying covered employees/retirees/spouses. Published March2026 cohorts require minimum8participants or may be postponed/cancelled. It says26sessions versus YMCA's25, so verify chosen course's actual dose. Its “similar programs over$500” is neither a local price nor an unfunded scholarship quote. Small-group recruitment can be a bottleneck independent of money.

[YMCA donation portal](https://donate.ymcasf.org/give) is general community giving; no DPP-specific accepted restricted next-gift offer verified. Branch/program choice alone would not demonstrate additional places. Need the cohort's SF-resident share because GreaterSF serves a wider region.

**Decisive missing evidence:** a named upcoming cohort's complete coach/setup/admin budget and minimum occupancy; current sponsored and insurance-paid places; eligibility and whether a proposed participant can access those routes; incremental outreach versus replacing fees; retained and otherwise unserved offers; ability to accept restricted SF DPP funds. Avoid buying sponsored SFHSS places twice, and do not assume lack of YMCA membership is a financial barrier—membership is not required.

## 12. Self-Help for the Elderly — proposed therapeutic tai chi (~$2.4M/10 QALYs)

**Actual model unit:** proposed24-week,48-class TJQMBB therapeutic curriculum for high-fall-risk older adults, not valuation of existing Sun-style Tai Chi for Arthritis/Fall Prevention. Central$600/person constructs instructor/support costs at15participants. Licensing, training, room and transport need pricing; no quote.

[Current tai-chi page](https://www.selfhelpelderly.org/our-services/activity-centers/tai-chi) still describes free SNAP-Ed-funded Bay Area classes. [January7,2026 DAS commission memo](https://www.sfhsa.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-12/self-help_for_the_elderly_calfresh_healthy_living_program.pdf), pp1–2, requests Jan1–Mar31,2026 grant$96,673 plus$9,667contingency=$106,340, using prior federal funds for program closeout as SNAP-Ed sunsets March31. Scope includes multiple exercise curricula and transition/sustainability work, including a self-paced tai-chi video. Neither$96,673 nor$106,340 is the cost or present gap of a24-week therapeutic course.

This provides a genuine dated funding-end signal, but does not prove current services stopped, no replacement grants exist, or every old expense should be replaced. The static provider page is not proof SNAP-Ed is still financing September classes. [FY2024–25 annual report](https://www.selfhelpelderly.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Annual-Report-2024-2025-DIGITAL.pdf) is retrospective, not post-March2026 financing.

General donation navigation exists on the provider site; linked processor failed this audit, so restriction options are **unverified**, not asserted absent. No priced TJQMBB offer identified.

**Decisive missing evidence:** post-closeout class schedule and payer ledger, whether provider would deliver the proposed curriculum, qualified instructors/certification, high-risk SF recruitment, marginal room/transport cost, restrictions and date-bounded added course. Existing Sun-style classes cannot inherit therapeutic-trial outcomes solely because both are called tai chi. Replacing a public grant supports a valid counterfactual only after verifying what would cease without the gift and what dose would actually continue.
