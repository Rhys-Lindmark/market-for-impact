## Assumption acceptance

Source fidelity supports a serious conditional core-giving model. Mortality .015/child-year, eligible age share .14, .5 transport, .5 financing/output additionality, protection schedule, survival schedule, utility and costs are **explicit analyst judgments**, not estimated from these sources. No precise aggregate AMF marginal forecast is available. The main risks are a low-risk marginal geography, existing functional-net substitution, fast net failure and benefit postponement.

Functional coverage is relative to the historical trial's community package, not a literal individual-use coefficient. Marginal additionality handles funding/functional-net replacement once. Neither term licenses a separate herd-immunity bonus. Retaining the price denominator for all funded nets while reducing health for displacement is appropriate to donor cash; gross package resources are a stress, not a net societal counterfactual.

The model does not use GiveWell's moral weights, monetary-income benefits, estimates of “lives saved,” or age weights to calculate its QALYs. Its post-event utility/survival integration is separately inspectable. Direct Bay share is0; unknown indirect benefits receive0credit.
