# New Incentives primary/source and assumption audit

September 8, 2026. Source-grounded conditional research, not GiveWell moral-weight conversion.

## Inspected sources

1. [NI donation page](https://www.newincentives.org/donate), accessed September 8: recipient and general vaccination-core appeal. No actual donation made or invented restricted offer.
2. [NI program page](https://www.newincentives.org/our-work), accessed September 8: actual Nigeria platform and public partners. Some embedded historical counters differ from current reach page; none is a causal model input.
3. [IDinsight trial brief](https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/NewIncentivesReport_13thNovember.pdf), November 2020, text inspected. Printed page 3 supplies design and coverage results; pages 4–5 describe comparator improvements and campaign context; page 6 distinguishes the earlier retracted pilot. No original mortality endpoint or QALY claim. The long endline report was not fully inspected in this pass; do not claim its tables were audited.
4. [PCV randomized trial primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15794968/), 2005: vaccine efficacy against mortality 16% (95% CI 3–28); serious events 110 vaccine versus 131 placebo. This is clinical plausibility evidence only, not the model's .5 efficacy parameter. Historical Gambian vaccine/disease context differs. No extra survival effect copied from it.
5. [Nigeria health ministry 2024 DHS release](https://health.gov.ng/fg-launches-2024-ndhs-report-unveils-data-on-fertility-child-mortality-others/), 2025 publication, accessed September 8, 2026: high burden context only. National birth-cohort mortality is not remaining vaccine-preventable risk at enrollment.
6. [Founder AMA](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/aqyGLEvmbCrwyNHvH/ama-svetha-janumpalli-ceo-and-founder-of-new-incentives), May 6, 2026 post with later replies: first-person primary operational source; roughly $16 per enrolled infant. Founder also acknowledges changing unit economics, current ORS-only work and new-model exploration. Claims are organizational self-report, not an independent audit. Do not treat its detected duplicate rate as perfect detection.
7. [NI financials](https://www.newincentives.org/financials) explains all-expenditure cost metric. [Linked chart dataset](https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/n3lPg/3/dataset.csv), retrieved by curl, ends at Q1 2023 ($16); not a current price. Current audit/report links were located but full 2024 audit/2025 report contents were not inspected. No audited current program weights or unit costs claimed.
8. [GiveWell March 2025 grant decision](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/new-incentives-extension-of-support-in-northern-nigeria-march-2025) is a primary funder record, not a primary vaccine-effect trial. $19,127,586 awarded for nine-state January 2027–March 2028 continuation after other funds/surplus. It quotes a budget forecast $16.25/enrollment and evaluator adjustment $19.08/unique child. It also models vaccine burden/effectiveness; those are not independently measured parameters. This proposal does not import its lives-saved, moral weights or cash-equivalent multiplier.
9. [NI future plans](https://www.newincentives.org/future-plans), accessed September 8: mixed-vintage, different-horizon funding figures do not establish current post-award room. They are not necessarily logically contradictory. Existing funder commitments take priority over interpreting an old ask as new room.

## Analyst inputs, not sourced facts

Core share .9; cash $25 per eligible catchment infant; transfer $7; additional resources $15; effective coverage increment .12; remaining targeted-disease death probability .03; efficacy .5; additionality .3; two-year implementation delay; equal early-death weighting over the next four years; survival horizon 40, survival .99, utility .85, persistent fraction .8; all shared/independent harm values. Each can change the conclusion. Particularly, the bundle's coverage and burden priors should eventually be replaced with joint disease-specific, age-specific data; multiplying marginal components is a simplifying assumption about which children receive extra protection.

The RCT estimate uses a catchment population, so the price is deliberately defined on that population too. There is no hidden division by observed uptake and no additional treatment-on-treated inflation. Price already absorbs failed enrollment/completion and duplicate costs. Favorable coverage .18 is a joint analyst scenario, not the measles confidence limit applied to all diseases. The event-time distribution is a simplifying calendar assumption, not observed timing. Preventable risk must exclude deaths before a vaccine could act and those avoided by later public catch-up anyway.

## Acceptance and remaining doubt

The organization merits independent review as a plausible high-upside representative-core proposal. It is more identifiable than broad unrelated portfolios but not solely vaccination: general-gift allocation can diverge. No mortality benefit is trial-measured; no live post-award funding gap or exact catchment-level marginal price is known. Cost and resource scenarios are broad, not statistical bounds. Unmeasured noncore and long-term adverse effects can invalidate a claimed lower bound. Bay direct share zero is geographic scope, not an estimate that all international work has no indirect local effects.

Executable checks independently sum event-year × post-death-year discounted survival, verify finite timing, positive-only ratios, gross-resource and transfer separation, and signed nulls. File: ./new-incentives-selfchecks.mjs.
