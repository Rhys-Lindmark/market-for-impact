## Minimal correction before integration

Define `.12 effectiveBundleCoverageIncrease` as **additional standardized full-course-equivalent coverage, with partial-series dose/completion adjustment applied once**, and `.5 bundleDiseaseMortalityEfficacy` as **protection of that standardized course against the specified disease-death risk**. Current wording says coverage already includes partial-series protection while efficacy is averaged protection, leaving room for readers/calculators to discount incomplete series twice. Author asked directly to pin the intended distinct estimands; no numeric change needed for that interpretation. Residualrisk must cover the same marginal children and exclude prevaccination deaths/alternativecatchup; the existing packet states this correctly, but the joint product remains a strong judgment.
