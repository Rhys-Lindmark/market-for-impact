# New Incentives — representative core, ordinary-gift proposal

September 8, 2026. [Model JSON](https://ai.rhyslindmark.com/givebetter/api/new-incentives-model) · [Executable checks](./new-incentives-selfchecks.mjs) · [Source audit](./new-incentives-evidence.md). Research only; no publication or organization count.

## Result and scope

**Central analyst estimate: $43,073 per 10 global incremental QALYs.** Including a gross allowance for associated public/clinical/household inputs raises this to **$66,332**. Favorable is $2,319 donor/$3,607 gross; pessimistic-positive is $12.98m/$18.66m. Joint scenarios are neither confidence intervals nor measured charity outcomes. This is a plausible high-upside new organization, with substantial uncertainty in the mortality bridge and fresh-donation additionality.

The recipient is New Incentives, EIN 45-2368993. Its [ordinary donation appeal](https://www.newincentives.org/donate) and [actual program](https://www.newincentives.org/our-work) focus on the All Babies Are Equal platform: caregiver incentives, awareness and vaccine-stock support alongside free government vaccination. This is an organizational representative-core estimate, not a hypothetical restricted measles gift. Central 90% marginal core allocation is an explicit judgment, not an audited percentage or overhead deduction. The core cost already includes administration. Other marginal activities receive no numerical health credit; they are not known to have zero impact. ORS distribution and new delivery experiments make an unconditional 100%-same-program assumption inappropriate.

No New Incentives slug was found among 48 unique charity slugs in available worktrees. That is not a certified current public roster; the coordinator must deduplicate before integration. The Nigerian program has **zero direct SF/Bay health share**; indirect Bay effects are unknown and zero credited. A California mailing address does not create California beneficiary health.

## Native causal evidence, not observed deaths

[IDinsight's 2020 trial brief, printed page 3](https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/NewIncentivesReport_13thNovember.pdf) covers 167 clinic catchments and 5,173 children aged 12–16 months. Caregiver-reported coverage rose by 16 percentage points for BCG, 21 for Penta1 and 14 for measles; full-series coverage rose 27 points. The comparator retained usual services and contemporaneous campaigns. These are population catchment effects, not treatment-on-treated effects or deaths observed. Self-report, selected operationally feasible clinics, geographic expansion and changing public coverage limit transport. Timing gains among vaccine recipients are not added as another independent coverage effect. The retracted incentive-size pilot was a separate study, not this cluster trial.

The model's **12-point effective mortality-weighted bundle coverage gain is analyst judgment**, below the individual primary point estimates. It denotes extra full-course-equivalent coverage: dose/completion differences are standardized here once, alongside measurement and contemporary transfer. It is not an empirical weighted average. The separate 50% efficacy is the protection of that standardized course against the modeled disease risk, with no second incomplete-series reduction. No second compliance, stockout or herd multiplier is applied.

## Mortality and finite QALY bridge

Central residual disease-death risk is **3%** among marginal not-yet-protected infants over the remaining period to age five, and effective protection against that risk is **50%**. Both are analyst assumptions. [Nigeria's 2024 DHS context](https://health.gov.ng/fg-launches-2024-ndhs-report-unveils-data-on-fertility-child-mortality-others/) reports national under-five mortality of 110 per 1,000 births; this is not the model input, because it includes neonatal deaths before many vaccinations and children already protected. The 3% must concern the marginal child and exclude deaths preceding access or after counterfactual catch-up.

[Cutts et al.'s vaccine trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15794968/) supports a causal mortality pathway for PCV in a different historical setting, but cannot calibrate Nigeria's entire vaccine bundle. Our 50% disease-specific efficacy is not that trial's all-cause mortality reduction. No neonatal, adult hepatitis, morbidity, cash-consumption, education or productivity benefit is added. Not counting them is incomplete health accounting rather than a proved lower bound.

For one prevented early death, central survival difference lasts at most 40 years, with annual survival .99, utility .85 and .8 persistent-survival fraction. These are transparent life-course judgments, **not RCT follow-up or a Nigerian life table**. Twenty percent gets no long-term credit, diagnostically allowing mortality displacement. The model separately discounts the timing of early deaths and subsequent life-years at 3%. A three-year postponement stress raises the donor result to $245,491/10Q, showing why sustained survival matters.

## Costs, denominator and public baseline

The [founder's May 2026 account](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/aqyGLEvmbCrwyNHvH/ama-svetha-janumpalli-ceo-and-founder-of-new-incentives) describes roughly $16 per enrolled infant, not a cost per additional vaccination or per catchment child. The model instead budgets **$25 all-in cash per eligible catchment infant** (favorable $18; pessimistic $40). This is a deliberately explicit planning price, not a provider quote. It covers incentives, repeat enrollments, failed visits, staff and overhead. Aligning a catchment-level trial effect with this funded population avoids applying an enrolled-child denominator without adjustment.

The public vaccine system is indispensable but already partly funded. Another **$15 per funded catchment infant** is an analyst gross allowance for vaccine, clinic, household and donated inputs not paid by NI. Under the central $10,000 gift, 360 infants are supported, so gross associated fiscal commitments are $15,400. Cash transfers are a fiscal cost, not themselves destroyed economic resources; a separate proxy subtracts $7 per supported infant of transfer cash. Neither proxy is a full net societal CEA: public replacement, opportunity costs, household expenditures and healthcare savings are not independently resolved. Clinical resources are not assumed free simply because vaccines are free to patients.

## Fresh donation, timing and funding room

[GiveWell's March 2025 grant decision](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/new-incentives-extension-of-support-in-northern-nigeria-march-2025) funded nine states into March 2028; Niger and Yobe had separate funding. Thus existing funded enrollments cannot be credited to a new gift. The model starts implementation two years after the gift and uses **30% combined financing/output additionality** beyond other donors, reserves and public substitutes. This is judgment, not a measured replacement rate. Identical replacement gives zero shared benefit and harm. A future unrestricted dollar may extend duration, permit expansion or free another funder's money; the last pathway is not credited automatically.

[NI's future-plans page](https://www.newincentives.org/future-plans) displays a $31m 2027 ask and a second $42.5m figure associated with an earlier horizon. These mixed-vintage, different-horizon figures do not establish a current post-award gap; they are not necessarily logically contradictory. This uncertainty weakens confidence but does not prohibit a conditional best estimate. The next decisive improvement is current post-award marginal allocation and mortality-weighted catch-up risk, not another generic proof vaccines work.

## Inspectable arithmetic and signed risks

For $10,000: supported catchment infants = 10,000 × .9 / 25 = 360. Before calendar discount, prevented early deaths = 360 × .3 × .12 × .03 × .5 = .1944. The finite post-death QALY factor is 13.6461; the equal-weight four-year event calendar factor is .8759304. Subtract discounted shared harm to obtain **2.32164 net QALYs**. Ratio = 100,000 / 2.32164.

The shared-harm prior .00002 Q per eligible supported infant, scaled by additionality, is a diagnostic allowance for vaccine/program/crowding harms, not an observed adverse-event rate. Independent gift harm sits outside that scaling. Ten scenarios retain clinical null, shared-harm clinical null, replacement null, replacement with independent harm, no core allocation, short survival and net harm. No positive ratio is shown for zero or negative health. Program-scale averages, uncertain future conditions and model priors prevent treating these numbers as guaranteed donor returns.
