# Remedy Alliance evidence and assumption audit

Accepted-definition update: response equivalents must be unique across all funded bundles; person-linked survival equivalents must exclude overlapping tails from separate rescues of the same person. The .05 survival coefficient incorporates this overlap judgment. It is not a measured first-rescue probability, and annual mortality alone does not perform deduplication. No numerical retune was made for this clarification.

## Clinical and counterfactual evidence

- [Walley 2013 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23372174/) and publisher indexed result were inspected; publisher direct page returned403. Observational community evidence supports plausibility, not a per-bundle causal probability or randomized donor effect. Current mortality trends must not be attributed wholesale to naloxone distribution.
- [Dasgupta et al., PLOS One, October 24, 2025](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0315026), abstract and disclosure inspected. The administrative series has no untreated contemporaneous comparator, reported outcomes and repeat recipients. Formulation-related emesis/anger differences are observed, not randomized. Aggregate dose use does not specify a new bundle's utilization horizon. Staff/board Remedy affiliations disclosed.
- No study in this packet measures 5% one-year use, 5% incremental acute survival or five-year future survival for a current Remedy cohort. These are inspectable analyst priors, not invented study statistics. The outcome chain explicitly retains alternative rescue, existing stock and ongoing mortality.

## Exact analyst judgments

Central core allocation .8; $15/standardized four-injectable-dose-equivalent bundle; $10 external downstream resources; .05 response probability within12months; .05 incremental acute survival conditional on response; .4 funding/output additionality; .5-year implementation delay; five post-rescue years; annual survival .9; utility .7; .03 discount; shared harm .00002 Q/bundle. Broad alternatives and signed nulls are in JSON. No prior was solved to hit $100,000. Geography is deliberately left unknown rather than allocated by CA population, warehouse address or shipment billing.

An organization-specific missing donor quote alone does not invalidate this model. The substantive acceptance risks are current core allocation, national-versus-CA funded baseline, last-mile distribution and incremental rescue survival. Those can move the best estimate substantially. The generic accounting bundle does not direct anyone how to administer medication.
