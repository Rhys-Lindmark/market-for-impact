# Remedy Alliance / For The People — general-gift core model

Independent-audit clarification: qualifying responses are deduplicated across ALL funded bundles, not merely capped within each bundle. Several bundles used at one scene share one response credit. The .05 survival coefficient is a person-linked marginal first-rescue-equivalent probability, already allowing overlapping future person-time when the same person has separate rescues; it is not an unrestricted raw acute survival rate. Each person's five-year survival tail can be credited only once, or partitioned into genuinely incremental nonoverlapping intervals. Annual survival .9 accounts for competing mortality but does not by itself solve duplicate person-time. This clarification changes no numeric prior; the model remains judgment-based, not an empirically linked-person estimate.

## Representative whole-gift boundary

Naloxone procurement/access is an actual core program, alongside technical assistance, drug checking and community support. Central **80% marginal core allocation** is a judgment, not an audited share. The remaining gift stays in the cost numerator and receives no numerical health credit; effects are not known zero. Core unit cost already includes its overhead, so this share is not another administration haircut. The model represents ordinary giving to the supply platform, not buying all existing paid orders anew or claiming every public contract is charitable impact.

The accounting unit is one **four-injectable-dose-equivalent supply bundle**. This standardizes procurement quantity, not a medical recommendation, fixed delivered formulation or assertion an overdose needs four doses. A bundle receives at most one response credit. Current route mix, donated stock and partner orders may differ. Changing formulation mix requires revisiting both unit price and utilization.

## Evidence and causal bridge

[Walley et al. 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23372174/) found lower community overdose mortality associated with overdose education/naloxone implementation in a Massachusetts interrupted time series. The adjusted ratios .73 and .54 are historical observational community comparisons, not Remedy's marginal donor effect. Confounding, current fentanyl supply and different distribution channels prevent direct transfer as a charity mortality percentage.

[Dasgupta et al. 2025](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0315026) analyzes Pittsburgh service records through January 2023. It documents 5,521 reported response events and 70,234 doses dispensed over 17.5 years; reported survival was 98%. The data include changing participant mix and repeat encounters. They cannot identify survival without naloxone or the one-year utilization of fresh Remedy supply. Researchers disclose Remedy affiliations. The paper supports actual peer use and foreseeable harms; **98% is not the survival benefit**.

Our central assumptions are **.05 nonduplicated qualifying response-equivalents per funded bundle within one year**, and **.05 person-linked marginal survival-equivalents per qualifying response**. Both are judgment. The first includes last-mile failures, nonuse, expiry and multiple bundles at one scene; the second retains alternative rescue and discounts overlapping survival tails from separate rescues of one person. Neither is calculated by dividing cumulative reported events by cumulative doses. No separate reversal-success multiplier or general community mortality multiplier is added.

Five years of post-rescue survival difference, annual survival .9, utility .7 and 3% discount yield **2.54364 QALYs per additional acute survivor**. These are finite analyst assumptions allowing recurrent overdose and other competing deaths, not a promise of lifelong survival. A half-year delivery delay plus a within-year midpoint places the rescue at one year after the gift. There is no extra future overdose-avoidance benefit and no assumed cessation or treatment enrollment.

## Costs and funded baseline

Central **$15 donor cash per bundle** is an all-in judgment: approximately $7 commodity allowance, $3 warehousing/fulfillment and $5 program support/administration. A [2023 organizational account](https://remedyallianceftp.org/blogs/news/remedy-alliance-for-the-people-distributes-over-1-million-doses-of-naloxone-in-the-first-10-months-of-operation) valued a million injectable doses at $1.7m, providing an order-of-magnitude historical anchor—not a current procurement quote or economic cost. Do not reuse that price for a nasal product. Failed distribution and wastage are charged. Favorable $10 and pessimistic $30 remain price judgments.

Another **$10 per funded bundle** allows for downstream staff/volunteer time, supplies and end-user distribution not paid by Remedy. It is charged on all funded bundles, including unused ones. This is a gross associated-resource stress, not a measured net societal cost or donated-retail-value multiplier. Paid medicine is already in the cash amount and must not be added again; free manufacturer inventory may instead require changing cash and resource boundaries. Healthcare savings are unquantified.

California NDP already supplies free naloxone through Remedy, and [all 58 counties have received NDP naloxone](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/individuals/naloxone-distribution-project/). This is funded baseline, not the current gift's health. National public grants, tiered sales, philanthropic commodity donations and a [2023 $1m working-capital loan](https://www.soroseconomicdevelopmentfund.org/newsroom/open-society-supports-an-innovative-model-to-make-naloxone-easier-to-access-amidst-the-u-s-overdose-crisis) also matter; a loan is not a grant or repeated gift multiplier. **40% financing/output additionality** is a national representative-core judgment after substitution, not a current verified unfunded offer. A separate .1 additionality stress for dense public provision yields $304,657/10Q donor. That stress is not a measured California result.

## Arithmetic, geography and harms

A $10,000 gift × .8 / $15 supports 533.333 accounting bundles. At .4 additionality and .05 response-equivalents, this gives 10.6667 additional nonduplicated qualifying events; × .05 person-linked survival-equivalents gives .533333 marginal first-rescue-equivalent survival credits before discount, not a measured count of unique lives saved. Finite survival and calendar discount, minus a small shared-harm diagnostic, yield **1.312953 QALYs**. The full gift divided by that outcome produces the central ratio. The legacy JSON field earlyDeathsPreventedBeforeDiscount holds these survival-equivalent credits, not an observed mortality count.

SF, Bay and California marginal beneficiary shares are **unknown**, not zero. A Berkeley warehouse is not a recipient-residence measure. Without a residence allocation, only logical positive-benefit bounds 0≤SF≤Bay≤CA≤overall are available under the model's common effects and no independently localized harm; these are not informative empirical bounds. At a purely illustrative 10% Bay share, the full-gift Bay ratio would be $761,642/10Q. No 10% share is asserted. Independent harms can invalidate simple positive-share bounds. No induced international or SF spillover is credited.

No mortality episode may receive full additive credit from Remedy, NDP, NHRC/DOPE, SFAF, HYA and a downstream distributor. An additional upstream supply bottleneck can be a distinct marginal mechanism, but existing funded recipients' reported reversals are not fresh outcomes for this report. Additionality must be adjusted jointly when comparing complementary gifts.

The shared-harm diagnostic is .00002 Q per supported bundle, scaled by funding/output additionality. It is not observed withdrawal incidence. Clinical null with shared harm is negative; identical replacement removes both shared benefit and harm; independent gift harm can persist. Pessimistic and favorable combinations are not probability bounds. Stronger acceptance requires current marginal supply mix, missing last-mile access and counterfactual rescue risk; biological naloxone effectiveness alone does not settle donor value.
