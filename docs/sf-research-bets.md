# SF research bets: mechanism first

Recorded 2026-09-07. These are research priorities, not proven recommendations. The question is whether an additional philanthropic dollar can buy health gains in San Francisco for less than **$100,000 per 10 incremental QALYs**. One “better life” is a reporting unit of 10 QALYs, not necessarily one person helped.

## Selection and evaluation contract

Start with a causal mechanism, identify a charity implementing it locally, then test whether its actual program fits. Do not start with ten favored charities and retrofit a story. A strong bet has severe avoidable harm, a cheap effective intervention, a reachable underserved population, durable benefits, and an actionable funding bottleneck.

For each lead: verify program and entity; examine intervention evidence; estimate additional reach over existing provision; model incremental costs, effect, duration, attribution and displacement; publish a central analyst estimate with wide scenarios and the assumptions that would reverse our conclusion. Keep philanthropic cost and total resource cost separate: public reimbursement is not free treatment. A scenario below the threshold is not a verified local funding opportunity. Record failed hypotheses as well as promising results.

| Prevent liver disease by identifying untreated HBV and maintaining indicated care. | [North East Medical Services — Liver Health](https://www.nems.org/programs/liverhealth/) | Additional treatment-eligible patients starting and adhering to care; reject screening dominated by people already immune, screened or treated. |

| Reconnect severely uncontrolled diabetes patients to effective existing care. | [St. Anthony Foundation — diabetes re-engagement](https://www.stanthonysf.org/how-st-anthonys-medical-clinic-is-enhancing-diabetes-care-rosas-journey-to-reconnection/) | Durable glycemic improvement and complications versus usual care, including hypoglycemia. | Relieve severe untreated dental pain with volunteer-supported clinical capacity. | [Clinic by the Bay, listed by SFCCC](https://www.sfccc.org/clinic-by-the-bay) | Additional completed treatments for painful conditions, durable utility improvement, and full episode cost. ## Ten unconventional program leads

This is an older program-level discovery list, not yet ten distinct researched organizations. PHC appears more than once; do not count its services as separate organizations toward the roughly-50 goal. Reconcile or replace duplicates during the unconventional phase. Local delivery gaps remain explicit.

1. **Lead-remediation navigation:** [PODER's Fix Lead SF announcement](https://podersf.org/2026/07/28/fix-lead-sf-program/). Can small enrollment support unlock public remediation? PODER is a possible outreach partner, not a confirmed remediation implementer. Need additional completed remediations, exposure change and defensible health valuation.
2. **Indoor air filtration:** [Brightline Defense](https://www.brightlinedefense.org/news/em-magazine). Test purifier use, maintenance, exposure reduction and clinical benefits; uncontrolled reported symptom change is insufficient for causal inference.
3. **Targeted fall-prevention repairs:** [Rebuilding Together SF](https://rebuildingtogethersf.org/what-we-do/). Test low-cost modifications for high-risk residents against injury reduction and current fully loaded delivery cost.
4. **Surplus medicine redistribution:** [SIRUM](https://sirum.org/). Find and verify a current SF dispensing partner before claiming local delivery; test usable drug mix, adherence gains and dispensing costs.
5. **Prescription glasses:** [Project Homeless Connect Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/). Model sustained use and measured utility improvement, not screenings.
6. **Hearing aids and follow-up:** [Project Homeless Connect Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/), with Hearing and Speech Center of Northern California. Test cost per sustained user including batteries and follow-up.
7. **Dentures restoring eating function:** [Project Homeless Connect Core Senses](https://www.projecthomelessconnect.org/programs/coresenses/). Test successful fitting, regular use, severity and durable utility gain, including adjustments.
8. **Pet support enabling human treatment:** [Shanti PAWS](https://www.shanti.org/programs-services/pets-are-wonderful-support/). Test whether fostering and care change human treatment uptake; do not count animal welfare as human QALYs or assume a closed waiting list means expandable capacity.
9. **Safe daytime sleep:** [Gubbio Project](https://www.thegubbioproject.org/). Test additional restorative sleep and sustained health effects against staffing and alternatives.
10. **Hygiene supplies through existing distribution:** [Simply the Basics](https://www.simplythebasics.org/basic-needs). Test additional clinical benefits and full delivery costs, not satisfaction alone.

## Research order and housing hypothesis

Latest user order: complete the original 25, publish the city-level theory, then research these ten mechanism-first bets, then the ten unconventional programs, then SPUR/GrowSF/HAC, and only then select the final four and rebuild the homepage. Existing candidate lists are provisional discovery, not permission to skip that order. Within the mechanism phase, prioritize overdose prevention, medication-treatment retention and inexpensive access interventions if the synthesis supports them. This order reflects short causal pathways and potentially inexpensive delivery, not established rankings. Housing remains a hypothesis: the three current leading housing estimates share an external QALY coefficient and a retention judgment. Their apparent agreement is not three independent measurements of housing's superiority.

Completion requires an inspectable program model and bottom line, including a best estimate when defensible, source/assumption separation, sensitivities, and a marginal funding assessment. Keep unsuccessful leads visible. Develop the initial city-wide theory from the original 25 before expanding, then revise it with the new findings; do not assume the present leaders must win.
