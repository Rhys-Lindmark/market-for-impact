## Whole-gift, duration and class-mixture coherence

70% gift delivery allocation and 30% retained operating/expansion costs are visible assumptions. Dispensed months, not nominal prescriptions, fund complete person-courses; increasing funded years divides fixed months among fewer people. Concurrent-drug divisors and mutually exclusive BP/statin/residual routing prevent additive-drug and repeat-person benefit. Actual routing is uncertain. After funded cardiovascular therapy, hazards coincide; the finite event-free gap subsequently narrows. Background mortality and discount apply to integrated states, and event-cost timing uses the same branch window. No lifetime prevention is implied.

Residual clinical health is genuinely represented by a named class-mixture prior rather than silently zero: psychiatric20%, diabetes15%, anti-infective10%, asthma15%, other40%, with stated class-average utility gains and no residual post-course survival. The common on-treatment utility .019 is the weighted average of those assumptions. The review explicitly says psychiatric latency/nonresponse and short appropriate infection-course duration, spontaneous recovery and prophylactic/non-target use must be averaged into these **per-funded-month** gains. Thus a full year of antibiotic effect is not asserted per acute prescription. This is a crude but inspectable expected-burden model, not clinical calibration. Other40% has zero central quantified effect and residual-wide/null/adverse sensitivities; future evidence can change this without altering the accounting framework.

Residual uncertainty is **dominant and must stay visible**: residual contributes6.012826 of7.856149 gross clinical Q; removing it reduces net Q to1.621738. Consequently the near-threshold national donor estimate is primarily driven by unobserved mix/utility assumptions, not the two cardiovascular trials. The report states this in summary and reservations. The favorable joint scenario is not assigned a probability or presented as a confidence interval. Its threshold pass is not evidence of likely performance. No input retuning was required for acceptance.

Public/free existing care is included through financing and treatment-contrast assumptions; low-cost generic availability and public pharmacy capacity remain uncertain. Nominal outside resource budgets cover all dispensed activity, not only successful high-risk patients. Their incrementality and public/private shares are separately exposed. Drug diversion must affect both alternative health and resource value, not be free by definition. This is an incomplete societal account, especially for residual medical spending, and is labeled accordingly.
