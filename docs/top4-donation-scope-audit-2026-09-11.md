# Top-four donation route and restriction audit

## Outcome

**No inspected route verifies a priced, currently available purchase of the modeled health benefit.** ReCARES has the closest scope match: general support for the whole organization modeled. GLIDE, Breathe California and Pacific Vision Foundation (PVF) have conditional activity estimates that must not be presented as returns to an ordinary unrestricted gift. PVF explicitly advertises patient sponsorship, but that is not evidence of an available, additionally funded surgery at the model's price.

**Concrete product correction:** GLIDE's saved `/ways-to-give/` URL returned HTTP 404. Its current official `/give/` page works and distinguishes Foundation programs from church giving. Breathe's report has no external donation URL; its header “Donate” only jumps to `#funding`. The homepage instruction to use each report's giving link is therefore not universally true.

## Exact destination evidence and limits

1. ReCARES public page offers PayPal/credit card and checks for mission support. The PayPal link resolves in the source to hosted button `6A3VRDAZMQM6G`; browser fetch failed. This supports **general-support route, restriction unknown**, not a claim that the processor forbids restricted gifts. [Primary page](https://www.recares.org/financial-donations/).
2. GLIDE's working page explicitly separates Foundation and Church. Foundation destination is [Click & Pledge](https://connect.clickandpledge.com/w/Form/a32eb37f-3165-4e32-8fac-16620bbe25ca?trk=websiteother). No form submission attempted. The [Welcome Center](https://www.glide.org/programs/welcome-center/) describes rental assistance, which establishes a service, not an earmarked donor offer. Prefer replacing the broken URL with the official Give page and explicit Foundation wording; do not silently promise rental allocation.
3. Breathe's [Make a Difference](https://lungsrus.org/make-a-difference/) page says donations support numerous programs. Its old expense graph does not establish current capacity. CPAP equipment and vehicle gifts are separate routes with different resource boundaries; neither validates the cessation estimate.
4. PVF's [current giving page](https://pacificvisionfoundation.org/get-involved/ways-to-give/) contains the sponsorship purpose but no purchase terms. Its prominent [CMIE donation destination](https://give.pacificvisionfoundation.org/campaign/811529/donate) must not be substituted for charity surgery. Campaign 496902's “General Website” title is not proof of its legal restriction terms; contents were inaccessible. Prior [sponsorship evidence memo](/private/tmp/mfi-pvf-sponsorship-evidence-update.md) remains applicable: sponsorship prose is not a funding ledger or verified additional case.

## Minimal redesign recommendations

- Label links by **gift scope**, not just “Donate”: general support, advertised sponsorship, or no verified route.
- Keep **research estimate** and **donation-ready opportunity** separate. None of these four qualifies as a verified purchasable QALY package.
- Replace GLIDE's broken URL; clearly select Foundation, not Church. Change Breathe's anchor label from Donate to Funding limitations.
- Retain the renderer's existing general-gift disclaimer, but place scope beside the price and CTA; a footer caveat does not make a program estimate a whole-gift estimate.
- Do not claim that the inability to inspect processor forms proves restrictions are impossible. The precise finding is **not publicly verified in this audit**.
