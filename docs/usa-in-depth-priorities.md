## Acceptance gates

Revisit three original financial years with consistent recipient scope, netted expenses, in-kind resources, fiscal periods and reserve restrictions. Trace ordinary marginal support to extra capacity and health against real alternatives, keeping patient substitution, organizational attribution and funding replacement distinct.

Reproduce clinical units, finite cohorts, time, USA resident allocation and zero/downside outcomes. Characterize material unpriced portfolio effects rather than silently setting them to zero or treating a partial model as a proven lower bound. Use actual Medium research intervals; prior research and this comparative planning are not new per-organization beta minutes.

Choose four only after independently accepted in-depth work supports a practical donation opportunity and a comparative judgment robust enough to recommend. This queue does not guarantee any four qualify.
