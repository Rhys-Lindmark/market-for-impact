# Via Heart Project: ordinary-gift feasibility
September 8, 2026. Exact recipient VIA HEART PROJECT, EIN 27-1530938. Research assets only.

## Finding
Real philanthropic AED subsidy pathway, but no verified marginal package or measured maintenance mortality effect. The prior-based estimate does not support a sub-$100,000/10-QALY claim. Whole gift $100,000 yields 0.090263 total, 0.081237 US, 0.063184 Bay and 0.013539 SF QALYs centrally. Favorable joint assumptions still cost $478,425 per10 total QALYs. No threshold tuning.

## Primary source facts
[Official services](https://viaheartproject.org/about/) describe a worldwide AED/CPR organization, with illustrative AED/cabinet values $1,950–$3,200 and training $800–$1,800. These are price anchors, not fully allocated incremental production costs. [Maintenance](https://viaheartproject.org/join/) costs $350/AED/year for most models, includes scheduled and post-use consumables, oversight and support; first year is included with purchase. [Donation intake](https://viaheartproject.org/donation-request/) establishes actual subsidies, asks ability to pay and site readiness capacity, and describes over 3,700 managed AEDs worldwide. Stock is neither annual placements nor independent rescue catchments.

[PHCD partnership](https://viaheartproject.org/phcd/) explicitly supplies public funding for local equipment, training and maintenance. Existing owner/public financing is the counterfactual, not additional gift leverage. No current public grant amount or unfunded queue verified. [Schools](https://viaheartproject.org/schools/) establish California and Bay activity, not beneficiary-residence fractions. The SF office does not establish SF health.

[Primary FY2025 Form 990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/271530938/202601289349301730/full), filed May 8, 2026: revenue $1,782,007; expenses $1,484,637; contributions $162,289; program fees $1,602,533; surplus $297,370. Program expense $1,283,155 and management $201,482 reconcile. Cash/total assets $772,855; liabilities $8,415; net assets $764,440. Two-dollar prior-period adjustment explains beginning net assets plus surplus difference. This is a tax filing, not an audit; no restriction or open-tranche inference. Fee dominance supports explicit financing replacement, not automatically poor additionality.

[PAD randomized trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa040566) randomized 993 community units to structured CPR versus CPR plus AED response. Thirty survivors among128 arrests versus15 among107; the primary comparison was survivors per randomized unit, not a randomized per-arrest risk difference. Trial equipment/response-system benefit does not identify incremental maintenance benefit. No trial coefficient is directly transferred: conditional survival improvement of0.10 is an explicit clinical-context prior.

## Model and priors
New placement: 30% of gift; $6,800 per one-AED independent service-location package = $3,000 equipment/cabinet +$1,200 initial training +$1,200 refresher allowance +four $350 renewals. Five years active. Refresher allowance and marginal fully allocated cash are planning priors; no quoted complete five-year offer. Retained 40% of gift includes additional administration, education and unquantified activities; no separate outcome attribution. Maintenance:30% at $350 per one-year supported existing-AED catchment. Do not multiply co-located devices into independent populations or bill new branch maintenance again.

Both branches funding additionality0.5 and retained distinct first-arrest catchment fraction0.9 are separate priors. A catchment can contribute at most one eligible arrest, and survivors' recurrent-arrest risk remains inside all-cause future mortality0.1/year. Hazard0.05/year per retained catchment is a site-event prior, not an AED-year rate extracted from trial or promotional saves. There is no patient mortality before the index event in an open service-location population.

New effective early-response coverage increases0.3→0.7; maintenance0.85→0.90. These are not battery failure probabilities or observed compliance effects. Each marginal covered arrest receives additional discharge probability0.1; no repeated PAD relative-risk multiplier. Discount0.03 continuous, utility0.7, horizon10 years from activation, activation delay0.05 years. All health ceases at the finite horizon. No extra health for repeated arrests, nonfatal morbidity, nonmodeled education or other activities.

Global benefits multiplied by nested US0.9, Bay0.7 and SF0.15 residence priors. Neither independent local fraction is measured, and total includes non-US health. Separate nested harm shares default to the same values. Full gift, not allocated local spending, is each regional donor numerator. These conditional point estimates are not posterior means; scenarios are unweighted.

Outside site labor/space resources: $500/new complete course and $50/maintenance year. They exclude already purchased hardware, pads and professional training. Incremental medical resource allowance $25,000 per net additional discharge beyond otherwise fatal care; no negative-survival savings claimed. Costs are gift-date planning dollars; future outside costs treated as upfront commitments, not a validated timed spending ledger. Gross associated = full gift + nominal outside resources + medical allowance; conditional net = full gift + funding-additional outside resources + medical allowance − explicitly signed real savings. Government payments themselves are transfers, not extra resources. No quantified central resource savings. Full society accounting remains incomplete, not concealed as a complete cost-effectiveness result.

Independent and displaced-alternative harms are gift-date present-value QALYs and persist if target efficacy is zero; per-added-course harms apply before distinct-benefit filtering. Signed coverage changes allow direct harm. Nonpositive health has null price, not zero price. Signed negative resource numerator remains visible.
